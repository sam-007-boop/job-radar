About this role 

 Company: BlackRock Financial Management, Inc.
 Job Title: Associate, Fixed Income Product Strategist
 Location: 725 Ponce De Leon Avenue NE Atlanta, GA 30306

 Job Duties: Coordinate responses to DDQ, RFIs, RFPs, and other client and consultant data requests with a focus on accuracy, consistency, and timeliness. Maintain and update product data across internal databases and consultant platforms. Develop and refine Excel-based tools and templates to streamline data collection, validation, and reporting. Apply Python and other scripting tools to automate repetitive tasks and enhance data quality checks. Collaborate with portfolio managers, compliance, and operations to source and verify investment data and narrative content. Support the creation of product collateral including performance summaries, attribution analysis, and strategy overviews. Conduct peer and industry analysis to benchmark product positioning and identify trends. Ensure all materials meet regulatory, compliance and brand standards. Contribute to the continuous improvement of internal processes and documentation standards. Timely and accurate delivery of DDQ and RFI responses with minimal revisions. Demonstrated ability to manage large datasets and complex product information with precision. Development of tools of processes that improve team efficiency and reduce manual errors. Recognition as a reliable resource for product-related data and documentation.

 Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in Mathematics, Economics, Finance, Business Management or a related field and three (3) years of experience in the job offered or as an Associate, Analyst or a related role. Must have three (3) years of experience with: Fixed Income and Securitized Asset Management experience; Quantitative and analytical skills in Excel to assess portfolio data and attributes related to risk; Scripting languages, including Python, for data automation and analysis; Fixed income and investment products including Securitized Assets and Collateralized Loan Obligations (CLOs); Knowledge of Macro economics trends and how it relates to Fixed Income asset classes; Data management including Fund characteristics and performance metrics; Experience with Marketing and presentation materials; Experience with Client presentations; Business writing including RFPs, RFIs and DDQs.

 Salary: $115,000 – 140,000
