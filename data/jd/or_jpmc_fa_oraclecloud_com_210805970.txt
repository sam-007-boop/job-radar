JPMorgan Chase — Finance Control Management Associate – Business Resiliency & Third-Party Oversight
https://jpmc.fa.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1001/job/210805970

Join a team that helps Corporate Finance prepare for disruption and manage third-party risk. You’ll work with partners across the business to identify issues, coordinate action, and make risks easier to understand and address.
 As a Finance Control Management Associate in Corporate Finance, you support business resiliency and third-party oversight. You’ll help coordinate planning, testing, risk assessments, and governance reporting while working with stakeholders to resolve issues and strengthen the control environment.
 Job Responsibilities 
 - Support resiliency planning and testing, including plan recertifications, recovery strategy tests, assessments, and critical dependency reviews.
- Coordinate incident preparedness activities and track remediation actions through resolution.
- Monitor third-party engagements, risk assessments, recertifications, exceptions, attestations, and ongoing oversight requirements.
- Advance initiatives related to supplier resiliency, critical third-party applications, concentration risk, and evolving risk programs.
- Identify, document, and escalate risks, issues, and dependencies; partner with accountable stakeholders to drive timely action.
- Prepare management and governance reporting on key risks, metrics, issues, trends, and decisions.
- Collaborate with finance, resiliency, third-party risk, operational risk, technology, and control management partners to improve processes and controls.

 Required Qualifications, Capabilities, and Skills 
 - Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience.
- At least two years of experience in risk management, controls, business resiliency, third-party risk, operational risk, audit, or a related function.
- Analytical and organizational skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities and coordinate stakeholders.
- Written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to develop clear governance materials and management reporting.
- Proficiency with spreadsheet, presentation, and office productivity tools.

 Preferred Qualifications, Capabilities, and Skills 
 - Experience in financial services or in a business continuity, resiliency, or third-party risk function.
- Familiarity with resiliency testing, critical dependencies, incident preparedness, supplier assessments, or ongoing monitoring.
- Experience supporting governance forums, issue tracking, remediation, or cross-functional initiatives.
