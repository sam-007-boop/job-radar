This position assists with monthly financial statement close process and issuance of financial statements, including capturing and reporting of monthly financial data, statistics and other financial indicators. This position is also responsible for maintenance of specific entity financial accounts, including reconciliations of balance sheet and income statement accounts. Individual will assist with budget preparation tasks as needed. Works as a liaison between the Finance staff and the external financial audit team during both interim and final audits. Individual will act as a resource to other co-workers within and outside of the Finance department and will perform other specific projects/duties as necessary. Position Details: Education: Bachelor's degree in accounting, Finance, or related, or equivalent experience.
Experience: Two (2) years accounting experience, preferably health care related.
Other: High level of proficiency in general ledger & PC applications, including Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, etc. Strong interpersonal communication skills, analytical abilities and organizational skills. Ability to handle multiple responsibilities simultaneously. Ability to work with and preserve confidential information. Works independently on assigned tasks. Able to self-review and identify/correct errors
Preferred Education: Bachelor's degree.
Preferred Licensure: CPA Certification is a plus.
Preferred Experience: Three to Five (3-5) years' experience in health care setting.
Preferred Other: Lawson skills highly preferred. Experience in the preparation of consolidating financial statements including consolidating and eliminating entries.
