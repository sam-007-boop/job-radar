 Posting Summary 
 Rutgers, The State University of New Jersey is seeking a Associate Director of Data Analytics for the School of Environmental and Biological Sciences. Reporting to the Sr. Associate Dean for Finance and Administration, the Associate Director of Data Analytics provides strategic leadership and operational oversight for data analytics, reporting, and decision support within SEBS and NJAES . The position collaborates with academic, administrative, and university partners to transform complex data into actionable insights that support student success, academic planning, enrollment management, financial stewardship, faculty and staff effectiveness, resource allocation, and institutional strategy. 

Among the key duties of the position are the following: 
- Leads the development and continuous improvement of dashboards, reports, data models, and other analytical tools.

- Analyzes data related to enrollment, admissions, retention, progression, graduation, student outcomes, course demand, faculty workload, budgeting, human resources, and operational performance.

- Provides data-informed recommendations to support strategic planning, assessment, program review, and organizational decision-making, while managing analytics projects from requirements gathering through implementation, testing, documentation, deployment, and evaluation.

- Establishes and maintains standards for data quality, definitions, documentation, security, privacy, and responsible data use.

- Partners with the department chairs, program directors, faculty, staff, institutional research, information technology, enrollment management, finance, student success, and other university offices to identify business needs and develop effective analytical solutions.

- Communicates findings through presentations, written reports, visualizations, and executive briefings.

- Develops data dictionaries, process guides, and training materials; and supports faculty and staff in interpreting and appropriately using data and analytics tools.

- Supervises, mentors, and evaluates analytics staff, student employees, or project teams, as applicable.

- Stays current with emerging analytics methods, higher education trends, and applicable privacy and compliance requirements, while promoting a culture of responsible, transparent, and effective data use across the School.

- Performs other duties as needed.

 Qualifications

 Minimum Education and Experience 

- Master’s degree in data analytics, statistics, information systems, business administration, institutional research, education, public policy, or a related field; or an equivalent combination of education and experience.

- At least five years of progressively responsible experience in data analytics, business intelligence, institutional research, reporting, strategic planning, or a related area.

 Certifications/Licenses 

 Required Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities 

- Demonstrated experience developing multi-year scenarios, sensitivity analyses, forecasts, or other analytical models that inform organizational or strategic planning.

- Experience translating complex data into clear recommendations for senior leaders and nontechnical audiences.

- Experience developing dashboards, reports, and visualizations using Tableau, Power BI, or similar tools.

- Experience with data quality, governance, documentation, and reporting standards.

- Excellent written, verbal, presentation, and interpersonal communication skills.

- Demonstrated ability to manage multiple priorities and projects in a collaborative, decentralized environment.

- Ability to handle sensitive information with discretion and maintain data privacy and security.

 Preferred Qualifications 

- Experience working in a college or university setting.

- Knowledge of academic program planning, enrollment management, student success, institutional effectiveness, accreditation, budgeting, or resource allocation.

- Experience advising leaders on the strategic implications of programmatic, organizational, or financial decisions.

- Experience with student information systems, learning management systems, enterprise resource planning systems, finance systems, or higher education data warehouses.
