 Team / Division Overview 

 The Deutsche Asset Management Private Infrastructure business (“DWS Infrastructure”), acquires and manages Debt and Equity investments in businesses that provide essential Infrastructure services in the transport, energy, utilities, digital, circular economy and healthcare sectors.  With investment teams located in Europe and the US, the business has a global footprint and is focused on managing infrastructure portfolios that can meet the needs of institutional investors and private clients worldwide. The division has funds under management of approximately €15 billion.

 The US Infrastructure Debt platform currently manages in excess of $7 billion of committed capital through multiple credit funds and separately managed accounts. Since the Platform’s inception in 2014 the investment team has deployed capital into over 300 investments.

 Investment Associates on the team are involved in all aspects of the private debt investment process for the credit funds across North America and assist in managing the assets in the funds’ portfolios. The appropriate candidate will have strong financial modelling, accounting & finance, credit analysis, communication, and presentation skills, and will demonstrate an interest in the infrastructure industry and its various subsectors.

 Role Details 

 As an Investment Associate, you will be responsible for:

 - Closely involved in all phases on the investment process from origination, financial modelling, due diligence, and investment memorandum preparation and presentation.
- Building, reviewing, and updating financial models to support with investment appraisal and valuation analysis/credit analysis.
- Taking on asset management responsibilities with respect to investments under management, with a view of maximizing the performance of such investments.
- Working with the investor marketing teams to develop marketing materials and assisting with fundraising endeavors as the business continues to actively market its future funds.
- Conducting due diligence on debt investment opportunities (Technical, Commercial, Financial, Insurance and Legal/Tax).
- Liaison with investment bankers, lawyers, tax specialists and other experts to facilitate the due diligence process, both internally and externally.
- Performing internal rating and ESG assessments of investment opportunities.
- Assistance in the preparation and presentation of Investment Committee papers.
- Monitoring and ongoing reporting performance of portfolio assets.

 We are looking for:

 - Relevant professional experience at an Analyst/Associate level within Project Finance, Leveraged Finance, Infrastructure Finance, Infrastructure Debt, Private Credit, M&A or investment banking advisory, with experience of structuring, underwriting, execution or analysis of loans or other debt financings.
- Demonstrable infrastructure sector experience, including one or more areas such as renewable energy, power, utilities, energy transition, transportation, digital infrastructure/data centres or telecommunications.
- Candidates from M&A or broader advisory backgrounds will be considered where they demonstrate meaningful experience of debt, financing structures and/or capital structure analysis as part of transaction or valuation work.
- Strong financial modelling and valuation skills, including experience of cash-flow modelling, debt sizing, leverage analysis, sensitivity/downside analysis, DCF, IRR/MOIC and/or credit analysis.
- Good understanding of debt and credit fundamentals, including leverage, debt capacity, capital structures, refinancing, financing terms, lender protections and/or credit risk assessment.
- Experience undertaking financial and commercial due diligence on live transactions or investment opportunities, identifying key financial, commercial, credit and downside risks.
- Demonstrable experience supporting live transaction execution and investment decision-making, ideally including preparation of Investment Committee, Credit Committee, lender or equivalent senior decision-making materials and coordination with internal and external advisers.
- Ability to leverage AI tools to enhance productivity, optimise workflows to solve business problems, while applying critical judgment to ensure responsible and ethical use of data and AI outputs.
- Educated to Bachelor’s degree level within a related financial subject or with relevant professional experience.
