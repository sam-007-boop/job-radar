 Job Summary: 

 The Corporate Banking Loan Administrator supports the Restaurant and Hospitality Franchise Finance team by coordinating loan closings and providing life-of-loan servicing for restaurant and hospitality franchise finance transactions.

 The role is responsible for managing closing activity for acquisition, refinance, development, and working capital transactions, while supporting accurate post-closing administration and ongoing servicing. The position serves as a key liaison among relationship managers, portfolio managers, middle and back office HUBs, legal counsel, title companies, borrowers, and third-party consultants to ensure timely, accurate execution and responsive loan administration.

 Responsibilities :

 Loan Closing: 

 - Coordinate commercial real estate and franchise finance loan closings from approval through funding.
- Review credit approvals and term sheets to confirm loan documentation accurately reflects the approved transaction structure.
 - Work directly with internal and external legal counsel on preparation and review of loan documents.
 - Review and coordinate closing due diligence, including title, survey, insurance, flood review, KYC, organizational documents, UCC filings, and collateral perfection requirements.
- Coordinate customer and collateral due diligence with participant lenders, as applicable.
- Prepare closing checklists and track satisfaction of conditions precedent through funding.
- Coordinate closing logistics with borrowers, attorneys, title companies, franchisors, and internal operations teams.
- Confirm funding requirements and wire execution with the middle/back office HUB.
- Create closing binders and document collateral identification for transition to servicing.

 Loan Servicing: 

 - Review loan documents, servicing forms, and system records to support ongoing loan administration.
- Monitor and respond to UCC tracking, flood insurance deadlines, post-close documentation exceptions, and related internal inquiries.
- Review payment and fee invoices for accuracy and investigate discrepancies as needed.
- Coordinate payoff documentation, lien releases, and related servicing actions with counsel and internal operations teams.
- Terminate flood determinations, tax contracts, and paid facilities in the bank’s system of record.
- Manage borrower, internal stakeholder, and servicing-related inquiries.
- Track fee revenue and related servicing activity, as needed.

 Skills & Experience: 

 - Minimum of 3 to 5+ years of commercial loan closing, loan servicing administration, or related commercial banking experience.
- Experience closing commercial real estate, specialty finance, or other commercial loans for a bank, credit union, finance company, or other institutional lender.
- Working knowledge of title insurance, surveys, collateral perfection, commercial loan documentation, UCC filings, guaranties, and post-closing follow-up requirements.
- Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Business, Accounting, Real Estate, or a related field preferred. Equivalent industry experience will be considered.
- Ability to manage practical issues and competing priorities in transactions with limited standardization.
- Basic accounting, reconciliation, and fee/payment tracking skills.
- Understanding of commercial loan documentation, closing mechanics, and life-of-loan servicing workflows.
- Excellent organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple closings, post-closing items, and servicing requests simultaneously.
 - Strong attention to detail and commitment to accuracy.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Office, including Excel and Word.
 - Ability to collaborate effectively with borrowers, legal counsel, title companies, consultants, operations teams, and internal stakeholders.

 Desired Requirements: 

 - Experience with franchised restaurant finance or hospitality lending.
- Experience with construction or bridge lending, including review of construction budgets, draw requests, and inspection reports.
 - Knowledge of syndicated or participated loan structures.
 - Understanding of KYC, regulatory, and compliance requirements related to commercial lending.
 - Paralegal or real estate law experience preferred, but not required.
