Amazon (incl. AWS data-center finance) — Senior Financial Analyst
https://www.amazon.jobs/en/jobs/10605909/senior-financial-analyst

We are Amazon's Finance Operations Services organization (FinOps), managing billions in financial transactions across North American business channels. Our team drives innovation in payment processing, billing operations, and revenue collection through sophisticated data analytics and process optimization.

As a Senior Financial Analyst in our HQ2 Service Center, you will have the opportunity to:

- Drive strategic financial analysis that directly impacts Amazon's cash flow and operational efficiency
- Partner with key stakeholders across Amazon's diverse business units, including Sales, Technology, and Finance teams
- Own end-to-end process improvements that scale across multiple business channels
- Leverage advanced analytics to identify trends, opportunities, and solutions that enhance our financial operations
- Design innovative approaches to streamline and automate core business processes
- Build and present compelling business cases to senior leadership

This role offers exceptional visibility and impact within Amazon's financial ecosystem, based in our state-of-the-art HQ2 campus in Northern Virginia. As part of Amazon's $2.5 billion investment in HQ2, you'll work in a modern, energy-efficient campus spanning 4 million square feet, with competitive benefits, world-class facilities, convenient public transportation, and proximity to Reagan National Airport.

If you're passionate about driving business impact through data analytics, process innovation, and strategic problem-solving in a dynamic, fast-paced environment, we want to hear from you. Join us in shaping the future of financial operations at one of the world's most customer-centric companies.

NOTE: This is a fully in-office role, five days a week.

Key job responsibilities
- Drive innovation by collaborating with cross-functional teams to integrate scalable Order-to-Cash (O2C) processes, directly impacting Amazon's financial efficiency
- Engage with senior leaders to shape and execute strategic initiatives, contributing to the long-term success of Amazon's financial operations
- Lead high-impact financial reporting and planning, providing critical insights and recommendations that influence business decisions
- Develop and implement systematic approaches to resolve complex customer account and receivables issues
- Identify trends, uncover root causes, and propose innovative solutions to enhance our financial processes
- Harness big data using advanced tools (Excel, SQL, and other data management systems) to generate actionable insights and drive process improvements
- Manage ambiguous situations and communicate effectively with stakeholders across global time zones

About the team
This is a team with highly skilled members operating with high volumes, high ambiguity, and high performance standards. The work location is Arlington, VA, and team members are expected to work from office.

- 3+ years of identifying, leading, and executing opportunities to improve, automate, standardize or simplify finance or business tools and processes experience
- 3+ years of finance, accounting, or related analytical field experience

- Experience working with large-scale data mining and reporting tools (examples: SQL, MS Access, Essbase, Cognos) and other financial systems (examples: Oracle, SAP, Lawson, JD Edwards)
