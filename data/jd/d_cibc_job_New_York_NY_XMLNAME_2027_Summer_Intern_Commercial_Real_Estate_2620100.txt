 Our Summer Internship is a 10-12 week program designed to provide hands-on experience and exposure to the dynamic world of commercial banking and technology. As an intern, you’ll work alongside experienced professionals, participate in real projects, and gain insight into how technology drives innovation and client solutions at CIBC.  

 Program Highlights:  
 - Leader Connect Sessions: Meet with members of our executive and leadership teams. 
 - Employee and Peer Connects: Build your network and connect with peers, business leaders and CIBC employees.

 What you'll be doing 
 CIBC’s Commercial Real Estate team is dedicated to supporting the financial needs of our business clients through expert advice, innovative solutions, and relationship-focused service. As an intern with one of our Commercial Real Estate teams, you’ll support credit analysis, financial research, and client service activities. You’ll work closely with Relationship Managers and Credit Analysts to help deliver high-quality insights and service to our clients. You’ll also contribute to projects such as market research, data management, and process improvement initiatives, gaining hands-on experience in commercial banking. This internship is designed to help you build your skills in a collaborative, fast-paced environment while learning from experienced professionals. 

 At CIBC we enable the work environment most optimal for you to thrive in your role. Details on your work arrangement (proportion of on-site and remote work) will be discussed at the time of your interview. 

 How you'll succeed 
 - Support credit analysis - Assist with financial analysis, credit reviews, and report preparation to support client needs and internal decision-making.
- Collaborate with partners - Work closely with Relationship Managers, Credit Analysts, and other team members to support client service and project delivery.
- Contribute to team initiatives - Participate in market research, data clean-up, and business case reviews that help drive results for clients and the bank.
- Build your knowledge - Learn new systems, tools, and processes, and apply feedback to strengthen your skills and performance.
- Deliver with accuracy - Maintain a high level of attention to detail and help ensure quality and consistency across deliverables.
 Who you are 
 - You're analytical. You enjoy investigating problems, working with information, and turning details into meaningful insights.
- You're collaborative. You work well with others and are motivated by shared success in a team environment.
- You're detail-oriented. You know accuracy matters, and you apply critical thinking to support strong decision-making.
- You have relevant education. You're currently enrolled in a degree or diploma program in finance, accounting, business, economics, or a related field.
- You have technical confidence. You're comfortable using spreadsheets and data management tools, and experience with financial analysis is an asset.
- Values matter to you. You bring your real self to work, and you live our values - trust, teamwork, and accountability.
