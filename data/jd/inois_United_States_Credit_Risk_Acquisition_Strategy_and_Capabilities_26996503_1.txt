 Job Description 
 This position resides within Citi's US Consumer Card (USCC) In-Business Risk organization. The role is responsible for leading the design, execution, and governance of underwriting and line assignment strategies across the USCC portfolio, while overseeing the enterprise test-and-learn framework across all consumer products.

 In this role, you will
 - Lead the development and execution of enterprise acquisition risk strategies, balancing growth, customer experience, and credit performance, while ensuring the organization remains at the forefront of industry innovation, data capabilities, and underwriting practices.
- Support management & optimization of the acquisition credit policies for a $150B+ portfolio that generates 15MM new accounts annually
- Continuously evaluate emerging capabilities and partner with data and technology providers to incorporate leading tools, alternative data sources, and advanced analytics into acquisition decisioning.
- Collaborate with Product, Marketing, Data Science, Technology, Operations, and Compliance teams to design, implement, and validate acquisition strategies across traditional and emerging channels.
- Design and lead test-and-learn initiatives, including A/B testing and pilot programs, to validate hypotheses, quantify trade-offs, and optimize underwriting and line assignment strategies.
- Monitor new initiatives and vintage performance at a granular level, conduct root cause analyses, and recommend strategic policy enhancements.
- Present portfolio performance, risk assessments, key insights, and strategic recommendations to senior and executive leadership.
- Lead, mentor, and develop a high-performing team of risk and analytics professionals.
- Ensure adherence to all internal policies, regulatory requirements, and governance expectations.
- Maintain a strong control environment and ensure acquisition strategies are executed in a safe, sound, and compliant manner.
- Champion the acquisition originations platform, aligning business, risk, and technology stakeholders to modernize decisioning, data integration, and strategy deployment capabilities while ensuring seamless execution and measurable business outcomes.

 We will be thrilled to have someone who is
 - An experienced credit policy manager – who has the vision and can adapt to changing technology and the demands of the governance and regulation.
- Creative –who can take a concept and give it shape and structure. Is not afraid to ask questions and loves playing with new ideas.
- Has good Judgment – knows when something does not add up and is not right. Has attention to details and can get into the weeds when needed.
- A Leader – leads by example and is not afraid to challenge status quo. Inspires people with presence. Knows talent when they see it and will create a conducive environment to nurture and grow it.

 Basic Qualification 
 - 15+ years of experience in credit risk management or lesser with relevant experience in acquisitions credit risk
- Experience in the financial services industry preferred
- Demonstrated accountability for delivery of a full range of services to one or more businesses/geographic regions
- Consistently demonstrate clear and concise written and verbal communication
- Proven negotiation skills, often used at a senior level
- Demonstrated ability to synthesize, prioritize and drive results with urgency
- Ability to obtain support and buy-in across a wide range of internal and external audiences

 Education: 
 - Bachelor’s/University degree in statistics, mathematics, economics, or equivalent experience. Master’s/PhD degree is a plus
