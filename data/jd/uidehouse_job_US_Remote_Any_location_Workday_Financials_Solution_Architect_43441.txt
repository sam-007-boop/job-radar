 What You Will Do : 
 - As part of our Workday Practice, you’ll be part of an energized team of advisory professionals who deliver more than just technology

- From initial assessments to entire transformations, you’ll deliver Workday Financial solutions equipping organizations with the information they need to make better business decisions.

- As a Financials Solution Architect, you will:

- Be a hands-on Financials Solution Architect on assigned projects helping to streamline client process

- Oversee and facilitate Design Sessions, capture client requirements, configure creative solutions, and mentor other junior consultants

- Responsibilities include documentation, configuration, testing support, go-live support, and post-production (hypercare) support

- Participate in an innovative, teaming culture for the team to enable constant innovation

- Strong ability to team with project leadership to communicate challenges and improvements to project processes

- Demonstrate consultative skills

- Have a passion for high customer satisfaction levels

- Participate in sales cycle activities

 What You Will Need : 
 - Bachelor's degree with a MINIMUM of SEVEN (7) years of prior Workday Financial implementation experience; OR a Master's degree with a MINIMUM of FIVE (5) years of prior Workday Financial implementation experience. Years of experience can be substituted for a formal degree, such as NO degree with a MINIMUM of ELEVEN (11) years of prior Workday Financial implementation experience

- Workday certification and demonstrated command of at least TWO (2) Workday Financial pillars

- Experience with the execution of projects within a structured methodology

- Experience in an ERP Software Sales cycle (RFx responses, orals presentations, etc.)

- Up to 50% travel balanced with a work from home and/or Guidehouse office schedule - consultants may provide on-site support for key milestones as needed

 What Would Be Nice To Have : 
 - A proven role in at least five full Workday Financial implementations

- Solid knowledge of Microsoft Office Suite, focused on Microsoft Excel, Microsoft Word and PowerPoint

- Strong written and oral communication skills (RFP responses, white papers, etc.) and presentation skills such as Workday demonstrations and client presentations

- Implementations involving deploying within a state or local government is preferred

- Leading Foundation Data Model (FDM) design for state or local government

The annual salary range for this position is $135,000.00-$225,000.00. Compensation decisions depend on a wide range of factors, including but not limited to skill sets, experience and training, security clearances, licensure and certifications, and other business and organizational needs.
