Associate Operations Specialist 
Location: Remote (Candidates in Arizona strongly preferred) 

 Ready to make a difference? 
To support our growth, we are seeking candidates with an interest in utility energy efficiency programs and knowledge of energy-efficient technologies associated with commercial building systems. As an Associate Operations Specialist , you will be responsible for identifying opportunities for process improvements, sharing best practices, collaborating with cross-functional teams, and conducting thorough analyses of program operations. This position provides administrative and invoicing support. The role supports monthly invoicing activities, including report reconciliation, documentation tracking, and data entry into invoicing systems. 
 Candidates must be located in the Pacific Time Zone to be considered) 

 Why you will love working here:   
 - Quality of life: Flexible workplace arrangements, work-life balance  
- Investment of the community: Donation matching, volunteer opportunities  
- Investment in you: Tuition reimbursement, access to professional development resources, 401k matching, Employee Stock Purchase Plan  
- And many, many more (Ask your recruiter for more details!)  

 What you will be doing:  
 - Work as part of the energy efficiency team ensuring that the evaluation, implementation, and verification of utility-sponsored energy efficiency projects are successfully executed.
- Collaborate with IT, Reporting, Marketing, and Customer Care teams.
- Conduct analyses to identify trends, issues, and opportunities for improvement. 
- Assist with processing incoming project applications.  
- Review project application forms and submittals to ensure compliance with program policies and procedures.
- Coordination with program staff, billing specialists, and project leads to support monthly invoicing timelines
- Support basic quality control checks to ensure invoices are complete, accurate, and submitted on time  
- Assist with documentation updates and process improvements related to invoicing and program administration 

 Skills you will use:  
 - Energy Program Operations: Understanding of utility-sponsored energy efficiency programs and commercial building technologies. 
- Process & Compliance Review: Skilled in reviewing project applications and ensuring adherence to program policies and procedures.
- Cross-Functional Collaboration: Experience working with IT, Reporting, Marketing, and Customer Care teams to support program delivery.
- Analytical Thinking: Ability to identify trends and opportunities for improvement through data analysis. Ability to follow established processes and meet recurring monthly deadlines 
- Customer & Contractor Support: Capable of handling inquiries and coordinating follow-ups for inspections and surveys.
- Technical Proficiency: Strong skills in Microsoft Excel and Office Suite; familiarity with Power BI, SharePoint Lists, and Power Automate.

 What we need you to have (minimum qualifications) 
 - Bachelor’s degree (or applicants can substitute one year of related experience for one year of education)
- 1+ year(s) professional experience in administration, operational support, data analysis, energy and/or related experience

 What we would like you to have (preferred qualifications)   
 - Strong time management, communication, and organization skills.
- Must be experienced in Microsoft Office Suite (Excel, Outlook, PowerPoint), with demonstrated experience in Excel. 
- Outstanding analytical, writing, and communication skills. 
- Willingness to learn invoicing systems, charge codes, and compliance requirements
- Knowledge of the utility market (specifically energy efficiency, demand response, and distributed energy resource programs and markets).
- Experience with Power BI (or similar data visualization tool).
- Experience with advanced Microsoft data software such as SharePoint Lists and Power Automate.
