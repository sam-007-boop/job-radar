At JPMorganChase, we recognize that rewarding careers do not always follow a conventional path. We value the diversity, fresh perspective and wealth of experience that returning professionals can bring.
 The ReEntry program offers experienced professionals, who are currently on an extended career break of at least two years, the support and resources needed to relaunch their careers. The program spans over 30 locations worldwide.
 The ReEntry Program is a 15-week fellowship program, beginning April 19, 2027  and ending July 30, 2027  with the prospect of an offer for permanent employment with JPMorgan Chase at the end of the program. The permanent placements will be based on both business needs and candidate skill set. 
 Please refer to our  ReEntry Overview page for further information regarding the Program 
 What We Look For 
 We are looking for professionals in our United States Center locations with previous experience including but not limited to;                
 - Agility Leads
- Business Analysts
- Client Experience Specialist
- Data Analytics
- Marketing/Media Solutions
- Process Improvement
- Product Associates
- Product Delivery
- Product Managers
- Regulatory Compliance
- Sales Strategy
- Travel Advisors
- Travel Support
- Vendor Management

 Eligibility Requirements: 
 - Must have been out of paid employment for at least 24 consecutive months before the program begins. 
- We cannot sponsor visa applications for this program
