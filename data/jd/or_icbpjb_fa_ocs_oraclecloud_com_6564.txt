Lazard is one of the world’s preeminent financial advisory and asset management firms.  Our people and culture make the difference . While global in presence and reach, ours is a close, collaborative   community of just over 3,000 professionals.  Through our financial advisory business, Lazard advises clients around the world on strategic and financial matters including mergers and acquisitions, restructurings, capital structure and capital raising. Our client relationships are built on trust and discretion. As the world’s largest advisory-focused firm, we have exceptional depth of expertise across industry sectors and geographies. We value the rich diversity that comes from blending local perspective with our global network.
 In Lazard’s Financial Institutions Group, our Analysts work in a generalist capacity spanning the following verticals: Insurance, Asset Management, Alternative Asset Management, Banks, Broker-Dealers, Specialty Finance, Financial Technology and Exchanges, among other areas.

 We’ll trust you to: 
 - Analyze client financial and performance information 
- Run financial models and conduct analysis
- Perform company and industry research
- Assist in the preparation of client presentations and marketing materials
- Interact with senior bankers and clients

 You’ll need to have: 
 - One year of investment banking experience, preferably in FIG covering Insurance, Banking, Asset Management and / or FinTech
- Strong analytical skills with training in the building of presentation materials and financial modelling
- Proven academic excellence with excellent attention to detail and high standards of accuracy 
- Team player mindset; ability to work as part of a close-knit team 
- Strong interpersonal skills and ability to communicate both to deal teams and external clients
- Strong ability to multi-task 
- High level of responsiveness with a strong work ethic and commitment to maintaining a high-quality work product  
- Fluency in the use of AI tools and prompt engineering
