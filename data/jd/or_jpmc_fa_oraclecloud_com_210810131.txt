JPMorgan Chase — Business Manager - Business Banking Product Deposit
https://jpmc.fa.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1001/job/210810131

Leverage your problem-solving skills to thrive in a fast-paced environment and drive customer-centric strategies. 
 As the Business Manager within Business Banking Deposits & Cash Management, you serve as a strategic and execution partner to the Head of Business Banking Deposits & Cash Management, helping promote the business agenda and translate priorities into coordinated execution.

 As a Business Manager in in Business Banking Product Deposits team, you will manage the Deposits & Cash Management book of work, providing transparency across priorities, sequencing, capacity, dependencies, and delivery. Partnering across Product, Technology, Finance, Risk, and Agile teams, the Business Manager connects business outcomes to the roadmap, surfaces trade offs, and keeps resources focused on the highest value priorities.

 The role also manages the organization’s operating rhythm, including business reviews, strategic planning, prioritization, executive reporting, governance, and follow-through on key decisions

 Job Responsibilities: 
 - Implement new business strategy, strategic initiatives or platforms, including aligning department and support groups (Finance, Tech, Ops, Legal, Compliance)
- Identify, escalate and mitigate business risks that could impair our ability to do business: e.g. legal, tax, regulatory, capacity issues, extraordinary transaction costs
- Analyze financial performance, including expenses; identify productivity initiatives and drive implementation
- Partner with the business, Operations and Technology in the definition of future workflows and implementation of related technology and infrastructure projects to prepare the business to meet the requirements of an ever evolving market structure
- Business representation in respective internal/external working groups. Lead planning and manage logistics to support the Head of Product
- Coordinate the product team’s book of work, including tracking incoming requests from field teams. Assess and prioritize requests, helping ensure the most important work receives appropriate attention
- Support communication about priorities, progress, and decisions across relevant stakeholders
- Understand the team’s reporting and help coordinate or interpret reporting as needed. Support the financial aspects of the product portfolio, including understanding its P&L.

 Required qualifications, capabilities, and skills 
 - Bachelor’s degree in Business, Finance, Economics, or other related area
- Previous product knowledge and/or prior Business Management or COO experience
- Experience using work-management and delivery tools such as Monday.com, Jira, or comparable Jira aligned tools
- Highly motivated self-starter with excellent time management/prioritization skills
- Able to forge strong internal relationships across a broad range of functions. Self-motivated, tenacious and able to work with high degree of independence
- Excellent written and oral communication skills to present to senior leaders
- Able to both define and deliver to conclusion a strategic agenda across multiple groups
- Strong analytical and problem solving skills with ability to analyze large data sets and present conclusions concisely
- Excellent project management and organizational skills. Attention to detail with logical thought process

 Preferred qualifications, capabilities, and skills  
 - Familiarity with deposits as a banking product.
