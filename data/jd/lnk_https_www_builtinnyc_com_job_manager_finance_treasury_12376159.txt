Do you want to leverage your analytical skills and be part of an exciting, fast-paced environment surrounded by smart, inclusive, and energetic individuals? Come and join our Capital One Travel Treasury team! 
As a Manager, Treasury, you will sit at the intersection of traditional cash management and next-generation financial technology. This twofold role requires you to oversee traditional treasury and liquidity functions while independently developing and leading Treasury-related AI and automation initiatives. Bridging the gap between our daily operational execution and our long-term strategic vision, you will architect the automated systems that replace manual workflows, oversee complex cash positioning. We are looking for a tech-forward treasury expert who can reliably run our business as usual (BAU) activities while building the AI-driven infrastructure of the future. 
 What You'll Do (Responsibilities): 
 - Treasury & Cash Management: Oversee daily liquidity operations, execute funding strategies, manage bank account administration, and refine our cash forecasting methodology to ensure optimal capital allocation. Participate in the team rotation performing regular cash reconciliations to maintain accurate financial accounting 
- AI & Automation Leadership: Develop, lead, and deploy AI-driven automation to modernize treasury operations. Engineer automated predictive cash models, streamline transactional workflows, and leverage large datasets to shift processes from reactive to proactive. 
- Business Engagement & Strategic Alignment: Actively engage with the travel business on new initiatives to represent the Treasury team and ensure treasury requirements are integrated early in product and strategic rollouts. 
- Financial Analysis & Reporting: Produce detailed financial modeling, variance explanations, and treasury KPI dashboards to support the Senior Manager and leadership in strategic decision-making. 
- Risk & Control Environment: Maintain and enhance the treasury control environment. Ensure flawless operational execution and proper Segregation of Duties (SOD) while utilizing automation to mitigate potential operational risks. 
- Cross-Functional Collaboration: Act as the primary bridge between Treasury operations, Tech, and Product teams to integrate AI solutions, modernize treasury infrastructure, and support an increased volume of specialized cash management requests. Experience leading project management or large initiatives to deliver treasury capabilities for new products or business rollouts is preferred. 

 What we are looking for: 
 - Treasury Excellence: Demonstrate deep subject matter expertise in liquidity management, cash forecasting, and financial controls to ensure flawless operational execution and robust risk mitigation. 
- Innovation & Automation: Act as a proactive self-starter who independently identifies operational inefficiencies, conceptualizing and deploying AI-driven solutions to modernize legacy treasury workflows. 
- Ownership: Take end-to-end accountability for bridging the gap between traditional daily cash operations and the strategic deployment of new technological enhancements. 
- Agility & Prioritization: Thrive in a highly dynamic, fast-paced environment, seamlessly juggling multiple initiatives and shifting priorities without compromising accuracy or performance. 

 Basic Qualifications: 
 - Bachelor's Degree or military experience 
- At least 4 years of experience in treasury, risk, audit, finance, or in capital markets 
- At least 4 years of experience in financial modeling and financial analysis 
- At least 3 years' experience managing a Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) function 

 Preferred Qualifications: 
 - Master's Degree in Business Administration (MBA), or Master's Degree in Finance, Accounting, Financial Engineering, Economics, or other quantitative field of study 
- Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) license, or Certified Treasury Professional (CTP) license, or Financial Risk Manager (FRM) license, or Certified Public Accountant (CPA) license 
- Project Management Professional (PMP) or Agile certification 
- 5+ years of experience in financial modeling and financial analysis 
- 2+ years of experience explaining technical subject matter to non-technical audiences, and leading discussions across functions to ensure clarity around business direction and strategy 
- Experience influencing senior level management, and developing strong relationships with both internal and external partners

 What you need to know about the NYC Tech Scene

 As the undisputed financial capital of the world, New York City is an epicenter of startup funding activity. The city has a thriving fintech scene and is a major player in verticals ranging from AI to biotech, cybersecurity and digital media. It also has universities like NYU, Columbia and Cornell Tech attracting students and researchers from across the globe, providing the ecosystem with a constant influx of world-class talent. And its East Coast location and three international airports make it a perfect spot for European companies establishing a foothold in the United States.
