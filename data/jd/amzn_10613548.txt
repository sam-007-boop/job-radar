The Insights, Planning, Econometrics & Decision Making (IPED) team is the Subject Matter Expert (SME) for all Alexa financial planning and analysis matters. Sitting at the intersection of business strategy, financial modeling, and data-driven decision-making, the team drives Alexa's long-term strategic and financial planning, produces leadership-facing documentation, and delivers high-fidelity analyses that shape how senior leaders evaluate performance and allocate resources. As Alexa continues to grow & transform, the IPED team plays an increasingly critical role in translating complex financial signals into actionable insights for the organization.

We are seeking a Finance Manager to join the IPED team and take end-to-end ownership of key financial planning and analytical workstreams. This role will partner closely with business and finance leaders to develop robust processes, deliver high-quality financial plans, and surface insights that drive strategic decisions. The ideal candidate is a self-starter who thrives in ambiguity, can build checks and balances into complex analyses, and communicates financial narratives clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences.

Key Responsibilities
Financial Planning & Forecasting — Own and deliver Alexa's financial planning cycles (OP1, OP2, monthly/quarterly forecasts), ensuring accuracy, timeliness, and alignment with organizational goals.

Business Performance Analysis — Conduct deep-dive analyses on revenue, cost, and key business metrics; identify trends, risks, and opportunities; and present findings with actionable recommendations to leadership.

Strategic Decision Support — Partner with cross-functional teams to model business scenarios, evaluate investment trade-offs, and provide quantitative frameworks that inform resource allocation and long-term strategy.

Leadership Reporting & Documentation — Prepare executive-level financial narratives, strategic focus area documents, and business review materials that clearly articulate performance drivers and forward-looking outlooks.

Process Improvement & Automation — Identify and implement opportunities to enhance planning accuracy, streamline reporting workflows, and build scalable, repeatable processes that raise the bar on analytical rigor.

Stakeholder Partnership — Build trusted relationships across Alexa business teams, acting as a thought partner on financial and operational matters and ensuring alignment between finance and business objectives.
