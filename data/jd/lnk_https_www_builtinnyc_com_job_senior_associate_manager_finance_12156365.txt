 About the Role: 
 Click Therapeutics is seeking a Senior Associate / Manager, Finance to join our Finance team, reporting to the Head of Finance. This is a generalist role at the center of a lean team, combining work like budgeting, reporting, and accounting support with the opportunity to reshape how the work gets done using AI. 
 Together with Finance leadership, you will help shape our AI transformation and serve as the hands-on builder who brings it to life. We are looking for an AI-native finance professional who can re-architect our processes and systems, automating routine work and embedding AI into how we close the books, forecast, and report. The ideal candidate is equally comfortable tackling complex work like revenue forecasting and rolling up their sleeves on the day-to-day tasks that keep the function running. 
 This position is based out of Click’s headquarters located in Tribeca, NYC, at the center of one of the fastest-growing digital health communities. We have a hybrid working model that consists of at least 4 days in office each week. 
 Responsibilities: 
 Financial Planning & Analysis 
 - Forecasting & Budgeting: Develop and maintain the company’s budgets, forecasts, and financial models, including revenue forecasting, and keep them current as the business evolves. 
- Management & Board Reporting: Prepare management reporting and contribute to board and investor materials, translating results into clear, actionable insights for leadership. 
- Variance & Performance Analysis: Analyze actuals against budget, identify the drivers behind performance, and flag risks and opportunities early. 
- Business Partnership: Work with leaders across the company to support planning, vendor and spend decisions, and day-to-day financial questions, building scenario analyses as needed to inform strategic and operating decisions. 
 Accounting & Finance Operations 
 - Accounting & Controllership Support: Partner with the Controller and step in as needs arise across the close process, accounts payable, cash, and audit and tax support. 
- Controls & Documentation: Prepare entries and supporting schedules with clear documentation, keeping review and control standards in place as processes evolve. 
 AI-Enabled Finance Transformation 
 - Process Re-Architecture: Partner with Finance leadership to assess existing finance workflows and systems, such as invoice processing and accrual calculations, and redesign them to reduce manual effort and embed AI where it creates meaningful leverage. 
- Design & Implementation: Develop, prototype, and deploy AI-enabled workflows and automations, and bring an independent perspective on the right tools and integrations for our needs. 
- Measurement & Impact: Document solutions, measure their results, and help set the standard for how AI is applied across Finance. 
- Continuous Improvement: Identify the next opportunity for improvement, with sound judgment on where automation can be trusted and where human review remains essential.
