JPMorgan Chase — 2027 Latin American Debt Capital Markets Analyst Internship – Emerging Talent Summer Experience Program
https://jpmc.fa.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1001/job/210811409

If you’re ambitious and eager to apply your knowledge to real-world financial scenarios, this internship offers an opportunity to work with sophisticated financing solutions in dynamic global markets. You’ll develop practical skills, learn from experienced professionals, and gain exposure to transactions that help clients achieve their strategic financing objectives.
 As a 2027 Latin America Debt Capital Markets Analyst Intern, you will support the execution of international bond transactions for high-grade and high-yield clients across Latin America and the Caribbean. You will work alongside experienced professionals to analyze financing alternatives, prepare client materials, support transaction execution, and evaluate market and company information. Through this experience, you’ll develop an understanding of debt capital markets while collaborating with colleagues and external partners across global markets.
 Job responsibilities 
 - Support the execution of international bond transactions for clients across Latin America and the Caribbean
- Prepare client presentations, proposals, market updates, transaction reviews, and other marketing materials
- Gather, organize, and analyze market information to support client discussions and transaction execution
- Analyze financing and refinancing alternatives across currencies and market conditions
- Evaluate client credit and financial information and benchmark companies against global industry peers
- Build comparable company analyses and other analytical materials to support financing recommendations
- Support transaction documentation, due diligence, and internal processes throughout deal execution
- Coordinate working group calls, transaction materials, and other deal-related activities
- Support roadshow planning and logistics for client transactions
- Collaborate with internal partners and external parties, including legal counsel, rating agencies, and auditors
- Maintain organized and accurate information while supporting multiple transactions and team priorities
 Required qualifications, capabilities, and skills 
 - Strong interest in investment banking, debt capital markets, and global financial markets
- Excellent analytical, quantitative, and interpretative skills, including knowledge of algebra, statistics, and probability
- Ability to thrive in a dynamic, collaborative work environment
- Adaptability, flexibility, and resilience when managing changing priorities
- Fluency in English and Spanish, including the ability to easily read, write, speak, and comprehend both languages
- Coursework in finance, economics, or a related discipline
- Expected graduation date of December 2028 or later, but no later than June 2029, from a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree program
- If pursuing a Master’s degree, the program must be within two years of receiving your Bachelor’s degree
 Preferred Qualifications, Capabilities, and Skills 
 - Excellent verbal and written communication skills
- Confidence and initiative to take ownership of responsibilities and projects
- Portuguese language skills
