About this role 

 Company: BlackRock Financial Management, Inc.
 Job Title: Associate
 Location: 50 Hudson Yards, New York, NY 10001

 Job Duties: Construct active asset allocation strategies and whole portfolio solutions across a wide spectrum of commingled funds, separate accounts, model portfolios, and outsourcing solutions in the wealth and institutional channels. Independently and in teams, evaluate and provide thoughtful recommendations on equity or fixed income strategies and asset classes across the globe. Make well researched recommendations on asset class opportunities and identify investment managers best able to complete these. Work closely with BlackRock multi-manager investments teams and tailor investment solutions to our global clients. Actively own recommended investment strategies and ongoing monitoring. Participate in the design and ongoing management of portfolios with well-researched proposals for strategic investments. Document research findings from initial due diligence and ongoing monitoring with well-prepared reports summarizing manager performance and portfolio positioning. Stay up to date with products and trends in the investment management industry. Represent BlackRock and Manager Research in client meetings and to the investment industry.

 Qualifications: Bachelor's degree or foreign equivalent in Statistics, Business, or related field and 36 months of experience in job offered, or closely related role. Must have three (3) years of experience with: • Equity and Fixed Income; • Sourcing investment ideas, conducting statistical analysis using eVestment, Morningstar, and Bloomberg; • Risk Modeling and Financial Modeling; • Calculating and analyzing risk with regard to asset classes and portfolios; • Creating Risk Reports; • Portfolio construction; • Equity, fixed income, liquid alternatives, and hedge funds; • Asset management; • Sourcing and conducting Investment due diligence on managers; • Python and SQL; and • Writing and presenting technical investment memos for internal and external stakeholders.
