 What You Will Do: 

 - Design and execute stakeholder engagement strategies that support federal programs, initiatives, and organizational objectives.
- Plan, coordinate, and facilitate webinars, workshops, listening sessions, focus groups, and stakeholder engagement forums.
- Develop strategic communications products, including newsletters, briefing materials, email campaigns, presentations, and outreach materials.
- Translate complex policies, procedures, and technical information into clear, accessible stakeholder-facing communications.
- Support conference participation and external engagement efforts through development of messaging, presentations, and communications strategies.
- Analyze stakeholder feedback and engagement metrics to identify trends and recommend improvements.
- Develop dashboards, performance reports, and continuous improvement recommendations that enhance program effectiveness and stakeholder experience.

 What You Will Need: 

 - Must be able to OBTAIN and MAINTAIN a Federal or DoD "PUBLIC TRUST"; candidates must obtain approved adjudication of their PUBLIC TRUST prior to onboarding with Guidehouse.
- Due to contract requirements, candidates must be U.S. Citizens and reside within the United States.
- Bachelor's degree from an accredited college or university.
- Five (5 ) years of consulting, communications, stakeholder engagement, public affairs, or program support experience.
- Experience planning and facilitating meetings, workshops, webinars, or stakeholder engagement activities.
- Exceptional written communication and editing skills.
- Experience developing professional communications products for executive, technical, and public-facing audiences.
- Strong analytical skills with the ability to derive insights from stakeholder feedback and engagement data.
- Experience managing multiple priorities and interacting directly with clients and stakeholders.
- Demonstrated ability to communicate complex topics clearly and effectively.
- Candidates must reside within commuting distance (approximately 50 miles) of the Washington, DC metropolitan area.

 What Would Be Nice To Have: 

 - Experience supporting federal government clients.
- Experience developing communications strategies, outreach campaigns, or stakeholder engagement plans.
- Experience supporting grants programs, technical assistance programs, or public sector initiatives.
- Familiarity with webinar platforms, virtual event management, and adult learning principles.
- Experience developing newsletters, GovDelivery campaigns, or digital communications products.
- Experience using Power BI or similar tools to analyze engagement trends and performance metrics.
- Experience supporting transportation, infrastructure, community development, or human services programs.
- Understanding of change management, customer experience, or human-centered service delivery approaches.
