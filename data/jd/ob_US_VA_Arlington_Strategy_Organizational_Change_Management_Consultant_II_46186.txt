 What You Will Do : 

 - Support multidisciplinary consulting engagements focused on strategy, organizational transformation, business process improvement, and change management.
- Support organizational change efforts, including reorganizations, operating model shifts, culture change, technology adoption, and leadership transitions.
- Support requirements-gathering activities through client interviews, workshops, focus groups, and working sessions.
- Collect, analyze, and synthesize qualitative and quantitative data to identify findings, gaps, and opportunities.
- Develop client deliverables such as gap analyses, findings and recommendations, requirements documents, process flows, strategic plans, SOPs, presentations, and communications.
- Support OCM activities, including stakeholder analysis, change impact assessments, communications, engagement, training, and adoption.
- Document current- and future-state processes and identify opportunities for improvement.
- Conduct research and translate findings into clear, actionable recommendations.
- Prepare materials for client meetings, workshops, and leadership briefings.
- Manage multiple assignments and deadlines in a fast-paced client environment.
- Build strong working relationships with clients and project team members.

 What You Will Need : 

 - Must be able to OBTAIN and MAINTAIN a Federal or DoD "PUBLIC TRUST".
- Must be a US Citizen due to nature of client engagements.
- Bachelor’s degree in business, management, organizational development, communications, public administration, human resources, or a related field.
- One (1) to Three (3) years of experience in consulting, strategy, organizational change management, organizational transformation, process improvement, or a related field.
- Strong analytical, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.
- Experience developing presentations, reports, analyses, or other professional deliverables.
- Ability to synthesize complex information into clear findings and recommendations.
- Ability to work independently and collaboratively as part of a consulting team.
- Strong organization, attention to detail, and time-management skills.
- Proficiency in Microsoft PowerPoint, Word, Excel, and Teams.

 What Would Be Nice To Have : 

 - Experience supporting federal government clients.
- Experience with organizational change management, stakeholder engagement, or strategic communications.
- Experience with process mapping, organizational assessments, or strategic planning.
- Familiarity with change management approaches such as Prosci ADKAR.
- Experience with tools such as Visio, Lucidchart, or Miro.
