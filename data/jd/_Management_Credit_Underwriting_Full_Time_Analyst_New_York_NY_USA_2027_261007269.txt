 Corporate and Leveraged Finance Underwriting (“CLFU”) team in North America. Citi ICM-Corporate and Leverage Finance Underwriting Team will be actively involved in Citi’s credit approval and origination process for episodic transactions, assisting clients in raising funds in the capital markets, as well as providing ongoing credit monitoring support for Citi’s Corporate and Leveraged Finance Portfolio of client relationships. Our Underwriters have a comprehensive understanding of the wide range of complex financial issues facing our clients and can provide Analysts the opportunity to develop a sound foundation of credit skills. Combined with an appreciation of the broad set of services offered by Citi, this understanding allows us to effectively deliver innovative solutions to our clients, as well as properly manage the credit exposure within Citi’s global portfolio, across Corporates and Leveraged Lending. This is being achieved through a strong partnership with Banking, Capital Markets and Risk.

 Your time here will look something like this... 
 - Working with a team covering Corporate and Leveraged Finance Underwriting, from Analysts to Managing Directors
- Evaluating and gaining a strong understanding of clients' business model, financial performance and key credit drivers across various industries (including specialized sectors), through due diligence process as part of transactions. Developing ability to understand nuances of local markets, local regulation, and the capabilities and practices of competitors
- Providing analytical support to Underwriters across CLFU’s episodic transactions; by drafting of the Credit Approval Memo (CAM) package, which includes conducting financial analysis, projection modeling, risk ratings and other in-depth analysis. Actively participate in due diligence process to evaluate credit decisions, coordinating with stakeholders across Banking, Capital Markets and Risk. Presenting in credit committees.
- Researching and developing robust understanding of the fundamentals of the key industry trends and risk issues and developing the ability to succinctly convey his/her thoughts and opinions to the Risk Managers and Bankers; providing well-reasoned, balanced assessments of risk/return trade-offs associated with transactions and client relationships
- Building and analyzing models used for valuation and to forecast the client's operating and financial performance and be able to articulate assumptions and analysis to Underwriters and Risk Managers;
- Collaborating with our product partners in Capital Markets, Investment Banking, Corporate Banking and Risk, in addition to Legal counsel (both internal and external) to develop the ability to understand the deal structures and to ensure they are properly documented and approved as per the Citi WCR Standards & Procedures
- Shadowing Analysts by attending company and industry meetings as well as industry conferences. Creating and sustaining a network of strong partnerships with the business in all segments & geographies and other Risk functions

 We will provide you with the knowledge and skills you need to succeed.
 We're committed to teaching you the ropes. Our Full-Time Program is two and a half years long. You'll start the Program with three weeks of training, which will introduce you to both Citi and Institutional Credit Management as a whole.

This training gives you: 
 - An overview of the key processes you should know as a new Citi employee
- An introduction to Corporate and Leveraged Finance Underwriting to help you discover the main business areas and the "ins and outs" of the credit industry; you'll also get to know Citi's end-to-end Leveraged Lending credit management process.
- Networking events across businesses
- Access to the most senior leaders in the firm
- Social and networking opportunities
- Mentorship
