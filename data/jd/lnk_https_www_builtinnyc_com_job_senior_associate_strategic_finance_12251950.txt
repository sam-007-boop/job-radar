 About this team and role: 
 Strategic Finance is a high-visibility, high-impact role embedded in the company’s decision-making core. You will partner with our executive, product, and go-to-market (GTM) teams, owning the financial modeling and analysis that drives our product and corporate strategy, optimizes our GTM engine, and informs critical operational decisions. 
 What you’ll do:  
 - Partner with Product and GTM leaders to evaluate the financial ROI and viability of new products, business lines, and R&D investments. 
- Own the long-range plan, connecting product and revenue metrics to financial outcomes and keeping reporting accurate, timely, and consistent. 
- Build and maintain financial models that translate operating plans into outcomes, risks, and opportunities, including unit economics, pricing analysis, and scenario planning. 
- Lead cross-functional initiatives on resource allocation, operational efficiency, and scaling business processes. 
 What you’ll bring:  
 - 5+ years of experience in strategic finance, FP&A, corporate finance, or investment banking. 
- Bachelor’s degree in finance, accounting, economics, or a related field, or equivalent experience. 
- Strong financial modeling skills, with a rigorous, quantitative approach to forecasting, budgeting, and measuring operational performance. 
- Exceptional communication skills, with the ability to turn complex financial concepts into clear narratives for non-financial stakeholders and to partner with senior leaders. 
- A high standard for operational excellence and a clear sense of how to turn ambiguous business questions into actionable plans. 
- Experience using AI tools to work faster and better, and a willingness to introduce them to your team and cross-functional partners. 
- Commitment to our values: 
 - Welcoming differences 
- Being relationship-minded 
- Practicing responsible participation 
- Having grit 
 Nice to haves: 
 - Experience with software, internet, consumption-based, subscription, advertising, or AI-driven business models. 
- Experience with ERP and planning tools such as NetSuite, Workday, Pigment, or Adaptive.
