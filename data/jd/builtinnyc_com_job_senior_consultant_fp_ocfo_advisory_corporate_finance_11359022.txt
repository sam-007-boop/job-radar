 What You’ll Do   

  The Senior Consultant will collaborate with various internal teams and help support and execute client engagements by. You will utilize your engaging and collaborative style to: 

 - Developing knowledge management, including tools, methodologies, reports, models, work plans, checklists and processes. 
- Performing high quality client work and developing client relationships for CFO advisory service assignments, including taking a proactive approach and anticipating client needs 
- Assisting with internal practice development and marketing, including developing materials, surveys, events and strategic case studies. 
- Helping lead engagement work streams and projects with limited oversight. 
- Serve as a positive role model to train, teach, and coach to junior team members.  

 How You’ll Grow   

 The performance expectation for our client’s finance function is at a new peak, and the demands placed on a CFO have never been greater.  This is where you can step in and make an immediate impact. This is an excellent opportunity for an individual with proven, hands-on project delivery experience to work with our Fortune 500 clients to help them plan, design, and execute key financial technologies to streamline their financial operations.  

 What You Will Need To Succeed   

 Basic Qualifications   
 - Undergraduate or Masters’ degree in a Business related major or Economics. 
- 3+ years of experience in financial consulting or advisory, or in a Corporate rotational program focused on developing financial leaders, or in execution or leadership roles in an industry finance, accounting or FP&A function 
- Experience as an external (or internal consultant) with demonstrated experience in 1 or more of the following areas: FP&A, Accounting & Financial Reporting, Finance Transformation, Finance Technology, and/or Cash, Cost, and Capital 
- Ability to quickly synthesize and advise on complex financial and operational concepts and issues; and translate those to clear and actionable recommendations and insights
- Expertise with Microsoft Excel and ability to develop financial models with minimal oversight
- Ability to travel to client sites and FTI office(s) as needed

 Preferred Qualifications 
 - Experience with business intelligence tools and/or data analytics is preferred
- Certified Public Accountant (CPA) certification, Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) certification, Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) certification, or MBA is preferred
