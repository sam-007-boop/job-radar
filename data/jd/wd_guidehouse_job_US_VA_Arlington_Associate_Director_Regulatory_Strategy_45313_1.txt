 What You Will Do: 
 - Lead regulatory strategy activities in support of Energy Transition initiatives, helping shape approaches that align with business objectives and regulatory requirements.
- Develop and execute regulatory plans, identify risks and opportunities, and provide strategic guidance to cross-functional stakeholders.
- Monitor evolving policy and regulatory developments, assess potential impacts, and translate findings into actionable recommendations.
- Collaborate with internal teams to support process improvement, strengthen governance, and ensure consistency in regulatory submissions and related materials.
- Build and maintain effective relationships with internal partners and relevant external stakeholders to advance strategic priorities.
- Support decision-making through analysis, issue framing, and clear communication of regulatory implications.

 What You Will Need: 
 - Must be a US Permanent Resident or US Citizen due to nature of client engagements.
- High School diploma AND Eleven (11+) plus years post-graduation work experience in the Utilities or Energy industry; Or Bachelor’s degree AND Seven (7+) plus years post-graduation work experience in the Utilities or Energy industry; Or Master's degree AND Five (5+) plus years post-graduation work experience in the Utilities or Energy industry.
- Demonstrated experience in policy and regulatory strategy, regulatory affairs, public policy, or a related discipline for energy affiliated organizations.
- Strong knowledge of regulatory frameworks, policy interpretation, and strategic planning.
- Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and process improvement skills.
- Ability to work effectively in a remote or distributed environment and manage multiple priorities.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to influence and engage stakeholders at all levels.
- Experience collaborating cross-functionally in a fast-paced, complex environment.
- Ability to travel.
- Ability to work onsite in a Guidehouse Office or Client Office location.

 What Would Be Nice To Have: 
 - Preference will be given to candidates residing within 50 miles of a Guidehouse Office or Client Office location.
- Experience supporting utilities, energy, or infrastructure-related regulatory initiatives.
- Experience working with external regulators, industry groups, or public agencies.
- Prior leadership experience in regulatory strategy or policy development.
- Familiarity with change management, organizational transformation, or operational improvement initiatives.
- Experience directly supporting Business Development initiatives or directly accountable for Business Development activities.
