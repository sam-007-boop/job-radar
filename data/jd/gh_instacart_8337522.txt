About the Job Responsible for analyzing and developing financial planning, budgeting, and forecasting to ensure accuracy and alignment with company goals. Duties include:

 Driving automation and process improvements

 Other duties as assigned. 

 About You Minimum Qualifications 

 Bachelor’s degree in Economics, Finance, or related quantitative field (or foreign equivalent).
