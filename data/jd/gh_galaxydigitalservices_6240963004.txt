Who We Are: 
 Galaxy Digital Inc. (Nasdaq: GLXY) is a global leader in digital assets and data center infrastructure, growing the economy that runs on code. Galaxy delivers the onchain infrastructure that connects institutions to digital assets, including trading, advisory, asset management, staking, self-custody, and tokenization. Galaxy also develops and operates data center infrastructure to power AI and HPC workloads. Anchored by its Helios campus in Texas, Galaxy is building a multi-gigawatt pipeline of more than 5.7 GW of potential capacity, positioning it among the largest and fastest-growing data center developers in North America. 
 The Company is headquartered in New York City, with offices across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia. 
 Additional information about Galaxy's businesses and products is available on www.galaxy.com .

 About the Galaxy Summer Internship Program 
 The Galaxy NYC Summer Internship Program is an immersive, nine-week experience taking place on-site in our New York HQ office. During the nine weeks, you will gain hands-on, practical work experience on our Investment Banking, working on real products and problems alongside the people building them. Interns join a lean team advising crypto and fintech companies on M&A, capital raising, and corporate strategy. In addition to learning on the job, Galaxy interns participate in professional development programming, social events, and networking opportunities with senior leaders. 
 Program Dates : June 7th – August 6th, 2027 
 What You'll Do: 

 Build and review financial analyses and valuation models, including comparable companies, precedent transactions, DCF, and merger models. 
 Research companies and protocols using public filings, earnings calls, industry research, and onchain data. 
 Map buyer and investor universes, analyze competitive landscapes, and assess business models and value drivers. 
 Prepare pitch materials, management presentations, teasers, and internal committee memos. 
 Support live deals through preparation, data room maintenance, and process tracking. 
 Use AI tools to accelerate research and analysis, improve internal processes, and help develop AI-enabled workflows and agents. 

 What We're Looking For: 

 A genuine interest in crypto and investment banking, demonstrated through independent research, coursework, projects, or relevant experience. 
 Initiative, intellectual curiosity, and sound judgement, with the ability to work through unfamiliar problems and ask thoughtful questions. 
 Strong analytical skills and a foundational understanding of accounting, corporate finance, and valuation. 
 Familiarity with blockchain technology, token economics, and how value accrues to companies, protocols, and tokens. 
 Strong attention to detail, a collaborative approach, and the ability to manage competing deadlines. 
 Clear, concise written and verbal communication, and the ability to simplify complex ideas. 

 Bonus Points: 

 Previous internship or work experience in crypto or investment banking. 
 Financial or valuation models for companies, tokens, or protocols. 
 Experience using AI tools beyond chat interfaces, including APIs, agents, or automated research and analysis workflows. 
 Published writing, research or other work demonstrating original thinking about crypto businesses, protocols, or markets. 
 Programming or data analysis experience (Python, SQL).

 Currently enrolled in an undergraduate degree program, with an expected graduation date between December 2027 and June 2029.
