 What You Will Do : 
 The Financial Analyst SME supports a Department of Justice (DOJ) component by delivering advanced financial analysis, data validation, and internal control support across multiple federal financial management systems. This role enhances the integrity, accuracy, and timeliness of debt‑related financial processing and reporting, contributing directly to a high‑performing team focused on strengthening financial operations and enabling sustainable, data‑driven improvements.

 - Review financial transactions, debtor payments, and third‑party disbursements to ensure actions are legal, proper, and compliant with federal requirements.
- Validate payees and payment allocations in case management and financial systems and ensure alignment across financial documentation.
- Execute monthly suspended disbursement reviews, identify root causes, and assist in remediation.
- Evaluate data captured in case management and financial systems to identify inconsistencies and propose corrective actions.
- Conduct trend analysis and continuous monitoring of high‑risk areas to detect process weaknesses, delays, or anomalies.
- Support internal control activities, including documentation reviews, identification of control gaps, and recommendations to strengthen financial operations.
- Develop dashboards, reporting templates, and analytics for senior leadership ​Track recommendations, corrective actions, and follow‑up testing to validate improvements.
- Participate in stakeholder engagements and provide clear, concise analytical insights to DOJ leadership.

 What You Will Need :

- Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, Economics, Business Administration or related business field
- FOUR (4) or more years of federal financial management, accounting, or auditing experience

 What Would Be Nice To Have : 
 - Professional certifications such as CPA, CGFM, PMP, or similar
- Thorough knowledge of GAAP, GAGAS, and federal accounting standards
- Master's degree in Accounting, Finance, Economics, or related field
- Experience with federal financial systems, ideally UFMS, CDCS, or Treasury systems
- Experience with business intelligence tools (e.g., Power BI, SQL)
- Federal project management experience
- Active or Ability to obtain Public Trust
- Experience reviewing records to confirm compliance with rules and regulations
- Prior knowledge or experience with government audits
