The Opportunity    
 Adobe is reinventing how it goes to market, and our team points the way! Integrated Go-to-Market (IGTM) advises Adobe's senior leaders on where to play and builds the path to get there, from launching agentic products to maturing product-led growth and opening new markets. 

 Our MBA intern owns a real project from day one, testing a hypothesis about how we sell next year or designing a new strategic capability. That work shapes how Adobe goes to market in FY28! Ready to take it on? 

 What You’ll Do    
 - Own one strategic project, from the first question to the final recommendation   
 - Build the fact base from pipeline, adoption and market data, plus conversations with sellers, marketers and partners   
 - Test hypotheses on how a new motion, launch or market expansion should work   
 - Design the plays, roles and metrics a new capability needs, and shape a pilot where possible   
 - Present options, tradeoffs and a clear recommendation to executives, then hand off a playbook for the team 

 What You Need to Succeed    
 - Currently enrolled in a full time MBA program and graduating between December 2027 – June 2028   
- 3+ years leading strategic or high-impact initiatives, ideally in consulting, SaaS, or go-to-market strategy and operations   
 - Sharp analytical skills, including forming hypotheses, working with imperfect data and turning findings into clear recommendations   
 - Excellent written and verbal communication, including briefing executives   
 - Track record of leading projects and building alignment across teams without formal authority   

 Nice-to-Have 
 - SaaS customer lifecycle and enterprise sales cycle knowledge   
 - Familiarity with digital marketing, digital experience or AI products   
 - Exposure to product-led growth, trial or other new go-to-market motions
