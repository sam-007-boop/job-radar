 What You Will Do : 
 Lead the development and execution of enterprise communications strategies and campaigns that align with NGA mission priorities and organizational transformation efforts. Serve as a trusted advisor to senior leadership, delivering executive-level messaging, talking points, and briefings that support decision-making, stakeholder alignment, and workforce engagement. Plan and manage high-visibility events, facilitate stakeholder discussions, and drive change management initiatives to ensure successful adoption of key programs across the Intelligence Community and international partner environments.

 What You Will Need : 
 - An ACTIVE and MAINTAINED TS/SCI Federal or DoD security clearance with a COUNTERINTELLIGENCE (CI) polygraph

- Bachelor's degree

- Minimum of TWELVE (12)+ years of experience in strategic communications, stakeholder engagement, or organizational change management supporting IC or DoD customer

- Demonstrated expertise advising senior executives, developing and executing communications strategies, and producing high-quality executive communications products

- Proven ability to lead complex initiatives, conduct stakeholder analysis, and align messaging across diverse internal and external audiences in fast-paced, mission-driven environments

 What Would Be Nice To Have : 
 - Experience supporting NGA International Affairs, GEOINT, or enterprise-level reorganizations, including development of communications strategies during large-scale transformation efforts

- Familiarity with multinational and interagency engagement, communications metrics analysis, and workforce engagement strategies

- Background leading high-visibility events (e.g., All Hands, senior leader engagements, or international forums) and experience with change management methodologies or related certifications.
