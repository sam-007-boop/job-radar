We're seeking a dynamic financial professional to become a critical partner in shaping Amazon's strategic financial landscape. In this pivotal position, you will play an instrumental role supporting processes that are essential to Amazon’s financial planning capabilities by partnering broadly across the Finance organization to translate complex numbers into compelling narratives and insights that guide executive leadership.

Key job responsibilities
- Develop comprehensive financial bridges, narratives and strategic documents for executive leadership, covering monthly, quarterly, and annual planning processes
- Develop daily and periodic company-wide revenue reports to communicate topline performance to Amazon’s executive leadership team
- Collaborate proactively with business finance teams using a customer-obsessed approach to ensure data accuracy and alignment of financial reporting
- Create guidance and workback plans to support key financial review meetings and processes 
- Utilize advanced financial systems to conduct deep-dive analyses and generate executive-level summaries

A day in the life
Your day will be a dynamic blend of financial analysis, strategic collaboration, and impactful storytelling. You'll dive deep into financial data, crafting narratives that illuminate Amazon's performance, working closely with cross-functional teams to deliver insights that matter.

We are the central hub of financial intelligence at Amazon, providing strategic support directly to Amazon’s highest leaders. Our team thrives on collaboration, innovation, and a shared commitment to driving business performance through data-driven insights.
