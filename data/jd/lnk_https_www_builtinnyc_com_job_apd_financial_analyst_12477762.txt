 Required Qualifications & Experience: 
 - Minimum 5 years of experience managing multiple priorities and projects simultaneously.
- Minimum 5 years of project management, training, and facilitation experience.
- Minimum 3 years of MDBT development, budgeting, or accounting experience.
- Minimum 3 years of experience writing, reviewing, or drafting financial narratives for APDs.
- Minimum 3 years of state government or public sector experience involving health and human services programs.
- Minimum 5 years of stakeholder engagement and collaboration experience.

 Key Responsibilities: 
 - Monitor APD-related project budgets, expenditures, and spending activities.
- Review and validate financial information for accuracy and compliance with CMS funding requirements.
- Develop, maintain, and update the Medicaid Detailed Budget Table (MDBT).
- Prepare and maintain APD budget tables, financial narratives, and supporting documentation.
- Gather, review, and validate cost estimates from project teams and stakeholders.
- Analyze financial data, identify potential budget issues, and support budget planning activities.
- Assist in preparing financial reports and documenting budget assumptions.
- Participate in budget planning, review meetings, and stakeholder discussions.
- Collaborate with Finance, Budget, and project teams to support financial oversight and reporting.
- Support the APO Finance Manager with financial analysis and budget management.
- Provide financial support for MES Certification activities, including ORR and CR.
