Amazon (incl. AWS data-center finance) — Senior Financial Analyst, Platform Experience Finance Team
https://www.amazon.jobs/en/jobs/10603410/senior-financial-analyst-platform-experience-finance-team

Amazon Web Services (AWS) is seeking a Sr. Financial Analyst to be a key member of the AWS Platform - Identity and Cloud Governance. This role will own end to end finance support including P&L management and planning for AWS Identity and Cloud Governance services. Additionally, this role will drive strategic new feature launches and process improvements across the organization. The successful candidate will be able to communicate effectively, manage multiple and time sensitive deliverables across a highly complex organization, along with managing risks and identifying opportunities. The individual will also possess strong business partnership skills and collaborate effectively with multiple key stakeholders. 

Key job responsibilities
- Take the lead in financial planning; build complex financial models and analytical tools to measure overall financial performance
- Owning the services end to end of revenue creation, and influence business decisions that drive pipeline creation and realization
- Identify opportunities for efficiency improvements including automation and coordination with upstream and downstream organizations
- Partner with finance, business, engineering and sales teams across AWS to standardize, streamline, and prioritize improvements, remove roadblocks, and increase the reliability and efficiency of reporting.

- Bachelor's degree in business, finance, accounting, tax, economics, or a related analytical field
- 3+ years of finance, accounting, or related analytical field experience
- 3+ years of creating process improvements with automation and analysis experience
- 3+ years of applying key financial performance indicators (KPIs) to analyses experience
- 3+ years of identifying, leading, and executing opportunities to improve, automate, standardize or simplify finance or business tools and processes experience

- MBA, or CPA
- 4+ years of participating in continuous improvement projects in your team to scale and improve controllership with measurable results experience
- Experience working with large-scale data mining and reporting tools (examples: SQL, MS Access, Essbase, Cognos) and other financial systems (examples: Oracle, SAP, Lawson, JD Edwards)
