 Overview 
 Rutgers University’s Office of Civil Rights serves as the University’s central resource for civil rights compliance, equal opportunity, and institutional accountability. The Office provides leadership and oversight for compliance with federal and state laws prohibiting discrimination, harassment, and retaliation, including Title IX, Title VI, Title VII , Section 504, the Americans with Disabilities Act ( ADA ), the New Jersey Law Against Discrimination ( NJLAD ), and related laws, regulations, and university policies. Working collaboratively with academic, administrative, and student-facing units, the Office develops policies, oversees complaint resolution processes, provides training and consultation, monitors legal developments, and advises University leadership on risk management and best practices. 

 Posting Summary 
 Rutgers, The State University of New Jersey is seeking an Associate Director of Investigations for the Office of Civil Rights.

Reporting to the Assistant Vice President for Civil Rights, the Associate Director of Investigations oversees the University’s investigative function for reports of discrimination, harassment, retaliation, sexual misconduct, and related civil rights concerns involving students, employees, and third parties. The Associate Director provides strategic leadership and oversight for investigators conducting prompt, equitable, and impartial investigations across Rutgers. The Associate Director establishes investigative standards, promotes consistency in complaint resolution, strengthens operational effectiveness, and helps foster confidence in the University’s civil rights processes.

 Among the key duties of the position are the following:

 1. Investigative Leadership and Case Resolution 

- Provide leadership and oversight for investigations involving alleged violations of University policies related to discrimination, harassment, retaliation, sexual misconduct, disability discrimination, and other civil rights matters.

- Ensure investigations are conducted promptly, equitably, impartially, and in compliance with applicable laws, regulations, and University policies.

- Review investigative strategies, evidence assessments, reports, and findings to ensure accuracy, consistency, and quality.

- As appropriate, conduct investigations of complex, sensitive, high-profile, or institutionally significant matters.

- Collaborate with University stakeholders to support implementation of investigative findings and recommendations.

- Monitor legal developments, regulatory guidance, and emerging best practices affecting civil rights investigations and complaint resolution.

 2. Strategic Leadership and Program Development 
- Develop, maintain and ensure implementation of investigative procedures, protocols, templates, and quality standards that support consistency across the University.

- Evaluate and improve investigative processes to enhance efficiency, effectiveness, fairness, and stakeholder experience.

- Identify trends, systemic concerns, and institutional risks arising from complaints and investigations and provide recommendations to University leadership.

- Support the development and revision of policies, procedures, and guidance related to civil rights compliance and complaint resolution.

- Serve as a member of the Office for Civil Rights leadership team and contribute to strategic planning and organizational initiatives.

 3. Supervision and Team Development 
- Lead, mentor, and develop investigative staff.

- Establish performance expectations and accountability measures that support high-quality work and professional growth.

- Provide coaching on investigative techniques, interviewing, evidence evaluation, report writing, and case management practices.

- Foster a collaborative, inclusive, and service-oriented team culture.

 4. Operations and Reporting 
- Manage investigative operations, including case assignment, workload distribution, and quality assurance.

- Develop and monitor performance metrics related to case volume, resolution timelines, outcomes, and operational effectiveness.

- Prepare reports, presentations, and analyses regarding investigative operations, trends, and compliance risks.

- Ensure accurate recordkeeping and documentation consistent with legal, regulatory, and institutional requirements.

- Support budget planning and resource allocation for investigative operations.

 Qualifications

 Minimum Education and Experience 

- Bachelor’s degree required.

- Seven years of progressively responsible experience conducting or overseeing investigations in civil rights, workplace, student conduct, compliance, employee relations, law enforcement, or a related field.

- Three years of supervisory experience leading investigators or other professional staff.

 Certifications/Licenses 

 Required Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities 

- Advanced degree (J.D., master’s degree, or related graduate degree).

- Demonstrated experience overseeing complex investigations involving discrimination, harassment, retaliation, sexual misconduct, disability-related matters, or comparable issues.

- Strong knowledge of investigative principles, interviewing techniques, evidence assessment, report writing, and case management.

- Knowledge of civil rights laws and regulatory frameworks applicable to higher education, including Title IX, Title VI, Title VII , ADA , and Section 504.

- Exceptional written communication skills, including preparation and review of investigative reports and findings.

- Excellent analytical and interpersonal skills.

- Demonstrated ability to exercise sound judgment, maintain confidentiality, and manage sensitive matters with professionalism and discretion.

- Strong organizational, project management, and leadership skills.

- Commitment to fairness, neutrality, integrity, and due process.

- Proficiency with electronic case management systems and Microsoft Office applications.

 Preferred Qualifications 

- Experience leading investigations within higher education or another large, decentralized organization.

- Experience supervising multidisciplinary investigative teams.

- Knowledge of federal and New Jersey laws governing discrimination, harassment, equal opportunity, disability accommodations, and sexual misconduct.

- Experience implementing or improving electronic case management systems.

- Experience developing investigative policies, procedures, and quality assurance standards.
