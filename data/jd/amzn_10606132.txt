Amazon (incl. AWS data-center finance) — Finance Manager, Amazon External Payments
https://www.amazon.jobs/en/jobs/10606132/finance-manager-amazon-external-payments

Amazon's external payment solutions facilitate transactions between Amazon customers and third-party merchants across the US, Japan, and Europe. Buyers encounter Amazon External Payments as a payment option when shopping on a merchant's site, store, or app, an agent, or through an Alexa skill, and they check out using the information already stored in their Amazon account. They choose Amazon External Payments because they trust Amazon, the experience is familiar, and checkout is effortless.

The Finance Manager will be a key member of the Amazon External Payments finance team, providing finance support to business partners and leadership worldwide. It is an excellent opportunity to help shape a fast-growing business within Amazon.

The right candidate is self-motivated and brings strong business judgment alongside considerable financial acumen. The role depends on close collaboration across sales, client account management, program management and GTM, integration, and marketing, so the ability to work effectively with cross-functional partners is essential. Success means balancing competing priorities, meeting tight deadlines, and delivering insights and recommendations grounded in data-driven analysis. The role also partners with accounting, legal, compliance, tax, VAT, audit, treasury, and transfer pricing to manage risk and ensure processes hold up to compliance requirements.

Key job responsibilities
- Lead large-scale change management initiatives and set strategic direction for how finance organizations function across Amazon
- Own the P&L, operational plan, and financial reporting, creating scalable processes and monitoring for accuracy
- Build financial models that drive business decisions, making judgment calls on complex challenges using your understanding of Amazon's and Amazon External Payments cost structures
- Generate insights from financial data, identify opportunities for greater efficiency, and communicate findings to the leadership team to drive business action
- Partner with Business Finance teams to allocate operating expenses to the appropriate businesses monthly and across planning cycles
- Partner with Accounting and Business Intelligence teams to define requirements for and prioritize projects that improve reporting accuracy and automation
- Support operations partners in areas including planning, productivity goals, defect tracking, metrics and performance measurement, and cost reduction projects
- Onboard and develop team members in financial reporting, create best practice materials, and promote knowledge sharing
- Provide ad hoc financial analysis and data support to the business team

A day in the life
This is an excellent opportunity to shape a pivot shifting and future growing business in an entrepreneurial environment. This person will dive deep to understand the intricacies of data and its sources, combine multiple sources of data to build a complete picture of business performance, partner with business teams to address reporting needs, and concisely communicate performance drivers to business partners and finance leadership. This role also has regular interaction with executives and various business units across Amazon and therefore requires strong interpersonal and communication skill. You will also help other teams across the geos (EU, US, JP) to gain access to vital insights and to collaborate.

- 5+ years of leading cross-functional initiatives that drive financial performance and strategy experience

- 5+ years of solving complex business challenges by delivering accurate and timely financial models, analysis, and recommendations that have a proven impact on business (e.g., financial savings, operational improvements, or customer benefits) experience
