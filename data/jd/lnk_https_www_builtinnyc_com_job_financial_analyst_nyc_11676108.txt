We are seeking a Financial Analyst to serve as a key analytical resource to our practice groups, providing financial intelligence that supports client service, strategic decision-making, and business development. The ideal candidate will bring 2–3 years of banking experience and a strong foundation in financial modeling, company and investment analysis, and market research. Working across practice groups and alongside Marketing and Business Development, this individual will turn complex financial and market data into actionable insights, client profiles, presentations, pitches, and other materials that help identify and advance opportunities. 

 KEY RESPONSIBILITIES: 

 - Financial analysis of target companies and clients, including stock ownership, stakeholder analysis, financial performance and ongoing financial analysis of client profiles.
- Review, analyze, and interpret data relating to investments made by financial institutions, including legal documents, that aids the practice groups in their decision-making processes.
- Perform, synthesize, and provide research and other due diligence and data on such investments and institutions and make informed recommendations to the practice group teams.
- Create pitches and presentation materials to be used by practice groups for prospective matters, client engagement and business development efforts.
- Conceptualize, write, produce, and distribute client services communications, including internal client newsletters, fact sheets, financial profiles and client news to be used internally and externally to secure or support client mandates.
- Manage data regarding external client development efforts, including tracking relationships and reporting activity to the client team leaders.
- Compile data from multiple systems, such as client and firm financials, relationship maps, 3rd party data providers, and internal/external communications to generate ad-hoc reports and dashboards.
- Monitor and track various industries, financial market dynamics, client news, and market trends and inform and educate the practice group teams
- Work with the Marketing and Business Development teams to create and/or update marketing materials for client teams and practice groups.
- Assist with other special projects, where financial acumen is needed.
- Knowledge of all key clients and priority targets.
- Perform various administrative tasks.

 QUALIFICATIONS: 

 - A minimum of 2-3 years banking experience (preferably M&A, Equity Capital Markets, Corporate Finance, Financial Restructuring, or Equity Research)
- Bachelor's degree or higher
- Ability to work in a fast-paced, team-based environment with minimal supervision
- Working knowledge of deal structuring and closing principals
- Strong communication and networking skills
- Impeccable research, quantitative and analytical skills, especially in explaining market events
- Ability to organize and track overlapping tasks and assignments, with frequent priority changes
- Strong financial modeling skills
- Ability to manage multiple projects while maintaining high-quality standards
- Ability to work quickly under short deadlines and in a team environment
- Demonstrates creativity, problem-solving, decision-making, and analytical skills
- Ability to develop credibility with internal and external clients
- Excellent attention to detail
- Ability to work independently, as well as a team, and deliver high-quality work product and service in a timely manner and with short deadlines
- Ability to take initiative with all aspects of daily and special assignments
- Ability to quickly adapt styles as the situation requires
- Proficiency with various software programs, including Microsoft Office and others
- Familiarity with one or more of the following: Bloomberg, S&P Capital IQ, FactSet, PitchBook, Thomson Reuters Eikon​
