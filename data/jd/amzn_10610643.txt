Amazon (incl. AWS data-center finance) — Senior Financial Analyst, Amazon Leo
https://www.amazon.jobs/en/jobs/10610643/senior-financial-analyst-amazon-leo

Amazon Leo is an initiative to launch a constellation of Low Earth Orbit satellites to provide broadband connectivity to unserved and underserved communities around the world. The Customer Terminal Supply Chain (CTSC) Finance team leads finance support for global fulfillment, inventory management, and rental tracking for Leo's customer terminals.

We are looking for a Senior Financial Analyst to join the CTSC Finance team. This role will primarily focus on the financial forecasting, assist in performance analysis, and assist in month-end close,.

The role will also contribute to reporting and analysis across the broader CTSC Finance team as business needs evolve. If you thrive on building from the ground up, navigating ambiguity, and driving financial rigor in a fast moving launch environment, this role offers the rare opportunity to shape the financial infrastructure of a global program from day one.

Key job responsibilities
As a Finance Analyst, you will work directly with business leaders to identify opportunities and provide key insights. Job responsibilities include:
• Compile and review financial results and operational plans, communicating results to leadership. 
• Drive the core financial processes for the business, including three-year strategic plan, annual operating plans, and monthly/quarterly reporting and forecasting.
• Lead key analytical projects that assist and influence the business.
• Lead automation efforts of critical processes.
• Develop business insights and KPI dashboards. 

This role requires a self-starter with a passion for developing a team, a keen attention to detail, and an ability to set clear priorities in a high velocity organization. The successful candidate will have shown success in influencing business owners and supporting decision making in rapidly evolving environments. 

A day in the life
You might start by checking overnight data and dashboards for anything unexpected. Next you update the CT inventory model with the latest production and demand inputs. Then you meet with Operations to understand a forecast change, and you finish the day writing variance commentary for a leadership review.

About the team
The Customer Terminal Supply Chain (CTSC) Finance team is the controller for fulfillment, inventory, and rental costs for Leo's customer terminals, supporting both the P&L and balance sheet. We lead Leo financial reporting, ensure finance readiness for every new country launch, drive cost optimization, and support strategic initiatives.

The team covers three pillars: global fulfillment (forward and reverse logistics, warehouse costs), inventory management (valuation, adjustments, transfers), and rentals. You'll partner closely with Devices Finance, Leo Accounting, Leo FP&A, Leo BizOps, and the Leo Inventory and Fulfillment Technology business team.
