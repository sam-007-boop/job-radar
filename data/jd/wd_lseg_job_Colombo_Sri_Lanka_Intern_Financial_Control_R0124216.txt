Position Summary 
 This internship offers a hands-on opportunity to explore financial reporting & control within a global organization. The Statutory Reporting team supports the preparation of financial statements, audit coordination, and process improvement across multiple legal entities. Interns are encouraged to contribute to meaningful work while developing skills in accounting, analysis, and collaboration. 

 The role is designed to support learning and growth. Whether pursuing studies in accounting, finance, or a related field, this experience provides exposure to real-world financial operations and the chance to work alongside experienced professionals. 

 Key Responsibilities 

 Interns in this role may be involved in: 

 - Supporting the preparation of statutory financial statements and related documentation. 
- Assisting with audit coordination and gathering required materials. 
- Participating in reconciliation activities and processing adjustments. 
- Contributing to process documentation and standardization efforts. 
- Learning how financial transactions influence reporting and disclosures. 

 Tasks are assigned with guidance and mentorship, allowing space to learn and contribute meaningfully. 

 Preferred Skills and Experience 

 This opportunity is open to students or recent graduates with a curiosity about finance, accounting, or business currently pursuing professional accountancy qualifications. Curiosity, attention to detail, and a collaborative mindset are valued. Familiarity with Excel and a willingness to explore financial systems are helpful. 

 The team welcomes individuals who: 

 - Are open to learning and adapting in a dynamic environment. 
- Communicate clearly and work well with others. 
- Approach tasks with care and a problem-solving attitude. 
- Appreciate diverse perspectives and enjoy working in a team setting. 

 What We Offer 

 Interns are supported with resources and mentorship to make the most of their experience. Benefits include: 

 - Mentorship and training from experienced team members. 
- Exposure to global finance operations and reporting practices. 
- Opportunities to build professional skills in accounting and audit. 
- Flexible working arrangements to support academic commitments. 
- Inclusive team culture that values contributions from all backgrounds
