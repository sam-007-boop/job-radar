Amazon (incl. AWS data-center finance) — Senior Finance Analyst, Devices FP&A, Opex Reporting and Innovation team
https://www.amazon.jobs/en/jobs/10598145/senior-finance-analyst-devices-fp-a-opex-reporting-and-innovation-team

Join Amazon's Devices & Services Financial Planning & Analysis team and shape the financial strategy behind innovative products like Alexa, Ring, FireTV, and Kindle. We are seeking a Senior Financial Analyst to lead Variable and Non HC Fixed Opex across the Devices organization.

We seek a strategic thinker who can manage a broad portfolio of spend categories, drive AI-powered innovation in financial processes, and serve as a trusted business partner to senior stakeholders.

Key job responsibilities
Own end-to-end financial planning, forecasting, and variance reporting for Variable Opex (including Device Managed Marketing and Partner Deals), Direct Variable Opex, and Non-HC Engineering spend.

Work across a team of finance professionals while championing AI adoption to automate and improve core workflows.

Serve as a trusted business partner to VP and Director-level stakeholders, translating complex financial data into strategic recommendations that inform executive reviews and drive decision-making.

A day in the life
As a Senior Financial Analyst on our Opex Financial Planning & Analysis team, you'll own the financial strategy behind some of our largest and most complex cost pools. Your day might include deep-diving into Variable Opex variances to surface insights for leadership, partnering with engineering and marketing teams to sharpen Non-HC spend forecasts, or presenting cost optimization recommendations in executive reviews that shape how we invest across Devices & Services.

You'll explore how AI tools can automate reporting workflows, accelerate variance analysis, and unlock deeper cost insights that manual processes miss. You'll also manage and develop a team of finance professionals, ensuring high-quality deliverables across MBR/QBR cycles while keeping cross-functional partners aligned on financial priorities.

Your work directly impacts how leadership understands the cost structure, efficiency levers, and investment trade-offs within our portfolio.

About the team
We are a collaborative Financial Planning & Analysis team dedicated to supporting the strategic growth of Amazon's Devices & Services organization. Our mission is to provide clear, data-driven financial insights that enable informed decision-making across our diverse product lines.

We work in a fast-paced, global environment where we partner closely with product teams, operations, and executive leadership to translate financial data into actionable business strategies. We're committed to modernizing our processes through technology and innovation, and we're excited to welcome someone who can help us leverage new tools and methodologies to enhance our impact.

Joining our team means becoming part of a group that values analytical rigor, clear communication, and a shared commitment to supporting Amazon's continued innovation in consumer devices and services.
