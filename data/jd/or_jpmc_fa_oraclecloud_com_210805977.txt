If you’re excited by turning data into insights and using analytics to inform real business decisions, we have a role for you. Global Finance & Business Management (GFBM) is dedicated to continually improving our business—strategically, financially, and operationally—by combining financial expertise with data‑driven analysis and emerging technologies. We’re looking for highly motivated individuals with a passion for analysis, innovation, and problem‑solving who are eager to help drive strategic business decisions.

 As a Full-Time Analyst in the Global Finance & Business Management Analyst Program, you’ll collaborate with top‑tier professionals to influence and shape key decisions, initiatives, and outcomes that support businesses across the firm. You’ll have opportunities to make meaningful contributions through hands‑on projects, applying analytical thinking, structured problem‑solving, and data‑informed insights in a dynamic team environment.  Working here means joining a collaborative and supportive culture. We provide the training, mentorship, and exposure you need to succeed, including access to senior leaders, peer mentorship, and projects designed to engage your full skill set—spanning financial analysis, data interpretation, and critical thinking.

 This two-year rotational program delivers in-depth industry training, peer mentorship and hands-on experience. You’ll be placed as a member on one of our finance teams where you’ll work within one of the following functional groups: Business Management, Controller, Financial Analysis, and Project Management.  You’ll leverage data, analytics, and technology‑enabled tools to support business objectives, improve processes, and provide insights that inform decision‑making.  In addition to ongoing technical and professional skill development, we offer dedicated CPA certification support and licensure-qualifying placement. On completion of the program, we will work with you to identify your next role.

 The responsibilities below align to each of our functional groups – Business Management, Controller, Financial Analysis, and Project Management

 Job responsibilities 

 Business Management 
 - Act as a trusted advisor to business and group heads by identifying, escalating, and mitigating business risks
- Optimize business performance by driving key initiatives supported by financial and data‑driven insights
- Leverage reporting, metrics, and analytics to support strategic planning and execution

 Controller 
 - Ensure the integrity and accuracy of financial statements and controls across the transaction lifecycle
- Perform critical reporting and controls functions, including legal entity, financial and regulatory reporting, and product valuation
- Partner with stakeholders to enhance controls, transparency, and data quality

 Financial Analysis 
 - Create meaningful financial analysis, reporting, and insights to support business strategy
- Coordinate with partners across the firm on budgeting, forecasting, and performance management
- Use data and analytics to identify trends, risks, and opportunities impacting the business

 Project Management 
 - Support initiatives that drive cross‑line‑of‑business or firmwide priorities, including new regulations, standards, or policies and contribute to large‑scale programs consisting of multiple projects and work streams
- Track progress, analyze outcomes, and support data‑informed decision‑making across project lifecycles

 Required qualifications, capabilities, and skills 
 - Pursuing a B.A., B.S., or 5th year M.A. or M.S., with an expected graduation date from  December 2026 through June 2027 
- Ability to thrive in a fast‑paced, collaborative, and dynamic environment
- Strong leadership, communication, interpersonal, and problem‑solving skills
- A well‑rounded academic background with an interest in financial management, analytics, and data
- Digital-first mindset; data driven critical thinking skills; curiosity, adaptability and willingness to learn new tools/skills/technologies; proficiency in Excel and PowerPoint
- Relevant internship experience and demonstrated leadership in academic or community organizations

 Preferred qualifications, capabilities, and skills 
 - Minimum cumulative GPA of 3.2 on a 4.0 scale
- Working knowledge of data analytics, visualization tools, programming language tools, etc. (such as Tableau, Alteryx, Python, etc.) 
- Intermediate Excel Skills (Vlookup, Pivot Tables, Index Match)
