Amazon (incl. AWS data-center finance) — Senior Financial Analyst, RME Finance, WW Ops
https://www.amazon.jobs/en/jobs/10598429/senior-financial-analyst-rme-finance-ww-ops

RME Finance Analysts own cost across the network. They provide FP&A support for North America Field RME teams, covering MEC, controllership, and operational planning cycles. Success in this role requires intellectual curiosity, comfort with ambiguity, and a genuine drive to improve how work gets done.

Key job responsibilities
- Run Month End Close for Personnel and Parts, Repairs, and Maintenance (PRM) accounts. You are accountable for accuracy, completeness, and controllership compliance from start to finish.

- Own the financial planning cycles, including forecast modeling, bridging, and variance analysis. You work with limited guidance and are expected to identify cost drivers and bring clear findings to senior managers.

- Deliver data-driven narratives that shape how leaders make decisions. Push back on business partner plans when the numbers tell a different story.

- Manage controllership for headcount and Coupa. When you see a gap, you fix it. Hold yourself and the process to a high standard.

- Build relationships across Field and Central RME, Finance, and Operations. Your credibility comes from the quality of your work and trust built over time.

- Find ways to make the work better. Lead process improvement projects, document what you learn, and share across the team.

- Use AI and automation to handle routine work. The goal is to spend time on work that actually matters.

- Use QuickSight, Cognos, and other internal systems to surface insights from financial and productivity data.

- Help grow the people around you—contribute to onboarding, share knowledge, and be a resource for junior teammates.

A day in the life
The FP&A work is cyclical. MEC runs Days 1-5 each month, with five budget cycles per year. That core work is non-negotiable. But about 15% of your time is spent on harder, less-defined problems—that's where the real opportunity is.

RME is one of Amazon's fastest-growing organizations. You'll be in the middle of the AI, robotics, and automation buildout across Stores sites. You'll partner with network leaders, influence how cost is managed in the field, and continuously look for ways to automate what doesn't need a human.

No two days look the same.

About the team
The team supports Field RME across North America Consumer Fulfillment (NACF), Amazon Transportation Services (ATS), and Global Delivery Services (GDS), along with the Worldwide (WW) RME Central Team. 

- Bachelor's degree in finance, accounting or related field
- 3+ years of finance or a related analytical field experience
- 3+ years of contributing to cross-functional initiatives that drive financial performance and strategy experience
- 3 years of experience dissecting financial data and identifying patterns that support business strategy

- MBA, or CPA
- Experience working with large-scale data mining and reporting tools (examples: SQL, MS Access, Essbase, Cognos) and other financial systems (examples: Oracle, SAP, Lawson, JD Edwards)
- 2+ years of participating in continuous improvement projects in your team to scale and improve controllership with measurable results experience
- Experience working alongside Operations or field-based teams in a fast-moving, high-volume environment
