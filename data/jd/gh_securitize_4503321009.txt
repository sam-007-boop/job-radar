 Website  |  X/Twitter  |  LinkedIn About the role 
 This role sits on the FP&A team and spends about 75% of its time on FP&A and about 25% supporting Investor Relations. You will build the models, automations and analysis behind forecasts, acquisitions and new business decisions, and support the Head of IR with daily market research and executive materials. You will report to the VP of FP&A. 
 FP&A Responsibilities 

 Own the forecast and performance tracking for acquired businesses, including integration into our FP&A system 
 Build financial models for acquisitions, new products, pricing and partnerships 
 Prepare revenue bridges, unit economics and KPI analysis for leadership; build AI-enabled automations and dashboards 
 Support the quarterly and annual forecast process 

 IR Responsibilities 

 Monitor sell-side research daily; summarize new notes and flag errors or notable items 
 Prepare the daily end-of-day recap for senior management (stock performance, research, market data) and a weekly recap 
 Build and maintain executive conference prep packets: investor research, holdings and AUM, meeting history, bios and likely questions 
 Keep the IR CRM current after investor meetings and conferences 
 Support earnings, including collecting research notes and analyst models 

 Qualifications 

 2-4 years of experience in FP&A, financial analysis, investment banking or transaction advisory 
 Strong financial modeling skills (3-statement, driver-based forecasting); exposure to deal or valuation models 
 Clear, concise written communication and polished PowerPoint materials for executive audiences; comfortable researching investors from public sources (13F, Form ADV, filings) 
 Advanced Excel and hands-on experience with an FP&A planning tool (e.g., Datarails, Adaptive, Anaplan, Planful, Cube, Pigment) and an ERP; AI-inclined, with experience building automations or dashboards 
 Sound judgment with confidential and material non-public information 
 Working knowledge of financial markets and interest in digital assets; able to judge which research and market news matters. Prior IR experience not required 

 Nice to have 

 Experience in fintech, digital assets, asset management or capital markets 
 Datarails and NetSuite specifically, including multi-entity, multi-currency environments 
 Public company experience
