About the Team 
 The Strategy & Operations Customer Experience team owns the discovery experience for how users explore DoorDash. Our mission is to help DoorDash consumers connect with their communities and discover “what’s good” in their city. Our Occasions pod sits within this team and our mission is to help DoorDash become the #1 destination to buy anything you need around seasonal and celebratory moments. Our team designs the strategy and executes these initiatives, while making Consumers feel that DoorDash is personalized just for them. We bring together a team across product, user research, marketing, analytics and operations to collaborate on platform and content projects that bring us closer to our vision.  
 About the Role 
 We’re looking for a detail oriented, highly-motivated individual to lead the execution of our occasions, owning the strategy development and implementation needed to launch these campaigns. As our execution lead, you will work closely with multiple XFN partners and stakeholders, guiding teams on best practices, workback schedules, timelines and campaign launching. You will become an expert in all internal tooling used for campaign execution, craft the roadmap for the rollout of our occasions, help teams setup, test and launch initiatives, identify areas where we can continue to streamline the execution process, and so much more. You will report into a Manager on our Occasions team.  
 You’re excited about this opportunity because you will… 

 Own the preparation and implementation of the execution plan for key initiatives and occasions: creating campaigns when needed, setting up experiments, conducting testing sessions and implementing in-app content 
 Collaborate across internal marketing and operations teams: maintaining documentation, communicating clearly about project status, and adhering to deadlines 
 Thoroughly review and test campaigns to ensure it meets design standards and merchandising guidelines, keeping the user experience in mind 
 Build documentation, setup guides , and conduct trainings so that operators can execute effectively 
 Become an expert on consumer-first merchandising principles and internal tooling to be able to lead cross-functional processes and forums  

 We’re excited about you because… 

 You have 3+ years experience working in strategy and operations, project management, growth strategy, or banking  
 You have a track record of owning key initiatives, hitting goals, and succeeding in a cross-functional team environment 
 You excel both as a generalist and specialist who can build the overarching strategy and deep dive  into specific issues and processes to drive continuous improvement. 
 You have a bias for action and track record of leading initiatives  
 You bring structure, organization, and focus to your work and work with cross-functional teams, including product, marketing, ads and promos, design and other S&O functions 
 You are highly organized and can efficiently manage multiple projects simultaneously 
 You have expert communication skills, both written & verbal 
 You are an optimistic & collaborative problem solver 
 You are meticulous, with a strong attention to detail 
 You are comfortable working with data in excel/google sheets and willing to learn basic SQL
