About the Role: 

 Grade Level (for internal use): 
13 Company Name: Standard & Poor's Financial Services LLC
 Job Title: Credit Analyst
 Location: 55 Water Street, New York, NY 10041 (Requires regular in-office attendance at New York, NY office. On other days, telecommuting permitted within normal commuting distance)

 Job Duties: Standard & Poor's Financial Services LLC seeks a Credit Analyst who will act as the primary analyst for complex entities, making recommendations to the rating committee and preparing materials for ratings publication. Serve as an active, vocal voting member of rating committees. Develop forecasts and recommend ratings for issuers and debt issues consistent with existing S&P criteria. Learn and apply S&P internal systems and effectively communicate S&P’s methodology and procedures to clients. Aid in identifying market trends. Write analytical reports, contribute to thought leadership pieces, and synthesize analysis into well-reasoned and well-supported rating recommendations. Work effectively with markets and jurisdictions outside of primary focus in a global environment. Ensure a risk aware culture to increase our ability to efficiently and effectively serve clients. Cover of a diverse portfolio of credits. Train other team members to help them resolve difficult day-to-day analytical issues and problems.

 Job Requirements: Master's degree in Business Administration, Business Analytics, Finance, or related financial field, plus five (5) years of experience as a Credit Analyst, Financial Analyst, or related occupation. Five (5) years of experience must include: Financial modeling and credit analysis; Understanding of accounting, financial reporting standards, and corporate budgeting; Developing financial models and forecasts relating to earning, cashflows, and liquidity to assess credit quality of companies; Utilizing company and industry knowledge and financial analysis to make well-reasoned and well-supported rating recommendations; Writing analytical reports to convey credit opinion to market participants; Industry knowledge of capital markets and the broader financial services.

 Alternatively, employer will accept Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Business Analytics, Finance, or related financial field and Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) certification plus five (5) years of experience as a Credit Analyst, Financial Analyst, or related occupation. Five (5) years of experience must include: Financial modeling and credit analysis; Understanding of accounting, financial reporting standards, and corporate budgeting; Developing financial models and forecasts relating to earning, cashflows, and liquidity to assess credit quality of companies; Utilizing company and industry knowledge and financial analysis to make well-reasoned and well-supported rating recommendations; Writing analytical reports to convey credit opinion to market participants; Industry knowledge of capital markets and the broader financial services.

 Any suitable combination of education, training, or experience is acceptable.

 About S&P Global Ratings 
At S&P Global Ratings, our analyst-driven credit ratings, research, and sustainable finance opinions provide critical insights that are essential to translating complexity into clarity so market participants can uncover opportunities and make decisions with conviction. By bringing transparency to the market through high-quality independent opinions on creditworthiness, we enable growth across a wide variety of organizations, including businesses, governments, and institutions.
 S&P Global Ratings is a division of S&P Global (NYSE: SPGI).  S&P Global is the world’s foremost provider of credit ratings, benchmarks, analytics and workflow solutions in the global capital, commodity and automotive markets.
