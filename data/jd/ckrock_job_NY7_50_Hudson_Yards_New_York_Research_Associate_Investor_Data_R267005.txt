About this role 

 About this role 

 Preqin, a part of BlackRock, provides private markets data and technology that complement Aladdin’s whole-portfolio solutions.
 The Research Associate combines alternatives research, investor-data stewardship, outreach and AI automation to deliver reliable intelligence across a growing universe of firms. As a Data Custodian operating under the designated Data Steward, the Associate is responsible for maintaining the coverage, quality and integrity of investor data across an assigned North American universe.

 Key responsibilities 

 - Research investors active in the alternative assets markets using approved public and proprietary sources; maintain investor profiles and interpret developments for clients.
- Help build, test and refine approved AI agents, agentic workflows and web-monitoring tools with technology and research colleagues; validate outputs with human oversight.
- Build investor relationships through targeted, approved outreach to verify information and capture intelligence on allocations, mandates and organizational changes.
- Review and correct data, reconcile conflicting evidence and resolve exceptions under Data Steward standards; document decisions and escalate unresolved risks.
- Maintain source provenance and audit-ready records; follow governance, privacy, security and AI-risk standards using approved tools and permitted data.
- Collaborate across research, data, product, engineering and client teams to improve workflows, share practices and measure coverage, accuracy, cycle time and rework.

 What you’ll bring to us 

 - Bachelor’s degree or equivalent professional experience.
- 4+ years in alternatives, private markets or institutional-investor research, ideally at an LP, GP, private-markets data provider or alternatives-focused firm.
- Strong knowledge of the alternative investment market.
- Sound analytical judgment, data literacy and commitment to governance; able to reconcile evidence, manage competing priorities and document defensible decisions.
- Strong technical literacy with an interest in learning and applying new tools, technologies, and automation techniques to improve research quality, efficiency, and scale.
- Strong communication and cross-functional collaboration skills, with curiosity and learning agility.

 Preferred experience 

 - Experience with alternative-assets datasets, workflow or data-quality systems, structured data tools or business intelligence, and demonstrable AI/automation improvements with human oversight.
