 What We Stand For and Where You’ll Come In As The Farmer’s Dog evolves into a multi-channel, multi-product, and multi-entity business, you will help develop and scale the planning processes, models, and reporting that support that complexity. You will support company-wide planning and forecasting while independently owning financial planning and performance management for designated product, channel, or business-level P&Ls. You will bring an owner’s mindset to those areas: accountable for the outcomes, and working with the teams that drive them. You will also join a team that uses AI to work faster and cover more ground, and we want you to help us do more of it.
 The Senior Finance Manager is accountable for financial planning, forecasting, and performance across designated areas of the business, and partners with business leaders as the company grows. This is an individual contributor role reporting to the Director of Corporate Finance. 
 One Team: We don’t think of ourselves as “Acquisition Marketers”, “Engineers”, “Data Analysts”, or “Product Managers”. Beyond denoting skill sets and areas of expertise, we don’t think departments matter. We’d rather align ourselves to the goals we’re working to achieve and make sure we have necessary subject matter expertise to drive meaningful impact. We strive to orient ourselves around customer problems TOGETHER – getting the right people, with the right context, in the right rooms/Zooms to solve problems holistically.
 We are skeptical about everything and precious about nothing:   Ideas can and should come from anywhere, and we aren’t tied to our own. We proactively source input. We talk to our customers and leads regularly and are quick to change course if we know there’s a better or more impactful way to solve problems.
 We consider the customer journey in all of our decisions:   We know that no interaction exists in a silo and therefore understand how important every single one is.  We ensure our strategy sets prospective and new customers up for success and drives long-term retention.  We answer questions and address problems early and proactively.  We understand the value of different channels, initiatives, and messages and know how to articulate impact and advocate for prioritization holistically.
 We Execute For Impact: We don’t subscribe to “best practices” or “industry KPIs”. We’re uninterested in how we compare to “benchmarks”; instead we orient ourselves around being the best we can possibly be. Similarly, we don’t subscribe to rigid or classical expectations of roles – i.e. acquisition is hyper-focused on improving customer retention and experience.
 We Are Focused and Work Without Assumption : We are not beholden to ideas. We have goals and believe everything beyond that is a series of hypotheses to validate. To that end, we seek to work in sequence and not in parallel. We constantly ask ourselves, “what’s the most important hypothesis I should be working on right now? How do I confirm or reject that hypothesis as fast as possible?”. We rarely have timelines/deadlines and are constantly taking in new information and adjusting our priorities accordingly. We don’t expect to be perfect the first time.
 How You'll Make An Impact - Own financial planning, forecasting, and performance management for designated product, channel, or business-level P&Ls: build forecasts, analyze variances, find the operational and financial drivers of revenue, costs, and margin, and influence the levers that drive results.
- Support company-wide budgeting, forecasting, and long-range planning, working with the Director of Corporate Finance to consolidate projections, test assumptions, and surface risks and opportunities.
- Partner with business leaders and cross-functional teams, bringing financial insight, analytical support, and constructive challenge to decisions and trade-offs.
- Build and maintain financial models for forecasting, scenario planning, business cases, and resource allocation, turning operational assumptions into projections and recommendations.
- Prepare monthly and quarterly reporting and business reviews for your areas, with clear visibility into trends, risks, and opportunities.
- Evaluate the financial case for initiatives and investments, quantifying trade-offs and tracking expected against actual outcomes.
- Support executive and Board materials, and ad-hoc strategic analyses.
- Help strengthen and standardize planning and reporting processes as the business grows across products, channels, and entities, working with Data, Analytics & Insights, IT, and other teams.
- Use AI and automation to augment your own work, taking on repetitive analysis and reporting so you can spend more time on insight and partnership.
 We're Excited About You Because - You have 7+ years of experience in FP&A, strategic finance, or finance business partnering, ideally within a high-growth consumer, CPG, or food/beverage company.
- You have experience partnering with Product teams (digital and physical) and understand the financial dynamics of bringing new products from concept to market (preferred).
- You're a strong model builder who thrives in ambiguity – comfortable creating financial frameworks from scratch when there's no template to follow, and exercising independent judgment on methodology and assumptions.
- You bring a strategic mindset paired with attention to detail – you can step back to evaluate portfolio-level trade-offs while also diving deep into the cost structure of an individual product.
- You're a confident communicator who can distill complex financial analysis into clear, actionable recommendations for senior leadership and cross-functional partners.
- You're proactive and opinionated in a productive way – you don't wait to be asked, you surface risks early, challenge ideas constructively, and advocate for the right outcome for TFD.
- You have a strong grasp of operations and supply chain dynamics and understand how formulation, procurement, and manufacturing decisions flow through to the P&L.
- You're a strong executor who can manage multiple concurrent projects with competing timelines, balancing thoroughness with speed.
- You build strong cross-functional relationships and can effectively facilitate alignment across groups with different priorities and perspectives.
- You’re proficient in Excel/Google Sheets with strong financial modeling skills, and have a mindset of continuously improving processes and proactively identifying opportunities to automate and increase efficiency. Familiarity with Omni and AI Tooling is preferred.
- You love dogs

 What you need to know about the NYC Tech Scene

 As the undisputed financial capital of the world, New York City is an epicenter of startup funding activity. The city has a thriving fintech scene and is a major player in verticals ranging from AI to biotech, cybersecurity and digital media. It also has universities like NYU, Columbia and Cornell Tech attracting students and researchers from across the globe, providing the ecosystem with a constant influx of world-class talent. And its East Coast location and three international airports make it a perfect spot for European companies establishing a foothold in the United States.

 Key Facts About NYC Tech

- Major Tech Employers: Capgemini, Bloomberg, IBM, Spotify
- Key Industries: Artificial intelligence , Fintech 
- Funding Landscape: $25.5 billion in venture capital funding in 2024 (Pitchbook)
