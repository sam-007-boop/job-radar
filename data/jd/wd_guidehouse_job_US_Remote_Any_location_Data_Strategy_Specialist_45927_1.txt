 What You Will Do : 

 - Assess current data, processes, tools, and information flows supporting staff planning, budgeting, forecasting, regulatory examination workflows, and technology resource planning to identify deficiencies and improvement opportunities.
- Define practical data strategies and requirements that support redesigned business processes and technology solutions and translate organizational priorities into implementable improvements.
- Map current-state and future-state data flows and identify data gaps, dependencies, quality issues, integration challenges, and remediation requirements.
- Support data remediation across enterprise platforms and workflows, including troubleshooting data-related issues that prevent processes or technology solutions from operating effectively.
- Support planning-model and capacity-planning improvements for budgeting, forecasting, workforce and staff planning, and associated system tools.
- Develop business, functional, and data requirements supporting process redesign and system improvements and work with technology teams to translate those requirements into executable solutions.
- Support regulatory examination management improvements, including data and information flows related to examination tracking, issue identification and resolution, document production, and response coordination.
- Establish data-driven measures and evidence to support performance monitoring, corrective action tracking, implementation validation, and audit-ready documentation.
- Partner across business, risk, compliance, operations, and technology teams to ensure improvements support organizational priorities, regulatory commitments, and sustainable operations. 

 What You Will Need : 

 - Must be able to OBTAIN and MAINTAIN a Federal or DoD "PUBLIC TRUST"; candidates must obtain approved adjudication of their PUBLIC TRUST prior to onboarding with Guidehouse. Candidates with an ACTIVE PUBLIC TRUST or SUITABILITY are preferred.
- US Citizenship is contractually required.
- Minimum of SIX (6+) years of professional experience
- Experience using data to support business process improvement, planning, forecasting, operations, technology implementation, or transformation is highly preferred.
- Understanding of data risk, controls, privacy, cybersecurity, and regulatory requirements is highly preferred.

 What Would Be Nice To Have : 

 - Bachelor's degree from an accredited university.
- Banking, financial services, payments, or financial regulatory experience.
- Experience with workforce/staff planning, forecasting, capacity planning, budgeting, or planning-model improvement.
- Experience with regulatory examination or audit-management workflows, including examination tracking, issue identification and resolution, document production, or response coordination.
- Experience supporting implementation or remediation associated with regulatory findings, audit findings, or exam-driven corrective actions.
- Familiarity with enterprise data and workflow platforms such as Snowflake, ServiceNow, and SharePoint .
- Experience working across business, technology, risk, compliance, and operations in highly regulated environments.
- Strong analytical, cross-functional facilitation, executive communication, documentation, and problem-solving skills.
- Demonstrated ability to take improvements from assessment through implementation and completion.
- Strong experience in data strategy, data analysis, data requirements, data flows, interfaces, integrations, dependencies, and remediation.
- Experience conducting current-state assessments, gap analyses, and future-state design and translating findings into actionable business and technology improvements.
- Experience developing business, functional, and data requirements and supporting solutions through implementation.
- Experience with business process improvement, workflow redesign, performance measurement, and evidence-based assessment.
- Strong experience in data strategy, data analysis, data requirements, data flows, interfaces, integrations, dependencies, and remediation.
