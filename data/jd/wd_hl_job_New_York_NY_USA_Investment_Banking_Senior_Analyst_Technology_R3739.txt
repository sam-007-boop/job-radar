 Overview 
Houlihan Lokey, Inc. (NYSE:HLI) is a leading global investment bank recognized for delivering independent strategic and financial advice to corporations, financial sponsors, and governments. With uniquely deep industry expertise, broad international reach, and a partnership approach rooted in trust, the firm provides innovative, integrated solutions across mergers and acquisitions, capital solutions, financial restructuring, and financial and valuation advisory. Our unmatched transaction volumes provide differentiated, data-driven perspectives that help our clients achieve their most critical goals. To learn more about Houlihan Lokey, please visit HL.com.

 Corporate Finance 
Houlihan Lokey has extensive expertise in mergers, acquisitions, divestitures, activist shareholder and takeover defense, and other related advisory services for a broad range of U.S. and international clients. Our experience in M&A has earned us consistent recognition throughout the industry. In 2025, LSEG ranked our firm the No. 1 investment bank for all global M&A transactions.

 Technology Group 
Our Technology Group is a global team and a recognized leader in providing high-quality, relationship-based advice, guiding clients through M&A and capital raise transactions. 
The environment at Houlihan Lokey is both collegial and entrepreneurial. Teamwork is essential to the firm’s success, and creativity and new ideas are encouraged. 
Houlihan Lokey’s Technology Group is the No. 1 technology advisor globally based on number of transactions. 

 Job Description 
As a Senior Analyst, you will:
 - At least 2 full years of investment banking experience (class of '24, technology coverage preferred)
- Work with senior professionals covering Software and Digital Media clients
- Learn about a range of end-markets: supply chain software, proptech, education technology, HCM software, semiconductors, sports tech, etc.
- Support teams working on M&A as well as equity / debt capital raises
- Research economic and industry-specific developments
- Analyze financial statements and other investment attributes of publicly traded as well as private companies
- Help prepare marketing materials and other presentations
- Qualifications 
- The ideal candidate will have the following qualifications:
- Strong academic track record, with a background in finance, accounting, business or economics
- Advanced knowledge of Excel and PowerPoint
- A high degree of intellectual curiosity and a desire to never stop learning
- Keen attention to detail and a high level of accuracy in work product
- Ability to manage rapid timelines and proactively manage any potential conflicts
- Ability to exercise sound judgment, make informed decisions, and act independently
- Comfort presenting to and engaging with senior colleagues, clients, and counterparties
- Commitment to the firm and our clients
