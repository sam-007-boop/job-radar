Newsweek reaches 100 million people each month with thought-provoking news, opinion, images, graphics, and video delivered across a dozen print and digital platforms. Headquartered in New York City, Newsweek also publishes international editions in EMEA and Asia.  

 Newsweek is looking for a Sr. Financial Analyst of FP&A to lead forecasting and analytics across all business lines of the company. In this high-impact role, you'll own topline forecast models—traffic, impressions, revenue, bookings, subscribers, and deliver critical insights that guide decision-making and performance across the businesses, as well as consolidated three statement models. You will be the go-to resource for topline actuals analysis and forward-looking projections in your areas of ownership.
 KEY RESPONSIBILITIES 
 - Lead the monthly/quarterly revenue forecasting & reporting processes, develop executive and board presentations, and build forward-looking business trends & insights
- Drive the annual planning cycle, working cross-functionally to align on strategic initiatives, growth levers, and revenue drivers 
- Act as a trusted finance partner to business leaders and teams; deliver data-driven recommendations and insights that drive performance and decision-making
- Build scalable forecasting models to improve accuracy and enable scenario planning
- Identify and implement operational efficiencies to streamline reporting and FP&A support
 QUALIFICATIONS 
 - 2-5 years of experience in FP&A, with significant focus on topline forecasting
- Proven experience delivering financial insights to executives and senior-level cross-functional stakeholders
- Exceptional financial modeling skills and the ability to synthesize data into clear narratives ·
- Strong analytical, communication, and presentation skills
- Experience working at a high-growth internet, digital media or consumer tech company
 DESIRABLES 
 - 3 statement modeling
- Experience with ERP systems and BI tools

 Compensation 
Base salary range: $80,000 - $100,000 USD
