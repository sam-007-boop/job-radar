About this role 

 Company: BlackRock Financial Management, Inc.
 Job Title: Associate, Technology Product Management
 Location: 50 Hudson Yards, New York, NY 10001

 Job Duties: Drive the overall product strategy and strategic initiatives to evolve the platform in response to client needs to increase revenue opportunity for the firm. Partner with the investment team, users, technology leaders, and other stakeholders to shape the vision for the next version of the platform and define the overall deployment strategy. Innovate, conceptualize, design, and pilot new capabilities available. Develop, prioritize, and maintain road maps, provide quarterly updates on deliverables, and create content for management communications. Prepare requirements documentation for use by software developers. Collaborate in the development or testing of new analytical software to ensure compliance with user requirements, specifications, or scope. Coordinate Product Development Life Cycle, leveraging Al agents to automate tasks to increase work output. Identify, track, or maintain metrics for reporting system operations. Establish processes and frameworks for continuous improvement of the platform to enhance stability, reliability, and performance. Coordinate data and report migrations from legacy systems to modern platforms, ensuring solutions are scalable & reliable.

 Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Information Technology, or closely related field, and 36 months of experience in job offered , or closely related role. Requires 3 years of experience in the following skills: Software development methodology including Waterfall, and Agile-Scrum; Change management tools including JIRA, Azure DevOps, and SharePoint; SQL and relational databases, including Oracle; Product development and delivery experience within financial services or fintech with deep domain knowledge in performance and accounting data domains; Work with multi-disciplinary teams to deliver measurable client and business outcomes; Document business requirements and translate them into technical specifications.
