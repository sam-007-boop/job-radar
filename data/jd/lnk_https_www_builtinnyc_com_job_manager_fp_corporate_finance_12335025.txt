 Job Summary: 
PayPal is seeking a Manager of Corporate FP&A to support company-wide financial forecasting, budgeting, strategic planning, reporting, and executive decision-making. This role partners with Finance and business leaders to deliver clear insights on performance, risks, and opportunities. 
 Job Description: 
 Key responsibilities 
 - Lead aspects of the monthly and quarterly forecast, annual budget, and long-range strategic planning processes.
- Analyze revenue, transaction margin, operating expenses, earnings, and free cash flow; explain variances and emerging trends.
- Prepare financial analysis and materials for senior leadership, including CFO and Board reviews.
- Partner with business units and cross-functional teams to align assumptions, assess risks and opportunities, and improve forecast quality.
- Identify ways to streamline planning and reporting through automation, analytics, and improved processes.
- Support and develop team members while managing multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
- Support special projects, including AI-enabled forecasting and analytics initiatives, and cross-functional strategic efforts as business priorities evolve.

 Qualifications 
 - Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or a related field; MBA or equivalent experience preferred.
- 5–7 years of progressive FP&A or corporate finance experience, including forecasting, budgeting, financial modeling, and executive reporting.
- Strong analytical, communication, and stakeholder-management skills, with the ability to turn complex financial information into clear recommendations.
- Advanced Excel skills and experience with financial planning systems and data tools.
- Experience in a large, complex company or payments/technology environment is a plus.
