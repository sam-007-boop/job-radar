JPMorgan Chase — Global Investment Banking – Strategic Investors Group Business Management – Associate
https://jpmc.fa.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1001/job/210786603

The Strategic Investors Group (SIG) covers financial sponsors, infrastructure investors and family offices within Global Investment Banking. 
 As a Business Management Associate in the Global Finance & Business Management team, you will support SIG’s global business management team and senior leadership in evaluating performance, advancing business priorities and improving day-to-day execution. This role requires strong analytical judgment, commercial curiosity and ownership of deliverables. You will investigate the drivers behind the numbers, translate findings into clear business insights and deliver accurate, timely materials in a fast-paced banking environment.
 Job responsibilities
 - Analyze revenue, deal pipelines, market share and client performance to identify trends, explain variances and highlight business opportunities.
- Own recurring reporting and assigned analyses, reconciling data across sources, investigating discrepancies and ensuring accuracy before distribution.
- Prepare concise presentations and commentary for senior management, business reviews and strategic discussions.
- Support client coverage and account planning through analysis of relationships, transaction activity and revenue opportunities across regions and products.
- Advance business priorities and operational initiatives, working with bankers and partners across Finance, Technology, Risk and other functions to resolve issues and deliver agreed outcomes.
- Improve reporting, data quality and processes through standardization, stronger controls and automation.
- Manage competing deadlines and time-sensitive requests, communicating progress and escalating issues early.

Required qualifications, capabilities and skills
 - At least three years of relevant experience in business management, finance, investment banking, capital markets, strategy or consulting.
- Familiarity with investment banking or capital markets businesses, products and the pace and demands of a banking environment.
- Advanced analytical skills, including the ability to interrogate financial data and analytical outputs, challenge assumptions, investigate anomalies and draw commercially relevant conclusions.
- Intellectual curiosity and a proactive approach to understanding the business, asking thoughtful questions and solving problems.
- Demonstrated ownership and attention to detail, with the ability to work independently, prioritize effectively and deliver accurate work within tight deadlines.
- Clear written and verbal communication, including the ability to distill complex information into concise insights for senior audiences.
- Advanced Excel and PowerPoint skills, with experience analyzing large datasets and preparing management presentations.
- A collaborative approach and the ability to build effective relationships across teams and levels of seniority.

Preferred qualifications, capabilities and skills
 - Experience using Dealogic or comparable platforms for transaction, market share and competitor analysis.
- Familiarity with financial sponsors, infrastructure investors or family office coverage.
- Experience with Business Objects, data visualization or workflow automation tools.
