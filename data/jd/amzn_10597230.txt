The Operations Finance Rotational Program (OFRP) - Operations Financial Analyst role is an intensive two-year, entry-level undergraduate rotational program spanning two year-long assignments in preparation for large leadership roles in finance. Candidates will be placed on various finance teams that support an aspect of customer fulfillment. What is fulfillment you ask? Fulfillment is how we refer to completing or fulfilling a customer’s Amazon.com order and the acts of picking, packing, shipping and delivering their order to meet or exceed their expectations! The program combines ongoing training opportunities and challenging job assignments to equip you with exceptional financial, business, leadership, and technical skills. The program is led by senior Amazon professionals and mentors that are committed to developing financial leaders for exciting careers. 

This rotational program will offer you the opportunity for accelerated career growth, and provides exposure to business leaders in a culture that recognizes and rewards excellence. After successful completion of this rotational program, participants are expected to take on financial leadership positions supporting one of Amazon’s many business units. 

This role requires the ability to begin full-time employment on August 9, 2027 and the willingness to relocate nationwide throughout the two-year rotational program. Placements are based on business need but do take into consideration individual location preferences.

Please note that we are unable to offer visa sponsorship.
