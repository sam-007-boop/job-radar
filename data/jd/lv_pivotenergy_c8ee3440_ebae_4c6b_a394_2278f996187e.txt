PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITIES: 

 Manage the end-to-end funding transaction proces s (e.g., draw requests, capital contributions, etc.) for one to two financing facilities (debt, equity, and tax equity/tax credit transfer) as assigned to ensure timely receipt of financing cash flows and well-organized funding processes. 
 Plan and manage execution strategy across multiple financings or facilities based on funding readiness, cash flow forecasts, and execution risks. 
 Review and provide feedback on financial reports, forecasts, and financial plans that support capital market initiatives and cash flow needs. 
 Oversee diligence, compliance, and development and construction readiness across assigned facilities, reviewing Associate findings, evaluating material risks, and approving mitigation strategies to minimize variances to financing cash flow forecasts. 
 Advise internal stakeholders and cross-functional teams throughout the project lifecycle, resolving conflicts, negotiating solutions, and aligning financing activities with company objectives. 
 Direct and review the work of Senior Associates and Associates on assigned facilities , setting priorities , resolving escalated exception s, approving funding deliverables, and providing regular feedback and input on their performance and development. 
 Set expectations with various consultants, brokers, and other third parties (e.g., appraiser, independent engineer, insurance consultants) to support project financings and evaluate diligence conclusions prior to financier presentation. 
 Facilitate and support negotiation of financing terms and documentation with lenders, tax equity investors, and counsel, owning resolution of material exceptions and final approvals. 

REQUIRED COMPETENCIES: 

 Minimum of 4 to 6+ years of experience in project finance, investment banking, M&A, or similar positions. 
 Strong project management, organizational, and analytical skills, with the ability to manage multiple facilities and deadline-driven workstreams simultaneously. 
 Proven ability to own financing outcomes independently and to direct and review the work of junior team members, exercising sound judgment on material exceptions. 
 Extensive knowledge and capabilities to work in Microsoft Excel, Word, and PowerPoint, including the ability to review project finance models and financial reports. 
 Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to draft and present detailed reports, proposals, and presentations that convey complex information to various audiences. 
 Strong problem-solving skills, with the ability to evaluate information objectively, identify root causes, and resolve complex issues across stakeholders. 
 Positive attitude and ability to enhance the company’s reputation through interpersonal interactions. 

PREFERRED COMPETENCIES: 

 Comprehensive understanding of Microsoft Excel-based financial modeling. 
 Experience negotiating and closing typical renewable energy debt and equity products/structures. 
 Working knowledge of tax credit transfer and tax equity structures and applicable tax laws and regulations. 
 Experience with Smartsheet, Salesforce, Procore, or similar platforms.

ABOUT PIVOT

Pivot Energy is a national renewable energy provider that develops, finances, builds, owns, and manages solar and energy storage projects that help decarbonize our nation’s electricity, increase equitable access to clean energy for local communities, and provide real cost-savings to American businesses and
families.

We are fiercely dedicated to accelerating the rapid transition taking place in the energy industry to a more decentralized and cleaner approach to power generation. We are committed to positively contributing to the local communities and people we serve with more than clean energy. We believe global warming poses an existential threat to our planet and that we have a responsibility to help mitigate the threat. Our portfolio includes projects that reduce energy burden for income-limited families, create workforce pathways for under-represented groups within the solar industry, and include robust community investment opportunities.

As a Certified B Corporation and one of Denver’s “Best Places to Work”, we believe that company success is driven by a healthy environment, thriving society, and workplace where all individuals are respected. We evaluate every internal decision on environmental, social, and governance (ESG) factors to determine if our actions will result in a net positive impact on the community, our employees, customers, shareholders, and the environment. Learn more in our annual ESG Report.

At Pivot, our core values guide our work internally and externally:
·       Impact – We hold ourselves accountable to having a measurable impact on our people, communities, and the planet
·       Balance – Put family first; work hard/have fun
·       Determination – Find ways to be successful no matter how difficult the challenge
·       Professionalism – Impress everyone we touch, be a team player
·       Honesty – Be truthful and transparent, externally and internally
·       Kindness – Create an environment where kindness, empathy, and vulnerability are embraced
