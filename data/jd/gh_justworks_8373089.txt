 Data & Analytics 
 About The Team 
 The Data & Analytics team is Justworks' data center of excellence — a connected set of functions spanning analytics, data science, analytics engineering, data governance, and platform data/ML engineering. You'll find a highly collaborative group motivated by craft, shared learning, and a genuine commitment to growing our impact for customers. 
 This role sits within our Analytics team, which owns how we understand company-level performance, including analysis for our most critical strategic programs. You'll be directly responsible for understanding the drivers of business performance and turning that understanding into recommendations for business leaders. 
 Who You Are 
 You are a strategic thinker and a strong problem solver. You can take an ambiguous question, break it down, and reach a sound answer. You take initiative and communicate clearly with senior leaders. 
 This is a highly consultative role accountable for generating analysis and insights that support programmatic decision-making and materially impact the business. Some recurring reporting comes with the job, but the focus is analysis: why a key metric moved, whether a program is working, and where the business should focus next. You'll own problems end to end, from framing the question with the business owner to forming and testing hypotheses and presenting a recommendation. 
 Reporting to the Senior Manager, Analytics, you'll lead 4-5 analysts as a player-coach, staying hands-on in the work while developing how the team thinks and communicates. 
 What You Will Work On 
 Performance & Program Analytics 

 Performance measurement establish key performance indicators for the business and its strategic programs and track performance over time 
 Performance diagnosis explain what's driving performance and why key metrics move, connecting signals across data sources and flagging issues early 
 Program analytics assess performance across our key strategic programs (e.g. Acquisition, Partnerships, Product Diversification) and where they can improve 
 Recommendations present findings with a clear recommendation, the evidence behind it, and the tradeoffs involved 

 Team Leadership & Partnership 

 Lead and develop a team of analysts through hiring, feedback, and career growth, coaching them to frame the question, form a hypothesis, and lead with the "so what" 
 Work closely with business leaders to understand priorities and help manage the team's time such that it goes to the highest-value work 
 Partner with Analytics Engineering and Data Governance so the team's work is built on trusted data and consistent metric definitions 
 Coordinate with Advanced Analytics on when a question needs an experiment or a model to advance our understanding 

 Embrace AI & Modern Ways of Working 

 Bring a strong AI test-and-integrate philosophy to your work, identifying points of leverage and efficiencies for delivering impactful analytical work 
 Establish practical guidelines for how the team uses AI tools in its analysis, including how output is reviewed before it is shared 
 Perform other related responsibilities as needed based on department and/or organizational needs

 Qualifications 

 7+ years of hands-on analytical experience, with a track record of consultative, hypothesis-driven problem solving for business leaders 
 Experience owning ambiguous problems end to end, from framing the question through analysis to a recommendation 
 Critical thinking and strong problem-solving instincts - you question the obvious answer and know where to dig 
 Exceptional SQL, with strong evidence of delivering sharp analysis 
 Excellent written and verbal communication, including presenting findings and recommendations to senior stakeholders 
 2+ years managing analysts and developing their thinking and communication 
 Strong analytical foundation, including cohort, funnel, and driver analysis, and working knowledge of Python or R 
 Keen understanding of B2B SaaS and its core metrics, including ARR/MRR, churn, LTV, CAC, and NRR 
 Experience building in Sigma or a comparable modern BI tool 
 Hands-on experience with AI tools in analytical work (e.g., Claude Code), and good judgment about checking their output 
 Ability to manage multiple projects and priorities at once
