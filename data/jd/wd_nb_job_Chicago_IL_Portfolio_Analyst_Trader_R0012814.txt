Neuberger Berman — Portfolio Analyst & Trader
https://nb.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/NBCareers/job/Chicago-IL/Portfolio-Analyst---Trader_R0012814

Position Overview: 

 We are seeking a Portfolio Analyst & Trader to join our Core Bond, Core Plus and US Rates teams, which are key strategies within Neuberger’s $88 billion Investment Grade Fixed Income platform. Working closely with Senior Portfolio Managers and Traders, the successful candidate will play a pivotal role in strategy implementation across institutional separate accounts and commingled funds. This role reports to the Lead Portfolio Manager for Core Bond and Core Plus, with additional mentorship from experienced team members and potential career paths in trading and portfolio management across Neuberger’s Investment Grade Fixed Income platform.

 Primary Responsibilities: 

 - Coordinate portfolio construction and implementation for Core Bond, Core Plus and US Rates portfolios, including a detailed understanding of objectives and guidelines across portfolios.
- Monitor portfolio exposures, performance and risk to inform portfolio strategy and maintain targeted exposures.
- Develop expertise in our portfolio management systems and processes. Identify opportunities for continuous improvement to our portfolio implementation and trading processes.
- Execute trades in Treasuries (as well as related derivatives), TIPS, supranationals and agencies across electronic and voice platforms.
- Develop and maintain relationships with sales and trading counterparties on the sell-side.
- Collaborate with colleagues in middle office and operations to ensure orderly trading and workflow.

 Experience & Skills Qualifications: 

 - 0-4 years of experience in trading or research on the buy-side or sell-side; fixed income experience preferred.
- Bachelor’s degree required; strong academic credentials in Economics, Finance, Accounting, Engineering, Mathematics or a related field preferred.
- Interest in financial markets, macroeconomics and investing; familiarity with fixed income concepts preferred.
- Analytical skills; capable of creatively solving problems in a constantly changing financial market environment.
- Interpersonal and communication skills.
- Motivated and resourceful self-starter.
- Attention to detail and organizational skills.  
- Experience with trading platforms, financial market data sources and portfolio management systems.
- Fluency with AI tools.

Applicants must be authorized and have the right to work in the country where the role is located without the need for current or future sponsorship.
