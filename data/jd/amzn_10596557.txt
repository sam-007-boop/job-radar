About the Role

At Twitch, Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) partners with key stakeholders across the business to guide financial decisions and deliver actionable insights that shape the company’s growth. We are seeking a Senior Financial Analyst to join our team, reporting to the Director of FP&A. In this role, you will act as a bridge between FP&A and emerging artificial intelligence tools, shifting from manual number-crunching to automated, agentic finance workflows and strategic data insights.

You will help drive visibility into Twitch’s financial performance, support strategic initiatives, and develop data-driven recommendations that influence key business outcomes. Your analysis and insights will directly inform decisions made by the CFO and executive leadership team. If you are passionate about uncovering insights that drive business growth and improve financial outcomes, this role is for you.

You can work from San Francisco, CA; Seattle, WA; New York City, NY; Los Angeles, CA.

You Will

- AI & Workflow Automation: Partner with tech teams to implement AI agents, agentic workflows, and automated data pipelines to streamline budgeting, forecasting, variance analysis, and reconciliations
FP&A Modernization: Leverage AI tools to move away from manual data extraction and report formatting to focus on real-time driver-based forecasting, KPI metrics, and strategic advisory
- Technology & ROI Business Analysis: Build financial models and evaluate business usage for enterprise software, AI licensing, and modernization initiatives. Support the annual planning and budgeting process, including data gathering, analysis, and coordination across departments
- Business Partnering: Serve as a financial partner to Enterprise Platform & Technology (EPT) and Fintech organizations to support all forecasting and performance reporting activities
- Executive Presentations: Create presentations that distill complex financial data into clear, concise charts and tables for senior leadership

- 3+ years of contributing to cross-functional initiatives that drive financial performance and strategy experience
- Bachelor's degree in finance, accounting, business, economics, or a related analytical field
- 3+ years of experience in finance, ideally with experience in FP&A at a public company
- Experience leveraging AI tools to improve processes and build scalable solutions
- Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills
- Ability to thrive in a fast-paced environment with high standards of quality, accuracy, and fidelity
- Strong organizational skills and attention to detail
- Confidence working with all levels of management

- Experience leveraging top-tier agentic AI tools (e.g., OpenAI Codex, Anthropic Claude Code, Google Antigravity) to automate and enhance workflows
- Experience with Corp FP&A functions, and know-how for leveraging finance applications (TM1, OFA, Hyperion, etc.) in analysis
- Experience in leading continuous improvement projects with measurable results
- Experience with coding languages (Python/SQL)
