Our Legal Finance and Business Management team is looking for a talented individual to help optimize how one of the world's most dynamic legal departments runs. If you're energized by variety, analytical challenges, and meaningful impact, this is your opportunity.
 As a Vice President, Finance and Business Manager in the Legal Finance and Business Management team, you will act as a trusted advisor to senior legal leadership while providing financial, analytical, and operational support that optimizes business performance. You will help navigate key initiatives, identify and mitigate business risks, and partner across teams to simplify and improve how we operate. This is a dynamic role where your ability to analyze complex information, manage competing priorities, and communicate with clarity will directly influence how the department performs and grows.
 Job Responsibilities
 - Manage  budget and forecast processes for headcount, direct expenses, allocations, and external legal spend in partnership with the Planning and Analysis team
 - Deliver  meaningful financial commentary and reporting to senior legal leadership on a recurring and ad-hoc basis
 - Prepare  management meeting agendas, business reviews, presentations, town hall materials, and offsite content for executive audiences
 - Respond  to executive ad-hoc requests related to project management, presentation development, and business analysis with a high level of quality and speed
 - Collaborate  with team members and global managers to identify ways to simplify, improve, and add value to existing business processes
 - Accelerate  legal department priorities and key areas of focus for the General Counsel and the Legal Management team
 - Partner  with Human Resources, Recruiting, Communications, Controls, Legal Projects, and Chief of Staff teams to support senior leadership and management objectives
 - Analyze  complex financial and operational data to surface insights that inform leadership decisions and resource allocation
 Required Qualifications, Capabilities, and Skills
 - Bachelor's degree in Business, Finance, Economics, or a related field
 - 5 years of experience in business management, finance, chief of staff, or a related operational role
 - Intermediate to advanced proficiency in Excel, including financial modeling, data analysis, and reporting
 - Intermediate to advanced proficiency in PowerPoint, including executive-level presentation design and development
 - Experience with budget management, spend forecasting, and headcount tracking
 - Demonstrated ability to analyze and organize complex information into clear, concise, and visually compelling deliverables
 - Excellent written and verbal communication skills with the ability to articulate thoughtful rationale to senior stakeholders
 - Proven project management skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities and deliver work to conclusion independently
 - Experience applying AI tools to assist in data analysis, reporting, and presentation development, with the ability to validate accuracy of output
 Preferred Qualifications, Capabilities, and Skills
 - Previous experience supporting a legal, compliance, or risk function
 - Familiarity with legal department operations, outside counsel management, or legal spend analytics
 - Experience preparing materials for and presenting to executive or C-suite audiences
 - Comfort working in a matrixed environment and influencing outcomes across multiple groups without direct authority
 - Experience with business process improvement or operational transformation initiatives
