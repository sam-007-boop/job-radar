About this role 

 BlackRock is one of the world’s preeminent asset management firms and a premier provider of global investment management, risk management and advisory services to institutional, intermediary, and individual investors around the world. BlackRock offers a range of solutions — from meticulous fundamental and quantitative active management approaches aimed at maximizing outperformance to highly efficient indexing strategies designed to gain broad exposure to the world’s capital markets. Our clients can access our investment solutions through a variety of product structures, including individual and institutional separate accounts, mutual funds and other pooled investment vehicles, and the industry-leading iShares® ETFs.

 Your team
 Systematic Active Equity (SAE) is the quantitative equity group within BlackRock’s Systematic investment group. We invest client assets using a systematic investment approach and have pursued client investment goals across global equity markets for more than 30 years. Research and innovation are central to our approach, bringing together finance and economics with data and computer science.
 The team manages more than $200 billion in assets and brings together quantitative finance researchers and portfolio managers, with access across BlackRock to investors and specialists in statistics, machine learning, and optimization.

 Your role and impact
 As a Systems Researcher, you will join our investment and quantitative research teams, focusing on computer science, data management, mathematical models, and computer simulations. You will apply algorithmic development and software engineering capabilities to help translate quantitative research and investment ideas into robust technology solutions.
 This role offers the opportunity to work across disciplines, collaborating with computer scientists, data scientists, quantitative researchers, and investors on complex problems that support systematic investing.

 Your responsibilities
 In every role at BlackRock, you'll be expected to apply sound judgement and critical thinking to solve complex problems, adapt as the business evolves, and combine the curiosity to explore new approaches and technologies with the rigor to challenge the results. The scope of this role also includes the following responsibilities:
 - Develop and implement algorithms and software that support systematic equity investment and quantitative research.
- Build and maintain data-management and computational solutions used by investment and quantitative research teams.
- Design and evaluate mathematical models and computer simulations to support research and investment applications.
- Collaborate with computer scientists, data scientists, quantitative researchers, and investors to translate research ideas into robust software solutions.
- Contribute to distributed-computing and high-performance computing solutions, with a focus on reliability, scalability, and research effectiveness.

 Your experience
 - The desire to work in a team environment with computer scientists, data scientists, and quantitative investors of many different backgrounds.
- A passion for algorithmic development and implementation, and a keen attention to detail.
- A fascination for some aspects of computer science: distributed computing, cryptography, and machine learning.
- Working knowledge of serverless architecture, ideally having developed or contributed to projects in that space.
- A Bachelors, Masters, or Ph.D. in computer science with 2+ years of applied experience.
- A proven track record of delivering awesome software.
- Excellent knowledge of Python / Pandas, as well as Java or C/C++.
- Well-rounded proficiency with Linux and UNIX systems.
- Experience in distributed computing and the use of GPUs.
