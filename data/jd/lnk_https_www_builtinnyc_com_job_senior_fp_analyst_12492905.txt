 The summary above was generated by AI The Opportunity: 
 The Senior FP&A Analyst will serve as the dedicated finance business partner to the General Manager of the Lifestyle Group ( Business of Home , Dwell , Domino ). In this role, you will lead commercial financial planning, drive revenue and margin optimization, and deliver actionable operational insights across digital media, print, events, and affiliate commerce. 
 We are looking for an intellectually curious, commercially minded finance professional who translates complex datasets into clear strategic recommendations for brand leadership. 
 Key Responsibilities: - Commercial Business Partnering: Act as the primary financial partner to the GM and brand leaders; translate growth strategies into long-range plans, evaluate new vertical/product launches, and evaluate sales/marketing initiatives. 

- Budgeting & Rolling Forecasts: Own the annual operating budget, monthly rolling forecasts, and quarterly outlooks across revenue streams (direct ad sales, programmatic, subscriptions, affiliate commerce, and events). 

- Month-End Close & Reconciliation: Partner closely with Corporate Accounting throughout the monthly close to review accruals, prepaid expenses, and deferred revenue schedules; reconcile operational data against the general ledger to investigate discrepancies and ensure financial reporting integrity.  

- Variance & Performance Reporting: Deliver monthly management reporting packages, highlighting key variance drivers (actual vs. budget/forecast) with narrative context and mitigation recommendations. 

- Event & Production Economics: Build deal models, event P&Ls, and production cost trackers (freelance, photo shoots, print distribution) to ensure strong contribution margins. 

- Print Media - monitor end-to-end print unit economics, liaise with third party services overseeing paper, printing, and postage/freight costs alongside rate base management, newsstand sell-through, subscriber fulfillment, and per-issue page profitability.  

- Incentive & Sales Operations Support: Model and administer sales commission and incentive structures in partnership with sales leadership and HR. 

- Systems, Automation: Collaborate with the Corporate team on the automation of financial systems using AI tools and lead financial automation initiatives within the brand.   

- M&A: Serve as the key contact for the Corporate Development Team regarding brand financial information and related analyses for buy side activities.   

 Job Qualifications: - Education & Experience: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or related quantitative field; 4–6+ years of progressive FP&A or commercial finance experience. 

- Industry Focus: Prior experience within digital media, publishing, ad tech, or subscription/e-commerce businesses is strongly preferred. 

- Technical Mastery: Advanced Excel modeling skills (dynamic financial models, sensitivity tables, scenario analysis); experience building polished executive presentation decks in PowerPoint. 

- Systems: Hands-on experience with core ERP/planning systems (e.g., Oracle, NetSuite, Adaptive Insights, or OneStream) and data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI, Looker).  Experience utilizing AI tools to optimize workflows and accuracy. 

- Communications: Strong interpersonal and communication skills, with a track record of effectively influencing operational leaders and distilling data for executive decision-makers.
