OUR MISSION

Reflection is a research lab making intelligence open and accessible for everyone to use, customize, and build on. We build open models that let anyone control their intelligence and help shape the future of AI. Our mission: make intelligence open and accessible to all.

ABOUT THE ROLE

We are looking for a Deployment Strategist to own the success of Reflection’s deployed U.S. Government programs. Once a contract is signed, you are the single accountable owner for delivery: you run the program, manage the customer relationship, orchestrate Forward Deployed Engineers and research partners, and convert successful pilots into production, renewal, and expansion. You will be the face of Reflection to program offices and end users, and the voice of the customer inside the company.

WHAT YOU’LL DO

 - Own end-to-end delivery for a portfolio of 2–4 government programs, from kickoff through production and renewal, including scope, schedule, milestones, and deliverable acceptance

 - Lead recurring customer governance: program reviews, executive syncs, and after-action reviews with COs, CORs, program managers, and mission users

 - Direct the work of Forward Deployed Engineers and coordinate research, security, and product teams to hit program commitments

 - Manage contract performance in partnership with Legal and Finance: CDRLs, invoicing milestones, modifications, and options

 - Build expansion plans for each account; identify follow-on missions, new user groups, and vehicle opportunities, and hand qualified leads to Sales and Strategy

 - Track and report program health, adoption, and mission outcomes; produce the evidence base for past-performance citations and case studies

 - Codify a repeatable delivery methodology for government deployments (onboarding, ATO support, training, adoption playbooks)

 - Serve as escalation point for customer issues and coordinate incident response with Security and Engineering

WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR

 - An active Top Secret clearance (TS/SCI required)

 - 7+ years in program management, consulting, or customer-facing delivery, with at least 3 years delivering technology programs to DoD, IC, or federal civilian customers

 - Demonstrated experience managing a multi-million-dollar government program through award, execution, and renewal

 - Working fluency in federal acquisition mechanics (CLINs, CDRLs, options, mods, ATO processes) and in how government stakeholders make decisions

 - Technical credibility with AI/ML or enterprise software; able to translate between mission users and engineers

 - Strong written and verbal communication; comfortable briefing GS-15/SES and O-6/GO-level stakeholders

 - Preferred: PMP or equivalent; prior FDE, consulting (McKinsey/BCG/Bain/Booz), or defense-tech (Palantir, Anduril, Scale) experience
