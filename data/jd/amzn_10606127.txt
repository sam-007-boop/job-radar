Amazon Leo is building the next generation of satellite broadband connectivity, and we’re looking for a talented Senior Financial Analyst to join our FP&A team. This is a unique opportunity to work in a high growth, start-up environment within Amazon, supporting financial planning and analysis for one of the company’s most ambitious initiatives.

In this role, you will support reporting and analysis for Amazon Leo, partnering closely finance and business leaders to ensure accurate, timely, and insightful financial data. You will help build and maintain reporting tools, support planning cycles, and contribute to data driven decision making across the organization.

Key job responsibilities
- Assist in building and maintaining automated financial reports and dashboards that provide senior leadership with clear visibility into business performance and financial trends
- Perform variance analysis across assigned financial areas, identifying root causes and communicating findings to finance and business partners
- Assist in developing financial models to forecast key business drivers, incorporating relevant assumptions and trends specific to the business
- Identify and lead opportunities to improve, automate, and simplify existing reporting processes — reducing manual effort and increasing data accuracy
- Explore and implement AI-driven tools and approaches to streamline workflows, accelerate reporting cycles, and strip out manual steps across finance processes
- Help rebuild and rethink existing reporting processes, bringing a fresh perspective to how work gets done and redesigning workflows to be more scalable and efficient
- Support ad hoc analyses and special projects as needed to inform strategic financial and business decisions

A day in the life
As a Senior Financial Analyst on our Leo FP&A team, you'll be at the intersection of financial strategy and operational excellence. You'll spend meaningful time looking at how the work gets done — digging into existing reporting processes to find opportunities to simplify, automate, and rebuild them from the ground up. You might spend part of your day exploring how AI tools can strip manual steps out of a recurring report, or prototyping a new approach to a process that's been built incrementally over time. Your work directly impacts how leadership understands the financial health and opportunities within our Leo business — and how efficiently our team can get there.
