JPMorgan Chase — 2027 Chase Leadership Development Program — Full-Time Analyst (Winter Cohort) Consumer & Community Banking Communications Focused - (NY Metro & Columbus)

Great communications shape how millions of customers, employees and other stakeholders experience Chase. If you’re a strong writer, a curious storyteller and someone who loves big ideas, this program could be a great fit. 
 As a Full-Time Analyst in the Chase Leadership Development Program (CLDP), Winter Cohort, you’ll learn from experienced communicators over 12 to 18 months. You’ll build your skills across external and internal communications as well as our centers of excellence. You’ll also support campaigns and events, get meaningful mentorship and explore different parts of our communications team. 
 The Winter Cohort kicks off in March 2027 in our New York Metro or Columbus, OH locations (please indicate location preference in application). This direct-hire early career program gives you a broad look at Consumer and Community Banking (CCB) Communications. You’ll take part in training to strengthen your business skills and sharpen your communications craft. You’ll also have a dedicated program manager and mentor to support your career growth. 
 During the program, you'll have opportunities to gain experience across: 
 - Internal communications:  Connect with and engage employees across Consumer and Community Banking (CCB). 

- External communications:  Help shape Chase’s story in the media, with customers and with the public. 

- Center of Excellence:  Support teams in multimedia, social media, business management or events. 

 Job Responsibilities: 
 - Write clear, compelling and on-brand content in formats like press releases, executive messages, internal emails, social posts, intranet articles and talking points. 

- Partner with internal and external communicators and our centers of excellence on high-profile campaigns, product launches and firmwide storytelling. 

- Help plan and deliver employee content and campaigns that bring the Consumer and Community Banking story to life for teams across the country. 

- Support media relations by drafting press materials, tracking coverage and preparing briefing documents for spokespeople. 

- Create social-first content, manage Chase’s editorial calendars and keep an eye on trends, cultural moments and audience behavior. 

- Work with teams across Marketing, Brand, Legal, Compliance, Product and Consumer and Community Banking to create clear, coordinated communications. 

- Use data and performance insights to understand what’s working and make your storytelling, targeting and results even stronger. 

- Share fresh ideas, including thoughtful ways to use AI tools and new technologies, while protecting accuracy, integrity and the Chase brand. 

 Required qualifications, capabilities and skills 
 - You have entry level experience from internships, jobs or volunteer roles in campus media, agencies, corporate communications or newsrooms, with exposure to communications, public relations, journalism, marketing and/or social media 

- You write, edit and tell stories exceptionally well, and you know how to make complex topics easier to understand. 

- You embrace change and stay curious about new technologies, including AI, and how they can improve communications work. 

- You can manage competing priorities, stakeholders and deadlines in a fast-paced environment. 

- You build trusted relationships easily. 

- You use good judgment, pay close attention to detail and care about delivering quality work. 

 Preferred qualifications, capabilities and skills 
 - You’ve earned a B.A., B.S. or fifth-year M.A. or M.S. degree, with a graduation date between December 2024 and December 2026. 

- You studied Communications, Marketing, Journalism, Public Relations or a related field, and have hands-on experience with writing, editing, project management and presentations. 

- You know your way around media relations and design tools, social platforms, content management systems or editorial workflows. 

- You’ve used AI-enabled productivity tools responsibly to support content development, research, synthesis and planning. 

- You’re genuinely interested in financial services, banking and the brands and products that reach millions of consumers every day.
