About this role 

 Company: BlackRock Financial Management, Inc. 
 Job Title: Associate, Emerging Markets
 Location: 50 Hudson Yards, New York, NY 10001

 Job Duties: Work alongside deal team members to help evaluate and execute infrastructure investment opportunities across the energy, transportation, digital and water and waste sectors. Responsible for conducting market research, financial modeling and analysis, and overall project management. Collaborate with and help support deal team members on aspects of new platform investments, add-on investments, divestitures and related financings. Take a role in processing new investment opportunities, ranging from market assessment, to competitive positioning, to initial underwriting and deal structuring. Work on diligence efforts on pursued deals, including business diligence, risk underwriting, and managing external advisors. Build robust flexible financial models to support deal underwriting and deal structuring efforts. Perform ad-hoc analysis to support transaction negotiations. Provide hands-on support to portfolio companies.

 Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in Engineering (any field), Finance, Accounting, Business Administration or a related field and three (3) years of experience in the job offered or as an Associate, Analyst or a related role. Must have three (3) years of experience with: Valuation analysis including cash flow modeling; M&A transaction processes; Leveraging publicly disclosed information, sell-side research, and premium data sources (including Bloomberg, FactSet and Capital IQ) to conduct business analysis; and Facilitating due diligence analysis of strategic sale targets for potential acquirers. Must have two (2) years of experience with: Investment banking experience; Facilitating financial and non-financial due diligence analyses for potential acquisition targets; and Liaising with external portfolio companies for monthly updates. Must have one (1) year of experience with: Private equity experience; Financial model development for budget and capital planning and oversight for portfolio companies’ models; Development of investment committee materials and memos; and Experience with infrastructure investment areas including energy, transportation, digital and water and waste sectors.
