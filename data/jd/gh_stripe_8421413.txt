Who we are 
 About Stripe  
 Stripe, LLC. is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. 
 What you’ll do 
 Responsibilities 

 Analyze and troubleshoot complex technical issues through direct user interaction (Slack) and leveraging your knowledge of API documentation.  
 Develop product and platform expertise, working closely with Engineering, Product, and Operations teams to diagnose and resolve user issues.  
 Strategically optimize and scale support processes to enhance efficiency and improve the overall user experience, incorporating data- driven insights and metrics.  
 Lead continuous improvement initiatives aimed at hitting key performance metrics.  
 Create and refine documentation to empower users to resolve issues via self-service, reducing dependency on support teams.  

 Constantly challenge the status quo and push for innovation in user support strategies and operational processes. 
 Minimum requirements 
 Must have a Bachelor's degree or foreign equivalent in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Business administration or a related field, plus 2 years of experience in customer-facing product support roles with a focus on troubleshooting technical issues. 
 Must also have 2 years of experience in each of the following: 

 Using SQL and ability to use template or premade SQL queries as part of investigation; 
 Debugging HTTP request/response cycles and identifying error codes (4xx/5xx); 
 Using Postman, cURL, or Insomnia to test endpoints and reproduce integration issues; 
 Blockchain Explorers including Etherscan and Solscan to verify transaction status and finality; 
 On-chain Event Logs and smart contract interactions including ERC-20 or SPL token transfers; 
 Card Authorization flow including Auth, Capture, Reversal, and Dispute; 
 Troubleshooting KYC/KYB (Know Your Customer/Business) workflows via API; 
 IDV (Identity Verification) status mapping and document upload failure points; and 
 Custodial vs. non-custodial wallet architectures.
