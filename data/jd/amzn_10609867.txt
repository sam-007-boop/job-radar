Amazon (incl. AWS data-center finance) — Senior Financial Analyst, AWS Internal Rates Team

Are you ready to make a impact by optimizing AWS Cloud Usage efficiencies through internal pricing decisions? As a Sr. Financial Analyst on the AWS Internal Rates team, you will play a crucial role in managing complex processes cross functionally to set internal pricing and forecast internal usage of AWS Cloud products. This position offers an exciting opportunity to partner with stakeholders across Amazon and AWS to maximize cloud cost efficiency and enhance Amazon's long-term free cash flow.

In this role, you'll lead cross-functional teams and processes, conducting data deep dives to inform critical pricing decisions. Your analytical expertise will be instrumental in developing structured pricing frameworks, while your strong communication skills will enable effective stakeholder management across multiple business units. If you thrive on solving complex problems through collaborative partnerships and data-driven insights, this role presents an exceptional opportunity to help shape financial strategy across AWS and Amazon.

Key job responsibilities
- Partner with stakeholders across Amazon and AWS to establish and maintain internal pricing structures that is sound and cost driven.
- Lead cross-functional teams and processes to identify opportunities for cost optimization, developing analytical frameworks that deliver actionable insights and recommendations to senior leadership.
- Analyze complex datasets to develop financial models that accurately forecast the impact of internal pricing decisions on P&Ls.
- Identify trends, anomalies, and opportunities for process improvement, creating scalable and auditable data analysis processes.
- Good understanding of AWS Services and cost drivers.
- Collaborate with Fintech and Finance teams to automate existing manual process at scale.
- Collaborate with forecasting data science teams and Finance to improve cloud usage forecasting processes. 

About the team
The AWS Internal Rates team sits at the intersection of finance and cloud technology, managing the complex process of setting internal pricing across AWS cloud services. Our mission is to ensure these rates aligns to underlying cost and also drive optimal internal usage behavior that maximizes cost efficiency and enhances Amazon's long-term free cash flow. 

Our team works collaboratively to develop sophisticated pricing frameworks that balance operational needs with financial objectives. We partner closely with stakeholders throughout Amazon and AWS to understand service requirements, usage patterns, and financial implications of our decisions. As we continue to evolve our approach to internal pricing, we're looking for analytical minds who can help us create innovative solutions to increasingly complex challenges.
