About Fervo Energy 
 Fervo Energy (NASDAQ: FRVO) is a modern power company developing next-generation geothermal resources to meet the growing demand for affordable, reliable, 24/7 carbon-free energy. Our mission is to transform geothermal into America’s most dependable and affordable source of clean, around-the-clock power. By combining innovations in horizontal drilling, fiber-optic sensing, and advanced reservoir engineering, Fervo has developed a repeatable approach to building utility-scale enhanced geothermal systems. Our flagship Cape Station development in Utah is planned to deliver 500 MW of firm, carbon-free power, and we are advancing a development pipeline of more than 50 GW. Join us and work alongside world-class engineers, geoscientists, operators, project builders, and business leaders working to make geothermal a cornerstone of a more reliable, affordable, and sustainable energy future. Position Description 

 Fervo is working to build the most cost-effective, repeatable geothermal power plants in the world. Delivering on this mission requires disciplined execution not only in the field, but across financing, project development, and capital allocation. As Fervo scales, strong financial planning, forecasting, and analytical rigor are essential to enabling growth while ensuring efficient deployment of capital. 
 The FP&A Associate will report to the Corporate Finance Manager and partner closely with executive leadership and cross-functional teams in a fast-paced, high-growth environment. This role is suited for a highly analytical and detail-oriented professional with strong financial intuition and the ability to translate complex data into clear insights. Key responsibilities include building robust financial models, supporting critical planning processes, and enabling informed decision-making across both project and corporate priorities. 
 The FP&A Associate will serve as a key contributor to Fervo’s financial planning and analysis function, supporting both project-level and corporate financial processes. This role requires strong analytical capabilities, attention to detail, and the ability to synthesize complex financial information into clear, actionable insights for a range of stakeholders. 
 Success in this position means providing high-quality financial visibility across the organization — ensuring budgets and forecasts are accurate, variances are well understood, and leadership has the information needed to make timely, data-driven decisions. The FP&A Associate will play a critical role in strengthening financial discipline, improving reporting infrastructure, and enabling efficient capital deployment as the company scales. 

 Responsibilities 
 Financial Planning & Forecasting 

 Build and maintain project- and corporate-level budgets, forecasts, and financial models to support strategic decision-making 

 Evaluate the financial impact of changes in project scope, including incremental work and design modifications 

 Forecast capital expenditures, operating expenses, and other key financial metrics 

 Support long-range planning and scenario analysis to inform capital allocation decisions 

 Business Partnership 

 Partner with engineering, drilling, and project development teams to develop and communicate project budgets and forecasts 

 Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to understand cost drivers and operational performance 

 Translate financial concepts into actionable insights for non-financial teams to support decision-making 

 Reporting & Insights 

 Support the preparation of monthly executive financial review materials, including KPIs and performance dashboards 

 Monitor project and corporate financial performance, ensuring accurate tracking and reporting of capital expenditures 

 Conduct variance analyses on forecasts and budgets, identifying drivers of current and projected deviations 

 Develop and refine dashboards and reporting tools to improve visibility into financial performance 

 Strategic & Ad Hoc Analysis 

 Perform ad hoc financial analyses and translate insights into clear, concise presentations for stakeholders 

 Support strategic initiatives through financial modeling and investment analysis 

 Contribute to continuous improvement of FP&A tools, processes, and reporting capabilities 

 Assist in evaluating new opportunities and initiatives through financial diligence and analysis
