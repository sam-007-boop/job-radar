 What We Stand For and Where You’ll Come In The Senior Finance Analyst will be the primary Finance partner for our Manufacturing, Procurement, and Inventory groups within Operations, driving analysis in co-man pricing, ingredient purchasing, and network optimization. This role supports the COGS and gross margin forecast for these functions and runs a recurring cycle of weekly, monthly, and quarterly work. As we add channels, products, co-mans, and suppliers, you will go deep on the details, understand our growing co-man network and recipe mix, and distill complex financial models into clear narratives on business performance. This position will report to the Senior Manager, Finance.
 One Team: We don’t think of ourselves as “Acquisition Marketers”, “Engineers”, “Data Analysts”, or “Product Managers”. Beyond denoting skill sets and areas of expertise, we don’t think departments matter. We’d rather align ourselves to the goals we’re working to achieve and make sure we have necessary subject matter expertise to drive meaningful impact. We strive to orient ourselves around customer problems TOGETHER – getting the right people, with the right context, in the right rooms/Zooms to solve problems holistically.
 We are skeptical about everything and precious about nothing:   Ideas can and should come from anywhere, and we aren’t tied to our own. We proactively source input. We talk to our customers and leads regularly and are quick to change course if we know there’s a better or more impactful way to solve problems.
 We consider the customer journey in all of our decisions:   We know that no interaction exists in a silo and therefore understand how important every single one is.  We ensure our strategy sets prospective and new customers up for success and drives long-term retention.  We answer questions and address problems early and proactively.  We understand the value of different channels, initiatives, and messages and know how to articulate impact and advocate for prioritization holistically.
 We Execute For Impact: We don’t subscribe to “best practices” or “industry KPIs”. We’re uninterested in how we compare to “benchmarks”; instead we orient ourselves around being the best we can possibly be. Similarly, we don’t subscribe to rigid or classical expectations of roles – i.e. acquisition is hyper-focused on improving customer retention and experience.
 We Are Focused and Work Without Assumption : We are not beholden to ideas. We have goals and believe everything beyond that is a series of hypotheses to validate. To that end, we seek to work in sequence and not in parallel. We constantly ask ourselves, “what’s the most important hypothesis I should be working on right now? How do I confirm or reject that hypothesis as fast as possible?”. We rarely have timelines/deadlines and are constantly taking in new information and adjusting our priorities accordingly. We don’t expect to be perfect the first time.
 How You'll Make An Impact - Serve as the primary Finance partner for Manufacturing, Procurement, and Inventory, providing analysis and insights that shape our supply chain decisions.
- Own the monthly co-man pricing analysis: review and validate costing models, explain price and cost variances with Manufacturing and Procurement, and identify and track cost savings opportunities.
- Support the COGS and gross margin forecast, including weekly pro-forma estimates, monthly variance analysis, and latest estimate (LE) updates.
- Build the monthly business review (MBR) materials for these functions, turning financial models into clear narratives on business performance.
- Analyze ingredient purchasing and our manufacturing network, including recipe mix, supplier costs, and network optimization scenarios, to inform sourcing and manufacturing decisions.
- Develop, maintain, and improve financial models, and run ad hoc and scenario analyses to support leadership decisions.
- Automate recurring reporting and build repeatable processes that scale as we add new channels, products, co-mans, and suppliers.
- Support integrated business planning and inventory forecasting, keeping operational plans and financial outlooks aligned.
- Contribute to the annual budget and long-range plan with financial analysis and modeling, partnering with budget owners.
- Work closely with the Operations Finance team and the broader Finance team to integrate the latest margin and inventory outlooks into forecasts, models, and analyses.
 We're Excited About You Because - You have 5+ years of experience in finance, consulting, or banking, including time in a high-growth D2C and/or subscription business, ideally within the food manufacturing industry.
- You are an expert in Excel/Google Sheets with deep financial modeling and forecasting experience, and a strong understanding of accounting fundamentals.
- You have a strong grasp of operations and supply chain dynamics and understand the nuances of subscription-based businesses.
- You are detail-oriented and comfortable rolling up your sleeves to understand what is driving the numbers, while stepping back to see the bigger picture.
- You are a skilled communicator who can distill complexity into simple terms to drive decision making.
- You are proficient with data: able to pull, join, and analyze large datasets independently and build repeatable, automated reporting.
- You are fluent with AI tools and use them to work faster and smarter (e.g., accelerating analysis, automating recurring reporting, pressure-testing models), with sound judgment in validating their outputs.
- You have experience with BI tools. Experience with BigQuery and NetSuite is preferred.
- You are a self-starter and owner who thrives in fast-paced, ambiguous environments, manages multiple recurring workstreams, and prioritizes effectively.
- You build strong cross-functional relationships, facilitate discussions, and drive consensus.
- You love dogs.

The city has a thriving fintech scene and is a major player in verticals ranging from AI to biotech, cybersecurity and digital media.
