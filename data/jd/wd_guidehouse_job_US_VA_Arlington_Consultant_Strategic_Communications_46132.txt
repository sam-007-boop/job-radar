 What You Will Do: 

 - Support the development and execution of strategic communications, change management, and stakeholder engagement strategies for complex organizational initiatives.
- Analyze organizational changes, stakeholder needs, and business priorities to identify communications and engagement needs.
- Translate complex organizational, operational, and policy information into clear, concise, audience-centered communications.
- Draft and edit executive communications, employee announcements, talking points, FAQs, briefing materials, newsletters, presentations, toolkits, and other communications products.
- Develop infographics, process diagrams, timelines, one-pagers, presentation graphics, and other visual communications that make complex concepts easier to understand.
- Create polished, executive-ready PowerPoint presentations that combine strong messaging with effective visual storytelling.
- Support development and maintenance of communications calendars, stakeholder maps, message frameworks, engagement plans, and related project materials.
- Help plan and support stakeholder engagement activities, including leadership sessions, employee engagement activities, listening sessions, and feedback mechanisms.
- Synthesize stakeholder feedback, employee questions, and other qualitative information to identify themes and recommend improvements to communications and engagement approaches.
- Collaborate with subject matter experts and client stakeholders to turn technical or evolving information into accessible communications.
- Manage multiple deliverables in a fast-paced environment while maintaining quality, consistency, attention to detail, and responsiveness to client needs.
- The role reflects the engagement's integrated approach to leadership alignment, change impact analysis, stakeholder engagement, strategic communications, workforce feedback, and visual communications. 

 What You Will Need: 

 - Ability to OBTAIN and MAINTAIN a Federal or DoD Public Trust; Candidates with an ACTIVE PUBLIC TRUST or SUITABILITY are preferred.
- Must be a US Citizen due to nature of client engagements.
- Bachelor’s degree in communications, marketing, public relations, business, graphic design, organizational change, public policy, or a related field.
- Two (2) years of relevant professional experience in strategic communications, management consulting, organizational change management, marketing, stakeholder engagement, or a related field.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with demonstrated ability to communicate complex topics clearly and concisely.
- Strong Microsoft PowerPoint skills and experience developing polished, professional presentations.
- Experience developing visual communications such as infographics, process graphics, timelines, executive presentations, or similar materials.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to work through ambiguous or evolving assignments.
- Strong attention to detail and demonstrated ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines.
- Ability to collaborate effectively with team members, subject matter experts, and client stakeholders.

 What Would Be Nice to Have : 

 - Experience supporting federal government clients or working in a federal consulting environment.
- Experience supporting organizational transformations, reorganizations, technology implementations, workforce initiatives, or other significant change efforts.
- Familiarity with organizational change management methods and concepts, including stakeholder analysis, change impact assessment, communications planning, and change readiness.
- Experience developing executive or senior leadership communications.
- Experience with visual design tools such as Adobe Creative Suite, Canva, or similar platforms.
- Experience developing accessible or Section 508-compliant communications and presentations.
- Experience facilitating or supporting stakeholder interviews, focus groups, workshops, or employee listening sessions.
- Demonstrated ability to use data, feedback, or stakeholder insights to improve communications and engagement strategies.
