 An 11-week Finance and Accounting internship offering placements in corporate accounting, fund and broker-dealer accounting, or financial reporting. Responsibilities include auditing expense reports, supporting cash receipts and journal entries, assisting with regulatory and financial reporting, monitoring compliance, overseeing accounting records and NAV calculations, supporting audits, and researching U.S. GAAP and IFRS questions. Interns also identify process improvements and collaborate with stakeholders.

 Placement Opportunities for Our 11-Week Program: 
 Corporate Accounting 
 - Perform audits of Travel and Expense reports through Concur in accordance with Citadel's Expense Reimbursement policy 
 - Support the accounting team on various cash receipt and disbursement related tasks 
 - Assist the team with the close process and various monthly journal entries 

 Fund & Broker-Dealer Accounting 
 - Assist with regulatory reporting for broker-dealer entities 
 - Assist with monthly closes and periodic financial reporting 
 - Assist with counterparty compliance monitoring and reporting 
 - Drive process improvements, among other projects 
 - Assist with overseeing the accounting books and records 
 - Oversee the calculation of fund and investor level NAVs and returns 

 Financial Reporting 
 - Support the Financial Reporting team in the preparation and review of quarterly and year-end financial statements 
 - Assist the team in the management of the external audits 
 - Support the financial statement publication process 
 - Engage with stakeholders throughout the financial statement process 
 - Research accounting questions under U.S. GAAP and IFRS

 What you need to know about the NYC Tech Scene

The city has a thriving fintech scene and is a major player in verticals ranging from AI to biotech, cybersecurity and digital media.
