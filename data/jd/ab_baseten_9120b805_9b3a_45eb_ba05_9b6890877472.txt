THE ROLE

As the Revenue Strategy & Operations Manager for Baseten’s AI Natives, AI Strategics, and Labs segments, you’ll be the day-to-day RevOps partner to the leaders who run them: our Sales Leaders across AI Natives & Strategics and our Head of Sales for Labs. You’ll give them the analysis and operating support to run their segments, and turn their priorities into the plans, targets, and operating cadence the segments run on.

These segments cover some of Baseten’s largest and fastest-growing customers. Revenue is consumption-based, so growth comes from workloads scaling inside existing accounts as much as from new logos, and planning, forecasting, and pipeline management have to reflect that. You’ll sit on the Revenue Strategy & Operations team and work closely with Finance, Comp, Enablement, Territory Ops, GTM Engineering, and Sales leadership. You’ll own the segments’ numbers, the reviews that inspect them, and the processes and systems that keep them reliable.

WHAT YOU'LL WORK ON

 - Segment planning: Turn segment leaders’ priorities into joint plans with clear decisions, owners, and deadlines, and track them to completion.

 - Operating cadence: Run segment MBRs, QBRs, and forecast reviews so leaders understand the business, decisions get made, and every follow-up has an owner.

 - Planning and targets: Build the models behind annual and in-year planning for the segments: headcount, quota, and pipeline targets.

 - Pipeline and forecasting: Inspect deals, pressure-test commits, and surface risk early in a business where revenue moves with customer usage.

 - Segment analytics: Own funnel and consumption analytics, and build analytics products with GTM Engineering that flag risks and opportunities early.

 - Process and systems: Build the segments’ sales processes into our systems and get segment managers onboarded onto them.
