 Posting Summary 
 Rutgers, The State University of New Jersey is seeking a temporary part-time Finance Manager for the Institute for Health ( IFH ), Health Care Policy and Aging Research.

Among the key duties of the position are the following: 
- Partners with CFO to lead budget and projection process for IFH including developing templates and updating financial system.

- Analyzes budget variances and prepares monthly grant-tracking spreadsheet.

- Manages and maintains workforce planning data and position-related financial information.

- Prepares and distributes recurring and ad hoc financial reports to support operational and strategic decision-making.

- Oversees budget projections, fiscal year grant planning, and reconciliation reviews to ensure accurate forecasting of revenue and expenses.

- Collaborates with the centers and other departments to ensure workforce charging instructions are accurately reflected in budgets and financial forecasts.

- Adheres to established financial deadlines and ensure deliverables are completed in a timely manner to allow for appropriate review and approval.
