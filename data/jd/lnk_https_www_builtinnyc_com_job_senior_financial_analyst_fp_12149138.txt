Our distribution and publishing services deliver, monetize and protect songs across the globe for over 450,000+ musicians, record labels, studios, brands, investors, and platforms. The Role 
 We are looking for a sharp, proactive Senior Financial Analyst to join our growing Finance team in NYC. In this role, you will be our dedicated FP&A owner, acting as the bridge between financial strategy, operational data, and business execution.
You’ll build and maintain our core financial models, partner closely with cross-functional leaders across A&R, deal operations, and marketing, and deliver actionable insights directly to executive leadership. If you love digging into complex data sets, building scenario models from scratch, and thrive in an environment where your work directly shapes growth strategy, we want to hear from you.   Based out of our New York office, this role will report directly to Too Lost's SVP of Finance and work on a hybrid schedule (4 days in office/1 day from home). 
 What You'll Do 
 Budgeting & Forecasting - Drive the annual budgeting process, ongoing forecasts, and long-term financial planning.
- Partner with department leaders to develop budgets, monitor performance, and evaluate business initiatives.
 Cash Flow & Liquidity Management - Develop and maintain cash flow forecasts to support liquidity planning.
- Prepare recurring cash and debt reporting, including projected borrowing and funding requirements.
- Forecast capital needs related to artist advances, catalog investments, and other strategic initiatives.
 Financial Analysis & Reporting - Prepare financial analyses for senior leadership and external stakeholders.
- Evaluate financial scenarios, business performance, and potential M&A opportunities.

 What You'll Bring 
 - 3–6 years of total experience in FP&A, Strategic Finance, Corporate Development, Investment Banking, or Finance Consulting (Entertainment/technology exp. preferred).
- Advanced financial modeling skills in Excel/Google Sheets; proven experience building dynamic three-statement models from scratch.
- Hands-on experience with modern ERPs (e.g., NetSuite, QuickBooks Online)
- Exceptional ability to translate complex numerical data into crisp visual presentations and strategic narratives for leadership.
- Degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or a related quantitative field.
