Amazon (incl. AWS data-center finance) — Senior Financial Analyst, AWS Infrastructure FP&A

Do you want to directly influence the future of some of the most successful products in cloud computing? Come join Amazon Web Services Finance! Amazon Web Services (AWS) is a highly reliable, scalable, low-cost infrastructure platform in the cloud that powers hundreds of thousands of enterprise, government, and start-up businesses and organizations in over 190 countries around the world. 

Amazon Web Services (AWS) is seeking a Senior Financial Analyst to play a key role on the Infrastructure Central Finance team. This team is responsible for planning and tracking billions of dollars of investments and operating expenses in data centers, servers and networking equipment and that allow AWS to serve customers across the globe.

This role will be responsible for driving insights and analysis that will help shape the AWS investment strategy in servers, inclusive Generative AI. The successful candidate be part of a team to build scalable analyses, key performance indicators, and insights that will be shared with AWS Business and Finance Leadership to inform decision making. This position will work closely with multiple stakeholders in finance, product development and supply chain across the organization to align on critical inputs, ranges of outcomes, and confidence intervals. This is a high judgment role that has direct exposure to Amazon’s C-suite and board of directors, requiring someone who can execute without structured guidance, navigate ambiguity, and effectively deliver results in a fast-paced environment. This is an exciting opportunity to join one of the most innovative and dynamic businesses at Amazon.

Key job responsibilities

- Develop and maintain detailed infrastructure capital and spending forecasts; track actual spending and bridge variances; partner with stakeholders to identify and communicate insights and business risks to senior leaders.
- Identify opportunities for simplifying or automating processes with upstream and downstream stakeholders to eliminate defects and reduce lead time to build reports and insights 
- Coordinate and track the development of infrastructure goals and strategic projects; identify and communicate insights and business risks to leaders.

- 4+ years of Accounts Receivable or Account Payable experience
- 4+ years of applying key financial performance indicators (KPIs) to analyses experience
- 4+ years of creating process improvements with automation and analysis experience
- 3+ years of finance, accounting, or related analytical field experience
- Bachelor's degree in business, finance, accounting, tax, economics, or a related analytical field
- Experience with advanced use of SQL for data mining and business intelligence

- 4+ years of participating in continuous improvement projects in your team to scale and improve controllership with measurable results experience
- MBA, or CPA
- Experience working with large-scale data mining and reporting tools (examples: SQL, MS Access, Essbase, Cognos) and other financial systems (examples: Oracle, SAP, Lawson, JD Edwards)
