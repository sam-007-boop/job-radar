 About the role 
 Ethos is looking for an ambitious self-starter to join our Finance & Strategy Team. As part of this team you will play a major role in defining the company’s financial plan, providing financial and operational decision support to cross-functional partners and developing and executing various strategic initiatives. You will play an integral part in Ethos' success by scaling and improving our corporate finance functions, driving financial planning processes, and working with various business partners to develop strategic analyses on key business initiatives.
 This role will have high visibility across the executive team and will require you to solve the most complex, interesting challenges of a rapidly-scaling business. If you enjoy working in an environment that is fast-paced, analytically rigorous, mission-driven and impact-oriented, read on and get in touch!
 Duties and Responsibilities: 
 - Apply business judgment and financial insights to drive decision making related to unit economics, operations, core business expansion, and new opportunities
- Develop and maintain financial and operational plans, including revenue, cost and headcount targets
- Apply a data-driven analytical approach to influence decision making and support company leadership in evaluating strategic initiatives
- Improve the speed and efficiency of our growth by collaborating closely with Product, Marketing, and Operations teams
- Develop and monitor critical financial performance metrics, and communicate actionable insights to relevant stakeholders. Champion analytics across the company
 Qualifications and Skills: 
 - A minimum of 1 to 4 years of relevant work experience in investment banking, consulting, and / or private equity / growth equity, strategic / operational experience at a high-growth technology company a plus
- Best-in-class excel skills and extensive experience developing insightful financial models and quantitative analyses, paired with strong critical thinking and sound business judgment
- Ability to flex between high-level strategic thinking and deep analytical problem solving
- Motivated self-starter with a track record of success and a proven ability to juggle multiple projects/tasks while hitting deadlines. Desire to own and drive projects from Day 1
- Excellent communication skills and effective cross-functional collaborator
- Exceptional attention to detail
- Deep understanding of valuation methods and strong working knowledge of GAAP 

 Bonuses: 
 - SQL knowledge
- Ability to use statistical analysis tools
