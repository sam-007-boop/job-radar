Amazon (incl. AWS data-center finance) — Senior Financial Analyst, E2E North America FP&A

North America End-to-End (E2E) Financial Planning and Analysis (FP&A) sits at the center of Amazon's Supply Chain. You'll have the opportunity to shape one of the largest, fastest supply chains on the planet. We collaborate across Amazon's end-to-end supply chain to ensure fast, on-time delivery at the right cost structure. Our Finance team works closely with operations and finance leaders to surface insights, track performance, and drive analysis to optimize capacity planning, network design, transportation execution, and customer experience.

E2E North America (NA) seeks a Senior Financial Analyst who is a creative problem solver ready to surface actionable insights and drive performance. This role owns E2E financial planning for NA Operations and partners closely across Customer Fulfillment, Inbound Transportation, and Outbound Transportation to consolidate financials and craft the E2E narrative. This is a high-visibility role that involves preparing documents for reviews with senior operations leadership. Our teams are rapidly expanding and increasing scope, providing a rich career-growth environment.

Your primary responsibility is to own NA Operations cost and performance reporting by developing insights, maintaining a pulse on key operations metrics, and driving the narrative of how those metrics impact financial performance. You will partner with a broad range of global finance and business leaders across Operations and Retail categories, as well as stakeholders in capacity planning and FP&A. You will design, develop, and maintain reports on strategic input drivers to continuously improve the economics of the business.

Key job responsibilities
- Drive strategic supply chain initiatives with business and finance partners by presenting financial and non-financial trade-offs using data, making thoughtful judgment calls in ambiguous environments where data may be scarce.
- Consolidate and improve NA FP&A planning and reporting processes, building and automating financial models and reports to increase accuracy and scalability.
- Own financial reporting for your business area, validating data integrity and surfacing insights that influence strategic decision-making across operations leadership.
- Identify and resolve data-integrity issues, strengthen internal controls to address financial risk, and maintain team tools, standard operating procedures, and process documentation to support knowledge sharing.
- Partner with cross-functional teams across Operations, Retail, and capacity planning to align on priorities, deliver variance analyses, and support monthly and quarterly business reviews.

A day in the life
Your day begins by reviewing the latest operational and financial metrics, identifying trends or anomalies that warrant a deeper look. You collaborate with partners in Customer Fulfillment and Transportation to align on the story behind the numbers, then translate those findings into clear recommendations for senior leaders. Between ad-hoc deep dives and ongoing process-improvement projects, you balance analytical rigor with the pace of a rapidly growing network.

About the team
The E2E NA FP&A team serves as a strategic partner to North America Operations, connecting the dots across fulfillment, inbound, and outbound transportation to deliver a unified financial perspective. We are a collaborative, inclusive group that values curiosity and continuous improvement. As the network scales, our scope grows with it, giving you the chance to tackle new challenges and expand your expertise while helping shape the future of Amazon's supply chain.
