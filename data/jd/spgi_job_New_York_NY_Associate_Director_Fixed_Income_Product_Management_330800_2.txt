About the Role: 

 Grade Level (for internal use): 
12 Company Name: S&P OpCo, LLC
 Job Title: Associate Director, Fixed Income Product Management
 Location: 55 Water Street, New York, NY 10041 (Hybrid work schedule. On days not in office, telecommuting permitted within normal commuting distance of New York, NY office)

 Job Duties: S&P OpCo, LLC seeks an Associate Director, Fixed Income Product Management who will conduct quantitative and qualitative analysis of fixed income markets and bond market data to support index design, methodology development, and product enhancement. Lead the development of new index methodologies both proprietary indices and indices created for specific client requests and coordinate the index launch process across internal teams including research, operations, governance, and sales. Perform data analysis to evaluate index performance, market trends, and product opportunities. Monitor fixed income market developments and competitor index offerings to identify opportunities for product innovation and enhancements. Prepare research reports, presentations, and analytical materials explaining index methodologies, market developments, and product strategies. Communicate fixed income index methodologies, market analysis, and product insights to internal stakeholders and external clients through presentations and written reports. Present fixed income indices and index capabilities in client meetings, webinars and industry events. Support periodic index reviews, consultations, and methodology updates to maintain the accuracy and relevance of index products.

 Job Requirements: Master’s degree in Finance, Economics, Mathematics, Statistics, or related quantitative field plus three (3) years of experience in fixed income market analysis or related occupation. Three (3) years of experience must include: Utilizing fixed income market and credit instruments or investment indices; Preparing and delivering presentations to clients regarding financial market and investment products; Conducting quantitative analysis of financial datasets; Using data analytics, visualization, and presentation tools; Utilizing financial data/information software.

 The anticipated base salary range for this position is $160,000 to $175,650 . Final base salary for this role will be based on the individual’s geographic location, as well as experience level, skill set, training, licenses & certifications. In addition to base compensation, this role is eligible for an annual incentive plan. S&P OpCo, LLC is part of S&P Global and this role is eligible to receive additional S&P Global benefits.  About S&P Global Dow Jones Indic e s   
 At S&P Dow Jones Indices, we provide iconic and innovative index solutions backed by unparalleled expertise across the asset-class spectrum. By bringing transparency to the global capital markets, we empower investors everywhere to make decisions with conviction. We’re the largest global resource for index-based concepts, data and research, and home to iconic financial market indicators, such as the S&P 500 ® and the Dow Jones Industrial Average ® . More assets are invested in products based upon our indices than any other index provider in the world. With over USD 7.4 trillion in passively managed assets linked to our indices and over USD 11.3 trillion benchmarked to our indices, our solutions are widely considered indispensable in tracking market performance, evaluating portfolios and developing investment strategies.   
 S&P Dow Jones Indices is a division of S&P Global (NYSE: SPGI). S&P Global is the world’s foremost provider of credit ratings, benchmarks, analytics and workflow solutions in the global capital, commodity and automotive markets. With every one of our offerings, we help many of the world’s leading organizations navigate the economic landscape so they can plan for tomorrow, today.
