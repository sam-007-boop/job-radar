WHAT YOU'LL DO

Sierra closes large, complex enterprise and public sector deals, and RFx responses (i.e. RFIs, RFQs, and RFPs) are an increasingly critical part of that process. This role writes and runs Sierra's public sector RFx responses from opportunity to final submission, adds capacity to the broader RFP team, and owns day-to-day execution with an emphasis on accuracy, speed, and quality.

 - Write and shape long-form responses: Draft and edit the narrative and technical sections public sector solicitations often require, explaining how Sierra's AI agents work, how they are governed, and the outcomes they deliver, in the buyer's own language

 - Own Sierra’s public sector RFx response: Identify and triage incoming RFx opportunities, build the response plan and timeline, coordinate with internal stakeholders, and own the deliverable through final submission

 - Drive cross-functional coordination: Partner with subject-matter experts (SMEs) in Security, Legal, Pricing, and Product to get the right answers fast, managing competing stakeholder priorities without losing momentum or quality

 - Own the content infrastructure: Maintain and continuously improve Sierra's knowledge base, identifying gaps, working with SMEs to close them, and raising the quality bar on every response

 - Flex across the RFP team: Support commercial RFPs and security questionnaires as volume demands, so the team can scale across deal types

 - Build toward automation: Implement and iterate on AI tooling and workflow automation to reduce manual work and increase consistency across the response process

 - Support Sales in high-stakes evaluations: Help AEs and the public sector team navigate complex solicitation requirements, decide which bids to pursue, and flag patterns in agency questions that inform how Sierra positions itself in government
