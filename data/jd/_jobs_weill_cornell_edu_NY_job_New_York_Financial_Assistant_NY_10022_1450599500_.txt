 Title: 

 Financial Assistant

 Title: Financial Assistant  

 Location: Midtown  

 Org Unit: Finance 

 Work Days:  

 Weekly Hours: 35.00 

 Exemption Status: Non-Exempt

 Salary Range: $28.02 - $30.77  

 *As required under NYC Human Rights Law Int 1208-2018 - Salary range for this role when Hired for NYC Offices 
 Position Summary
 Under general supervision, provides financial support to the Department Job Responsibilities
 - Responsible for procurement activities related to goods, services, and/or equipment as needed.
- Responsible for disbursement/reimbursement activities as needed.
- Maintains electronic and hard copy files and databases. Maintains integrity of information.
- Processes equipment service contracts and equipment leases and monitors for timely payment and renewals.
- Tracks income and expense items.
- Assists with the annual budget process.
- Prepares and analyzes routine and ad-hoc financial reports for any discrepancies. Compiles summaries as needed. Escalates said reports or analysis for review if necessary.
 Education
 - High School Diploma
 Experience
 Approximately 2 years of related experience Knowledge, Skills and Abilities
 - Advanced problem solving and analytical skills.
- Excellent communication skills (both verbal and written).
- Demonstrated organizational skills and ability to pay close attention to detail.
 Licenses and Certifications
 Working Conditions/Physical Demands
 Standard office work
