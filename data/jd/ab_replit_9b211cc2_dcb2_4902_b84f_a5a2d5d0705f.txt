Replit — Business Operations Analyst
https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/replit/9b211cc2-dcb2-4902-b84f-a5a2d5d0705f

Replit is the agentic software creation platform that enables anyone to build applications using natural language. With millions of users worldwide, Replit is democratizing software development by removing traditional barriers to application creation.

RESPONSIBILITIES

 - Drive end-to-end analysis of product data, market research, and competitor analysis to help drive understanding of key business lines and future company planning. 

 - Work cross-functionally with senior leadership and product teams to identify, assess, and execute key opportunities. 

 - Scope and execute zero-to-one projects to determine long-term value for the company. 

 - Support new go-to-market initiatives, including product launches and influencer campaigns. 

 - Manage vendor relationships by combining strategic analysis, execution, and operational rigor to help scale business. 

 - Manage and monitor vendor contracts, including both initial procurement and the renewal process.

REQUIREMENTS:

 - Bachelor’s degree, or foreign degree equivalent, in Finance, Business Administration, or a related field.

 - Four (4) years of experience in job offered, Associate Analyst, Associate Consultant, or in a related occupation.

 - Must be authorized to work in the United States. Sponsorship and/or relocation are not available for this position.

ACADEMIC TRAINING OR WORK EXPERIENCE MUST INCLUDE: 

 - Large-scale data analysis using SQL on Google Cloud BigQuery. 

 - Data mining using Braintrust. 

 - Building dashboards and data analysis using Hex with Python. 

 - Advanced Microsoft Excel analytics including pivot table, XLOOKUP, and data modeling. 

 - Regression Modeling. 

 - AI usage analytics and business outcome modeling using Python and SQL.

This is a full-time role that can be held from our Foster City, CA office. The role has an in-office requirement of Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.
