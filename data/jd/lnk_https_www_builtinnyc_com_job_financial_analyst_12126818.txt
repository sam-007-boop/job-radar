 Position Summary: 
 DOHMH has an opening for a Financial Analyst of Close Out Unit and is responsible for conducting fiscal reviews and completing the annual fiscal closeout of delegate agency contracts. The analyst supports accurate reconciliation, timely closeout determinations, and clear communication with internal program staff and external providers. 
 Reports To: Director, Close Out Unit 
 Specifically, the Financial Analyst will: 
 - Perform fiscal review and annual closeout of delegate agency contracts, including analysis of claims, budgets, and supporting documentation.
- Review provider expenditures for allowability, allocability, and compliance with contract requirements and City/DOHMH fiscal policies.
