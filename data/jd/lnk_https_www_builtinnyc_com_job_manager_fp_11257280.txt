 About the Role :   
 The Senior Financial Analyst, FP&A will have a unique opportunity to join a high performing FP&A team and contribute to the growth of Dragos by providing analytical insights, decision support, financial reporting, as well as acting as a business partner to operational groups.  Reporting to the Senior FP&A Manager, the Senior Financial Analyst will work closely with Accounting, Revenue Operations, People Team, and department leaders across the company.
 Responsibilities :   
 - Manage headcount planning, tracking, and analysis at a company-wide level
- Support the development of the Annual Operating Plan (AOP) and rolling six-quarter forecasts for operating expenses and headcount; and variance analysis against plan and forecasts
- Facilitate financial reviews with business partners to understand their budgets, provide decision support and budget-to-actual feedback, ensure effective cost management, and maintain financial discipline
- Develop an in-depth understanding of key business drivers to incorporate into monthly commentaries
- Support sales/revenue forecasting, metrics, reporting and analysis
- Perform data-driven analyses to monitor business operations; provide management with clear and actionable insights into drivers of business growth, risks and opportunities
- Proactively engage with cross-functional teams to develop continuous process improvements to planning, analyses, and reporting
- Provide analytical support and insight to operational leadership across the company
- Perform ad hoc financial analysis to support strategic initiatives and business decisions across the organization
 Qualifications :   
 - 3-5 years of experience in FP&A, corporate finance or a similar analytical/reporting role 
- Strong Excel user with proficiency in advanced data/financial operations
- Experience with NetSuite and forecasting/budgeting tools such as Vena or Adaptive
- Experience with CRM & analytical tools, Salesforce strongly preferred; Tableau, Power BI, or other cloud-based platforms a plus
- Experience in budgeting, forecasting, and variance analysis processes
- Hands-on experience in supporting a company-wide financial planning process, including department-level operating budgets, headcount plans, and management reports 
- Proven ability to prepare clear, concise reports, presentations and analysis for executive leadership, translating complex financial data into actionable insights
- Strong financial and business acumen to see and communicate the “what” and “why” behind the numbers, and ability to connect the dots between operational metrics and financial results
- General knowledge of GAAP and an understanding of the difference between accrual and cash accounting
- Exceptional attention to detail, problem-solving and critical thinking skills
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills and ability to establish strong relationships and collaborate with stakeholders across the organization
- Ability to thrive in a rapidly changing environment and solve complex problems
