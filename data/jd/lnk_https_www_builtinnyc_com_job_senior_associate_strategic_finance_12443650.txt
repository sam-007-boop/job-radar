 How This Role Makes a Difference As a Senior Associate, Strategic Finance at Care Access, you will help drive financial, operational, and strategic decisions that support our mission to make a difference in people’s lives and improve the health of future generations. You will work with a growing, globally distributed team and gain exposure to senior leadership across the business. 
 This is a high-impact, Individual Contributor role spanning Corporate Finance & FP&A, Strategic Finance, Corporate Development, and Investor Relations & Capital Markets. You will build and own rigorous financial models, evaluate corporate development opportunities, translate complex analysis into clear recommendations, and support recurring planning processes and high-priority strategic initiatives. 
 How You'll Make An Impact - Corporate Finance & FP&A : Own end-to-end budgeting, forecasting, long-range planning, and financial reporting. Build and maintain complex, bottoms-up financial models; analyze key business drivers, unit economics, and results versus plan; partner with functional leaders on resource allocation and operating decisions. 
- Strategic Finance : Evaluate growth initiatives, investment opportunities, pricing, and business model decisions through research, quantitative analysis, qualitative analyses, and scenario analysis. Distill complex findings into clear, actionable recommendations and executive-ready memos and presentations. 
- Corporate Development : Support evaluation and execution of corporate development opportunities including acquisitions, strategic partnerships, and other transactions; perform market research, valuation and financial analyses, due diligence, and develop supporting investment materials. 
- Investor Relations & Capital Markets : Support the creation of board and investor materials. Perform financial analyses, write commentary and performance narratives, and respond to information requests. Assist with financing or capital markets transactions as applicable. 
- Finance Infrastructure & Cross-Functional Initiatives : Partner with Accounting and Cross-Functional teams to improve financial infrastructure, data quality, reporting, controls, and governance. Build and improve Finance and Accounting processes and tools. 
- Ad-Hoc Analysis & Reporting : Own and execute ad-hoc financial modeling, scenario analysis, and repeatable monthly reporting packages as required by Finance and Cross-functional leadership. 
 The Expertise Required   - Exceptional financial modeling and analytical capabilities with advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel 
- Strong quantitative and qualitative analytical skills, with the ability to structure complex problems, develop insights, and formulate actionable recommendations 
- Intellectual curiosity, sound business judgment, and a demonstrated ability to think critically and independently 
- Exceptional attention to detail, commitment to accuracy, and ability to produce high-quality work under tight deadlines 
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to synthesize complex analyses into clear, compelling materials for senior executives and other key stakeholders 
- Strong ownership mentality, proactive approach to problem-solving, and ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced, dynamic environment 
- Ability to collaborate effectively across functions, build relationships with stakeholders, and translate business priorities into financial implications 
- Evidence of building scalable processes, systems, frameworks, or automated workflows 
- Demonstrated proficiency with AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) to accelerate research, analysis, financial modeling and reporting, and problem-solving, while exercising appropriate judgment and maintaining data confidentiality 
- Proactive mindset toward leveraging AI, automation, and technology to improve processes, streamline workflows, increase efficiency, enhance analytical capabilities, and support decision-making 
- Experience in a high-growth company and/or the healthcare industry is a plus 
 Certifications/Licenses, Education, and Experience - Minimum Education:
 - Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Engineering, or a related quantitative field   

- Minimum Experience:
 - 1 – 3+ years of experience in investment banking, private equity, venture capital, hedge funds, management consulting, strategic finance, or a similarly rigorous analytical environment
