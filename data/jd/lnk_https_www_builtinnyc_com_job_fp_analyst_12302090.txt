 Job Description: We are seeking an analytical and strategic FP&A professional to build and scale Quartermaster’s operational financial planning function from the ground up. This role is critical to connecting our hardware deployment, logistics, and SaaS drivers directly to financial outcomes, enabling executive leadership to optimize unit economics and allocate capital effectively as we expand globally. The ideal candidate thrives in fast-paced, ambiguous startup environments and brings deep financial modeling expertise across both hardware and recurring software business models. You will partner closely with the Head of Finance, Head of Operations, Accounting, and department heads across the organization.
 Key Responsibilities 
 - Drive the annual budgeting process, quarterly reforecasting cycles, and monthly variance analyses (actual vs. budget vs. forecast), partnering with department heads to deliver actionable insights and root-cause recommendations.

- Develop and maintain integrated three-statement and operational financial models connecting revenue drivers, headcount, hardware deployment costs, fulfillment logistics, and cash flow projections.

- Partner with Operations leadership to define, track, and optimize hardware unit economics—including COGS breakdowns (sensor hardware, installation, satellite communications), contribution margins, and executive KPI dashboards.

- Build business cases, sensitivity analyses, and scenario models for strategic initiatives such as new market expansion, pricing strategies, capital investments, and fundraising/investor data rooms.

- Prepare monthly Board reporting packages and executive presentations while designing scalable FP&A processes, tools, and close-to-reporting workflows in partnership with Accounting.

 Qualifications (Preferred) 
 - Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or a quantitative field (MBA, CFA, or CPA designation is a plus).

- 4–8 years of progressive FP&A experience in high-growth, venture-backed, or operationally complex businesses (hardware, supply chain, logistics, or SaaS experience strongly preferred).

- Expert-level Excel/Google Sheets modeling skills (complex formulas, scenario and sensitivity analysis), with hands-on experience in modern FP&A platforms, BI tools, and SQL, Python, or R.

- Proven track record of building financial models and FP&A processes from scratch, partnering effectively with operational leaders, and distilling complex datasets into clear executive and Board-level narratives.

- Exposure to international multi-currency operations, government contracting, defense dynamics, or maritime/security sectors is a plus.

 Work Environment 
 - Flexible working hours, with occasional intensive sprints aligned to monthly close, Board reporting, and annual planning cycles.

- Remote-friendly or hybrid collaboration with direct visibility and regular interaction with the Head of Finance, Head of Operations, and executive leadership.

- High-impact ownership opportunity to build the operational FP&A function from the ground up, with clear growth pathways into senior FP&A or operational leadership as the team scales.
