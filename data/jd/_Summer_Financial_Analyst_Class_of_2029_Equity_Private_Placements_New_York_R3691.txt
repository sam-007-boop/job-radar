 Equity Private Placements 
Our Equity Private Placements practice, a dedicated team within the Equity Capital Solutions vertical of the broader Capital Solutions Group, originates, structures, and executes private financings. Members of the Equity Private Placements team work closely with a range of sponsor-backed, family-owned, public, and privately-owned clients, as well as with Houlihan Lokey’s industry coverage, M&A, and financial restructuring teams.

The Equity Private Placements team executes on a variety of private financing transactions, such as growth equity, structured equity, Private Investments in Public Equities (PIPEs), co-invest equity, and other minority equity placements for a variety of situations across industries. Use of proceeds for these transactions include growth capital, acquisition financings, refinancings, dividend recapitalizations, leveraged buyouts (LBOs), and special situations.

 In 2024, Houlihan Lokey completed more equity private placements than any other non-balance sheet bank.

The Equity Private Placements team is seeking to recruit a Summer Analyst to join the team in New York, New York for the Summer of 2028. The Summer Analyst will be responsible for supporting Analysts, Associates, and senior bankers on the day-to-day aspects of equity private placement transaction and marketing efforts, as well as other Capital Solutions projects.

 Equity Private Placements transactions involve similar stages to M&A deals, e.g. creating marketing materials (CIMs, three-statement financial models), spearheading counterparty diligence, coordinating management meetings, and ultimately structuring/negotiating the financing to close.

 You will:

- Work across various industries/sectors and business models;
- Assist with evaluating and structuring customized financing alternatives for a range of clients, including modeling, preparing marketing materials, and preparing structuring analysis, among other activities;
- Gain exposure to a variety of investors, including venture capital funds, growth equity funds, private equity funds, hybrid capital funds, co-investment funds, insurance companies, hedge funds, asset managers, and family offices, as well as growth / venture debt providers;
- Gain hands-on transaction experience by playing a role on deal teams, with activities and workstreams ranging from initial pitches through closings; and
- Broaden your corporate finance and valuation skills relevant to raising capital.

 The environment at Houlihan Lokey is collegial and entrepreneurial, and, as such, rewards our Analysts with substantial responsibility and interaction with senior-level professionals.
