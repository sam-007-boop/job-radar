 The Role: We are seeking an experienced and highly motivated Senior FP&A Analyst to join our dynamic team. This role involves managing the operating plan, supporting strategic decision making, and working cross-functionally on key initiatives that will help shape the future of a $1 billion Series D company backed by Insight Partners.

 What You’ll Do: - Annual operating plan : Build and update the company’s driver based long term financial model, leveraging your strong understanding of key financial drivers to form the company’s operating plan in a way that connects objectives and drives accountability across GTM functions to product planning.

- Company-wide budgeting and forecasting : Provide department leaders with ongoing financial insights, analysis, and partnership on decisions to accelerate growth. Additionally, this role would own the management of Glia’s financial planning tool

- Company-wide financial reporting : Help prepare materials and narratives for Board of Director meetings, a polished executive level financial reporting package and internal executive meetings

 Requirements: - 2+ years of prior experience in SaaS FP&A (or investment banking / consulting, etc)

- Strategic and creative problem solver ready to roll up their sleeves

- Ability to work in a fast-paced environment, managing competing priorities and deadlines

- Strong attention to detail coupled with an ability to see the bigger picture

- Excellent communication, interpersonal and analytical skills

- Advanced Excel skills and experience working with NetSuite, Salesforce, and Tableau

- Experience using and managing a company’s financial planning software platform like Adaptive Insights, Pigment or Vena

- Extensive experience collecting, analyzing, and interpreting large volumes of data

- Strong understanding of P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow (There will be a modeling test)
