Job Summary 
 ERM is seeking a motivated Environmental Modeller who is interested in participating on a variety of projects covering the full spectrum of ERM’s services. The role will be based in Bangkok, Thailand.

 This is an excellent career opportunity to work with an expert global consulting company on challenging environmental projects for large industrial and global clients. Access to ERM’s worldwide network of EHS experts provides the knowledge sharing of best practices across the industry and ongoing learning opportunities for everyone.

 Why This Role Matter:
Building Sustainable Capital Projects for Asia Pacific’s Future 
Asia Pacific’s rapid growth in renewable energy, mining and infrastructure demands responsible development. ERM’s Capital Project Delivery Team leads this transformation, helping organizations design, permit, and operate projects that align with global sustainability standards and local expectations. From impact assessments to biodiversity and resettlement planning, this role ensures projects across region are investable, resilient, and ethical. Your expertise will shape initiatives that balance progress with purpose, creating a sustainable legacy for industries driving the region’s future. 

 ROLE PROFILE: 
 - Lead the environmental modelling scopes, including providing technical inputs to the design of baseline surveys and modelling approaches, coordinating and supervising fieldwork, developing and running models, drafting technical reports and sections, and providing technical advice support to baseline, environmental baseline reports, international environmental impact assessment reports and management plans;
- Apply technical knowledge in baseline environmental data collection, modelling, reporting and impact assessment and foster technical growth of junior members of the environment team;
- Undertake field trips within Thailand and other countries across APAC where needed.
- Perform site visits or inspections and supervise subcontractors as and when required for projects. Focus on preparing international environmental impact assessment and management plans.
- Provide environmental support, as and when needed, by undertaking research and data analysis for environmental and social managers within the broader Capital Project Delivery - Impact Assessment team.
- Work with ERM Thailand office and other ERM offices in the world to develop our environmental modelling teams.

 QUALIFICATIONS: 
 - Master’s degree in a relevant environmental, scientific or engineering subject.
- Minimum 2-5 years of experience in a relevant environmental modelling field of expertise.
- Fluent in English and Thai. The ability to communicate clearly and inclusively with colleagues, clients and stakeholders, both verbally and in writing.
- Ability to use Microsoft Word and Excel to an advanced level.
- Hands-on experience with one or more modelling tools
