 Mission 
 Databricks is seeking a Manager to join our Corporate FP&A team. In this role, you will report to the Director of Corporate FP&A and help empower the broader FP&A organization. You will play a key role in driving cash forecasting processes and translating complex financial data into clear reports. You’ll partner with leaders across functions to support cash management.  
 The impact you will have 

 Drive the cash flow forecast. Maintain the company's direct and indirect cash flow forecasts through each monthly forecast cycle, annual plan, and long-range plan. Bring together inputs on collections, payroll, vendor payments, commissions, taxes, capital expenditures, and cloud infrastructure spend into one company-wide view. 
 Lead quarter-end cash landing. Run the intra-quarter process and work with cross-functional finance teams to minimize free cash flow variance.  
 Report the metrics that matter. Manage the key metrics of cash flow for monthly business reviews, quarterly CFO reviews, and Board materials.  
 Capital timing. Partner closely with our treasury and strategic teams to coordinate our capital, fundraising, and debt forecasts. 
 Automate and scale. Use Databricks, SQL, and AI tools to automate cash positioning, cash landing, and cash flow reporting, and help guide our planning processes through upcoming finance system changes. 

 What we look for 

 Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or a related quantitative field 
 6-8+ years of experience in FP&A, Corporate Finance, or a related field (such as banking or accounting), ideally at a technology, SaaS, or consumption-based company 
 Hands-on experience building and owning direct and indirect cash flow forecasts 
 Strong understanding of the three financial statements and how billings, collections, payables, payroll, and capital spending flow through to cash 
 Experience working with Treasury on cash, liquidity, and interest forecasting 
 Strong financial modeling skills in Excel and Google Sheets, with experience in a planning tool such as Anaplan 
 Experience using, or a strong interest in using, SQL, dashboards, and AI tools to automate recurring finance work 
 Familiarity with ERP and finance systems such as SAP and NetSuite is a plus. Experience with Databricks is preferred. 
 Clear written and verbal communication, including turning complex analysis into simple takeaways for executives 
 Calm, organized execution under tight quarter-end deadlines, and the confidence to raise risks early and hold partners accountable for their inputs
