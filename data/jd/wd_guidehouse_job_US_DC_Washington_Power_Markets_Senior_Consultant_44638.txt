 What You Will Do: 

 Guidehouse is currently seeking an energy professional to join our Federal Civilian Agencies (FCA) Energy & Environment market team supporting the Department of Energy, Department of the Interior, Environmental Protection Agency, and National Science Foundation.
 Incoming Senior Consultants will support client leadership through data-driven analysis of energy-sector trends, infrastructure challenges, market dynamics, and policy considerations. Candidates will utilize modern analytical platforms and quantitative methods to develop insights, answer complex questions, and support strategic decision-making. The ideal candidate should possess knowledge of electricity markets and energy systems, experience working with energy-related datasets, and comfort engaging directly with senior federal stakeholders.

 - Conduct quantitative analyses related to energy systems, infrastructure, markets, and policy issues to support client decision-making and program objectives. 
- Research emerging issues affecting the energy sector and evaluate their implications for programs, investments, and strategic initiatives. 
- Develop analytical models and assessments that identify trends, evaluate outcomes, and inform actionable recommendations.
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams to solve complex energy-sector challenges using quantitative, policy, and operational perspectives. 
- Manage multiple concurrent analytical efforts by working with stakeholders to establish priorities, define methodologies, evaluate assumptions and constraints, and communicate timelines and findings. 
- Leverage modern data platforms, analytical technologies, and automation techniques to efficiently collect, manage, analyze, and interpret large and complex datasets.
- Prepare technical memoranda, presentations, analytical products, and other client deliverables that communicate complex findings to technical and non-technical audiences. 
- Brief senior client leadership on analytical results, emerging trends, strategic considerations, and recommendations.

 What You Will Need: 

 - Must be able to OBTAIN and MAINTAIN a Federal or DoD "PUBLIC TRUST"; candidates must obtain approved adjudication of their PUBLIC TRUST prior to onboarding with Guidehouse. Candidates with an ACTIVE PUBLIC TRUST or SUITABILITY are preferred.
- Must be a US Citizen due to nature of client engagements.
- Bachelor’s degree from an accredited college/university.
- Three (3) to Five(5) years of relevant professional experience in energy-sector analysis, quantitative modeling, economics, policy analysis, program evaluation, or related fields.
- Knowledge of energy systems, infrastructure, markets, policy, or other topics relevant to the energy sector.
- Experience applying quantitative methods and analytical frameworks to solve complex business, policy, or operational challenges.
- Demonstrated experience leveraging modern analytical tools, programming languages, and data platforms to develop models, evaluate data, and generate actionable insights from large and complex datasets.
- Strong analytical, written, and verbal communication skills. Strong attention to detail, well organized, and able to self-prioritize multiple activities.
- Ability to communicate complex energy concepts and analytical findings to executive-level leadership in a clear and concise manner.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Word, PowerPoint, and Excel.
- This hybrid position requires up to 3-5 days at the Client's office in Washington, DC. 
- This hybrid position is open to candidates who will reside within 50 miles of Washington, DC.
