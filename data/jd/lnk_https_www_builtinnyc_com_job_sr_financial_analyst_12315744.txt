 Your Mission We’re hiring a Senior Analyst to own the analytics engine behind GTM finance: the bookings forecasts that drive revenue planning, channel and team targets, and investment decisions. You’ll work directly with GTM finance leadership and partner with Sales, Marketing, Brand and Account Management. You’ll also automate the monthly close and forecast cycle so that accurate, decision-ready numbers come out faster and with less manual work.
 What You’ll Do - Act as the lead analyst for GTM finance, scoping open-ended questions on bookings, GBV, consumption, and channel performance, and turning them into rigorous, data-backed answers.
- Own and evolve the GTM forecasting models. Build driver-based scenarios covering new logos, expansion and NDR, and cohort ramp.
- Explain how cohorts convert and where the gaps are.
- Automate recurring work: month-end actualization, forecast-versus-actual variance, consumption by channel and concentrated account, and anomaly detection. Aim for a model that refreshes at month end with minimal manual intervention powered by AI.
- Build and maintain GTM dashboards so leaders have current visibility into bookings, GBV, pipeline conversion, and capacity.
- Use advanced SQL and statistical methods (regression, time-series forecasting, driver analysis) to improve forecast accuracy.
- Go beyond the numbers to explain the “why” behind GTM performance, quantify opportunities, and make clear recommendations for sales and finance leadership.
- Support the annual planning cycle, including target-setting, top-down and bottom-up reconciliation, and stretch and downside scenarios for executive and board review.
- Partner with Data Engineering on definitions, data quality, and reusable tables, so that GTM metrics are consistent across teams.
 What We’re Looking For - Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Statistics, Data Science, Operations Research, or a related quantitative field.
- 5+ years in strategic finance, FP&A, GTM or sales finance, business analytics, or consulting, ideally in high-growth SaaS or marketplace businesses.
- Advanced SQL and hands-on experience building data models or pipelines. 
- Strong grounding in statistics and forecasting, including regression, time-series methods, and scenario modeling.
- Practical experience using AI tools and automation to speed up analysis and remove manual work. 
- Solid financial literacy: P&L structure, unit economics (CAC, LTV, payback, NDR), bookings-versus-revenue timing, and how GTM investment connects to outcomes.
- Clear communicator who can turn complex models into a crisp narrative for sales leaders and the executive team.
- Comfort operating with ambiguity, ownership of the full workflow from data pull to recommendation, and a willingness to challenge assumptions.
 Why Join Us - Be the analytics partner to GTM finance leadership, and shape how we set targets, invest in go-to-market, and allocate capacity.
- Build an AI-enabled, automated forecasting foundation that scales with the business.
- Join a data-driven team that values ownership, speed, and real impact
