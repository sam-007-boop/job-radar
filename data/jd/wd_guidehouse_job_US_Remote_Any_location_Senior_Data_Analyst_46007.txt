 We are seeking a Data Analyst to support the NIH DataForce Service Program. This role will maintain program and workforce data, develop trackers and reports, monitor participant progress, and provide insights that help NIH manage recruitment, onboarding, and participant lifecycle activities.
 The Data Analyst will collaborate with program managers, coordinators, mentors, and Government stakeholders to ensure program information is accurate, current, and useful for decision-making.
 What You Will Do 
 - Collect, organize, validate, and analyze recruitment, onboarding, participant, workforce, and program performance data. 
- Maintain participant lifecycle trackers covering application status, onboarding, mentor assignments, training, milestones, deliverables, and administrative actions. 
- Monitor onboarding requirements, including background investigations, credentialing, badging, required training, and system access. 
- Track recruitment activities, application processing, interviews, mentor matching, and selection coordination. 
- Develop dashboards, charts, reports, and briefing materials that clearly communicate program status and trends. 
- Prepare monthly program operations and performance reports. 
- Support quarterly analyses of participant progression, workforce utilization, mentor-recommended task alignment, and program outcomes. 
- Identify missing information, inconsistent records, delayed activities, operational risks, and opportunities for process improvement. 
- Maintain data dictionaries, metric definitions, reporting templates, and data-quality procedures. 
- Support documentation of recruitment, onboarding, selection, and participant lifecycle workflows. 
- Respond to requests for program and workforce information using verified data. 
- Protect sensitive applicant and participant information in accordance with applicable Federal and NIH requirements. 
- Use Government-approved tools and platforms, including Microsoft Excel, Power BI, SharePoint, and Teams. 
- Ensure reports, dashboards, and other electronic products meet applicable Section 508 accessibility requirements. 
 What You Will Need 
 - Bachelor’s degree 
- Minimum of FOUR (4) years of professional experience 
- Experience in data analysis, program analysis, workforce analytics, performance measurement, program evaluation, or operations reporting is highly preferred. 

 What Would Be Nice to Have 
 - Advanced Microsoft Excel skills, including formulas, pivot tables, lookup functions, charts, and data validation. 
- Experience developing dashboards and reports using Microsoft Power BI, Tableau, or a similar business-intelligence tool. 
- Experience collecting, cleaning, reconciling, and analyzing information from multiple sources.
- Experience developing and maintaining operational trackers, performance measures, or management reports. 
- Ability to identify data-quality issues, operational trends, risks, and reporting gaps. 
- Strong written and verbal communication skills. 
- Ability to explain data and analytical findings to technical and nontechnical audiences. 
- Strong attention to detail and the ability to manage recurring monthly and quarterly deliverables. 
- Experience handling sensitive workforce, applicant, participant, or program information. 
- Ability to collaborate with program managers, coordinators, mentors, and Government stakeholders. 
- Proficiency with Microsoft SharePoint, Teams, and other Microsoft 365 tools. 
- Bachelor’s degree data analytics, statistics, business analytics, information systems, public health, economics, mathematics, computer science, program evaluation, or a related field. 
- Master’s degree in a quantitative, analytical, public health, information systems, or program-evaluation field. 
- Experience supporting NIH, HHS, or another Federal agency. 
- Experience supporting a fellowship, internship, recruitment, workforce development, training, or rotational program. 
- Experience analyzing recruitment pipelines, onboarding completion, participant progression, workforce utilization, or program outcomes. 
- Experience using Power Query, Microsoft Lists, Power Pivot, or Microsoft Forms. 
- Working knowledge of SQL, Python, or R for data preparation, analysis, automation, or visualization. 
- Experience conducting trend analysis, forecasting, root-cause analysis, or process-performance analysis. 
- Familiarity with Federal privacy, security, records-management, and data-governance requirements. 
- Experience developing Section 508-compliant reports, dashboards, and presentation materials. 
 What Success Looks Like 
 - Program and participant records are accurate, complete, and current. 
- Recruitment, onboarding, milestone, and workforce trends are clearly communicated. 
- Monthly and quarterly reports are delivered on time and supported by validated data. 
- Data definitions and calculations are applied consistently across reports and dashboards. 
- Missing information, delayed activities, and operational risks are identified early. 
- Program leaders receive clear, reliable information to support Government decision-making. 
- Sensitive applicant and participant information is handled appropriately. 
- Analytical findings lead to practical improvements in program operations and reporting.
