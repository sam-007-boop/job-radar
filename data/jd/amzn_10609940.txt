Amazon Health Services (AHS) is reimagining how customers access and experience healthcare — making it easier, more affordable, and more convenient. AHS spans Amazon Pharmacy, One Medical, and a growing portfolio of services that put customers first across primary care, pharmacy, and specialty needs. Within AHS, Amazon Pharmacy is building the most convenient, transparent, and lovable pharmacy experience in the world — and we're still early. We are growing rapidly, serving millions of customers, expanding partnerships across healthcare, and reinventing what it means to get medications delivered to your door with speed, simplicity, and clinical quality.

Amazon Pharmacy Finance is the strategic finance partner to this business. We sit at the intersection of strategy, operations, and execution, focused on driving sustainable growth, advising senior leadership on high judgment decisions, and building the financial foundation for Pharmacy's growth. Our team partners across Pharmacy, AHS, Operations, WW Stores, and broader Amazon. We are building an AI-native finance team, using generative AI, agentic workflows, and automation to transform how we plan, forecast, analyze, drive controllership, and communicate.

We are looking for a strategic, operationally-minded Finance professional to join our Pharmacy Operations Finance team and help steward our growth in this complex, fast-growing space. The ideal candidate thrives in ambiguity and challenges the status quo. This role offers advancement opportunities to support different businesses and cross-functional teams, lead new programs and initiatives, and manage larger teams across the company.

Key job responsibilities
- Own CapEx financials — lead planning, depreciation, and lease forecasting across the Amazon Pharmacy network, including quarterly guidance, operating plan, and long-range planning cycles
- Build and maintain investment models — develop NPV/ROI analyses for site launches, building retrofits, and automation investments in partnership with Engineering
- Steward the capital allocation request (CAR) process — audit capital requests, maintain a consolidated CapEx tracker, and draft leadership approval communications
- Inform network expansion decisions — partner with network topology, demand forecasting, and transportation teams to determine where and when to launch pharmacy sites, aligning capital investment with operational strategy
- Leverage AI tools — accelerate modeling, reporting, and financial workflows using available AI capabilities

A day in the life
The successful candidate influences business owners, supports thoughtful decision-making, and adapts quickly in a rapidly evolving environment. They collaborate with executives and business units across Amazon. A typical day offers the opportunity to identify high-priority work supporting Pharmacy Operations, solve complex problems that shape strategic decisions, and drive innovation.

This is a highly visible role within the finance team and requires a strong leader and open communicator.
