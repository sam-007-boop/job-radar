 The Opportunity  
 We are seeking a Senior Project Finance Analyst to support the structuring, analysis, and execution of financing for the Company’s renewable energy assets under development and construction. This role will focus on financial modeling, capital structure analysis, diligence coordination, and transaction execution across a variety of financing solutions, including senior construction and term debt, tax equity, mezzanine and HoldCo facilities, and equipment financing. 

 Working closely with senior leadership and execution teams, the Senior Analyst will evaluate capital requirements, develop and maintain detailed financial models, support capital markets engagement, and assist in managing transactions from diligence through closing. This role is ideal for a motivated renewable energy finance professional who wants to deepen technical expertise and gain broad exposure to complex capital structures in a fast-paced environment.
 This is an on-site role requiring five days per week in the office. Candidates must be based in New York or Denver, or be willing to relocate as required. Applicants with experience in the solar industry are highly preferred.
 What You'll Do  
 Capital Structure & Financial Analysis 

 - Build, maintain, and audit detailed project-level and portfolio-level financial models for renewable energy assets. 
- Analyze and compare capital structure alternatives across debt, tax equity, mezzanine, and HoldCo financing. 
- Perform detailed quantitative analysis, including discounted cash flow modeling, sensitivity analysis, and scenario analysis, to assess risk, return, and capital structure optimization. 
- Analyze debt sizing, covenant compliance (including DSCR), draw mechanisms, and refinancing or term-out structures. 
 Transaction Support & Execution 
 - Support preparation of marketing materials, CIMs, and lender/investor presentations. 
- Support capital markets transactions by managing due diligence processes, financial data requests, and investor/lender communications .
- Help manage transaction timelines, deliverables, and closing checklists. 
- Coordinate with legal counsel and third-party advisors to track documentation and diligence items. 
 Cross-Functional Coordination 
 - Work closely with development, EPC, and asset management teams to understand project schedules, budgets, and key technical inputs. 
- Coordinate with portfolio managers on project timing, milestones, and financing readiness. 
- Assist in translating financial results, risks, and sensitivities into clear materials for internal review. 
- Support ongoing facility management, including compliance tracking, draw requests, and reporting. 
- Assist in maintaining organized data rooms and transaction documentation. 
- Help monitor post-closing obligations, amendments, and future financings.

 What We're Looking For  
 - Strong financial modeling skills. 
- Bachelor’s degree in finance, economics, business administration, or a closely related field with coursework in financial modeling, corporate finance, and quantitative analysis required. 
- 2–4 years of experience in investment banking, project finance, private equity, infrastructure finance, or a related financial field requiring advanced financial analysis. 
- Demonstrated expertise in financial modeling, including project finance and cash flow analysis. 
- Knowledge of debt structures, tax equity financing, and capital markets transactions. 
- Highly detail-oriented with a strong execution mindset. 
- Comfortable managing multiple active transactions in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment. 
- Strong analytical and quantitative abilities. 
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills. 
- Self-motivated, organized, and eager to learn. 
- Collaborative, humble, and accountable team player.
