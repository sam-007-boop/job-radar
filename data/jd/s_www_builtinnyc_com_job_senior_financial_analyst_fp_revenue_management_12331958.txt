 Edelman is looking for a Senior Finance Analyst to join our Commercial Finance team. This is an exciting opportunity for a finance professional who enjoys working at the intersection of financial analysis, business operations, and client partnership.

 In this role, you'll support financial planning, revenue forecasting, billing, and reporting across multiple client accounts, working closely with finance leadership and account teams to drive financial accuracy and informed business decisions. You'll also have the opportunity to take ownership of client financial management and grow your responsibilities over time.

 If you're a detail-oriented, collaborative finance professional ready to take the next step in your career, we'd love to hear from you.

 Who You Are You're an analytical and proactive finance professional who enjoys turning financial data into meaningful insights. You're comfortable managing multiple priorities, collaborating with cross-functional teams, and taking ownership of your work. You bring a solid understanding of financial processes, strong attention to detail, and a willingness to learn and grow in a dynamic agency environment.

 What You'll Do - Support financial planning, budgeting, and revenue forecasting across multiple client accounts, ensuring accurate and timely financial reporting.
- Manage revenue-related activities, including accruals, deferrals, reconciliations, and monthly revenue tracking.
- Partner with finance leadership, project managers, and account teams to monitor project budgets, profitability, and financial performance.
- Coordinate and support high-volume client billing processes, ensuring accuracy, compliance, and timely invoicing.
- Analyze budget-to-actual performance, identify financial trends and variances, and provide actionable insights to stakeholders.
- Support financial operations, including purchase orders, service orders, contract reviews, and project financial documentation.
- Take ownership of assigned client financial activities, with opportunities to independently manage client accounts as you grow in the role.
- Collaborate with colleagues across teams, embracing diverse perspectives and contributing to an inclusive and supportive work environment.

 Basic Qualifications - A bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, or a related field and a minimum of two years of relevant finance or accounting experience; or at least four years of relevant experience in lieu of a degree.
- Experience in financial analysis, budgeting, forecasting, or financial reporting.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and Microsoft Office applications.

 Preferred Qualifications - Experience in commercial finance, FP&A, or financial operations, ideally within an advertising, public relations, communications, or professional services environment.
- Understanding of revenue recognition principles, including accruals, deferrals, and revenue forecasting.
- Experience supporting client billing, reconciliations, project budgets, and profitability analysis.
- Ability to interpret financial information and communicate insights clearly to non-finance stakeholders.
- Strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and ability to manage multiple priorities.
- Experience with financial systems such as Workday, Ariba, or Coupa is a plus.
