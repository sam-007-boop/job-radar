Great financial products shape how millions of people experience their money every day, and at JPMorganChase, you can help build them. 
 As a Consumer Banking Financial Product Analyst, within the Chase Leadership Development Program (Winter Cohort) you'll sit at the intersection of data, strategy, and customer experience, asking the big questions about how our products work, who they work for, and how to make them even better. You'll dig into trends, pressure-test ideas, run experiments, and turn insights into things customers actually use usually a tap away in an app. 
 You'll team up with designers, engineers, marketers, risk partners, and senior product leaders to turn early ideas into real, launched products grounded in strategy, risk, and customer experience. 
 This is a  direct-hire role , not a rotational program. You'll join one team, take real ownership from day one, and grow with a dedicated manager, a program manager and a mentor who's genuinely invested in you.   The learning curriculum is built to sharpen both your business instincts and your technical expertise. It's a focused, hands-on start to your career with the full scale of JPMorganChase behind you. 

 Job Responsibilities 
 - Analyze market data and customer behavior to identify trends and opportunities, supporting the development of product strategies that enhance revenue and customer satisfaction. 
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure seamless product lifecycle management, from initial concept through to implementation and ongoing optimization. 
- Assist in the preparation and execution of controlled tests to validate product hypotheses and inform strategic decisions. 
- Monitor and report on product performance metrics, providing insights that drive improvements in product offerings and customer experiences. 
- Support risk and control management efforts by identifying potential risks and ensuring adherence to regulatory and policy standards across product processes. 

 Required Qualifications, Capabilities, and Skills 
 - Earned a B.A., B.S., or 5th-year M.A. or M.S. degree with a graduation date between December 2024 and December 2026. 
- Entry level experience gained through internships, jobs, or volunteer roles in financial product management, ideally with exposure to lifecycle management, strategic alignment, and/or consulting, advisory, or strategy work. 
- Demonstrate proficiency in analyzing financial data to inform product strategies and drive business outcomes. 
- Experienced in applying structured ideation frameworks to design customer-centric solutions within technical, budget, and time constraints, driving measurable product value. 
- Skilled at leveraging quantitative and qualitative data to inform recommendations and influence stakeholders, achieving alignment on product strategy and prioritization. 
- Experienced in embedding risk management and control frameworks across product lifecycles to ensure regulatory and policy compliance. 

 Preferred Qualifications, Capabilities, and Skills 
 - A genuine interest in financial services, banking, and the brands and products that touch millions of consumers every day. 

- Willingness to embrace change and bring an innovative mindset — curious about emerging technologies (including AI) and how they can elevate effectiveness in the workplace.
