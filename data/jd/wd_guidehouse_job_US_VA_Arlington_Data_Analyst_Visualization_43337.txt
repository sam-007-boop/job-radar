 What You Will Do : 
 Guidehouse is seeking a Data Analyst to join our AI & Data Defense and Security Segment. The individual will provide data analytics, dashboard development, and general analytical support to a federal client team. This role will support the Data Analytics Branch and contribute to both day-to-day analytical needs and longer-term capability development. The ideal candidate will bring a strong mix of technical ability, communication skills, and relationship-building to work effectively with client stakeholders and help mature the partnership across the team.
Responsibilities of this role are as follows, to include but not limited to:
 - Develop, maintain, and improve dashboards, reports, and visualizations that support mission and program needs

- Analyze data from multiple sources to identify trends, patterns, and actionable insights

- Translate stakeholder questions and business needs into practical analytical outputs and reporting solutions

- Support ad hoc and recurring data calls, reporting requests, and analytical tasks across the team

- Work as a generalist across a range of analytics efforts, adapting to evolving priorities and client needs

- Present findings in a clear, concise manner to both technical and non-technical audiences

- Collaborate closely with the Data Analytics Branch and broader team members to support delivery of high-value analytics products

- Contribute to the growth and maturity of the client relationship through strong customer engagement, responsiveness, and professionalism

- Support process improvement related to analytics, reporting workflows, and data-driven decision support

 What You Will Need : 
 - Must be able to OBTAIN and MAINTAIN a Federal or DoD "PUBLIC TRUST"; candidates must obtain approved adjudication of their PUBLIC TRUST prior to onboarding with Guidehouse. Candidates with an ACTIVE PUBLIC TRUST or SUITABILITY are preferred

- Bachelor’s degree

- THREE (3) or more years of experience in data analytics, reporting, and dashboard development

- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to work across varied assignments

- Strong written and verbal communication skills

- Ability to work effectively in a client-facing environment and collaborate with both internal and external stakeholders

 What Would Be Nice To Have : 
 - Active CBP BI or reciprocity is strongly preferred

- Exposure to programming languages such as R, Python, or SAS is welcome, but not required

- THREE (3) to NINE (9) years of professional experience

- Strong preference for Federal experience

- Strong communication skills with the ability for oral and written communication with senior levels of management and the Customer
