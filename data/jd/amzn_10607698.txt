Amazon (incl. AWS data-center finance) — Finance Manager, NACF Central Reporting
https://www.amazon.jobs/en/jobs/10607698/finance-manager-nacf-central-reporting

Are you a finance professional who thrives on turning complex data into actionable business insights? Amazon is looking for a Financial Analyst III to own financial reporting for a business area and partner with leaders to drive informed, data-driven decisions. In this role, you will build financial models, craft compelling narratives, and deliver recommendations that shape both operational and long-term growth strategies. You will work at the intersection of finance and business operations, where your analytical rigor and curiosity will directly influence how we serve our customers and allocate resources. If you are energized by solving ambiguous problems, collaborating across teams, and making a measurable impact, this is the role for you.

Key job responsibilities
- Own financial reporting for your business area, ensuring accuracy and compliance with internal and external policies while mentoring others on reporting standards and best practices.
- Build and maintain financial models and analyses to support pricing, operational, and growth decisions, translating complex data into clear recommendations for senior leadership.
- Identify and resolve data integrity issues, design improved controls to address emerging risks, and develop scalable, auditable data analysis processes that strengthen financial governance.
- Lead cross-functional projects end to end—managing timelines, setting priorities, and collaborating with upstream and downstream teams to deliver results with minimal oversight.
- Identify opportunities to improve, automate, and scale finance tools and processes across teams, leveraging data management techniques and generative AI tools where appropriate to increase efficiency.

A day in the life
You might start your morning reviewing financial reports and preparing a narrative for a leadership review, then shift to a working session with business partners where you challenge assumptions and align on key trade-offs. After lunch, you could be deep in a financial model, stress-testing scenarios for an upcoming initiative. Later, you might connect with a cross-functional team to design a new control process or mentor a colleague on reporting methodology. Throughout it all, you bring curiosity, precision, and a collaborative mindset.

About the team
Our Finance team is focused on delivering insights that help Amazon make smarter, faster decisions on behalf of our customers. We operate in a collaborative environment where analytical thinking, creative problem-solving, and open dialogue are valued. As a member of this team, you will contribute to strategic goals that shape how we grow and invest for the long term. We are committed to building an inclusive culture where every voice is heard, and we actively invest in the professional development of our team members through knowledge sharing, community programs, and mentorship.
