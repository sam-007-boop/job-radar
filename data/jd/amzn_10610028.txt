Amazon (incl. AWS data-center finance) — Senior Financial Analyst, AWS Service FP&A
https://www.amazon.jobs/en/jobs/10610028/senior-financial-analyst-aws-service-fp-a

The AWS Services FP&A team is looking for a seasoned Senior Finance Analyst to be part of a critical, high-growth area of the business. The person in the role will own the processes, analytics, and generate business insights for Services' financial performance. The role requires a self-starter who can exhibit thought leadership, develop processes, communicate with senior executives, and collaborate across teams. In addition to traditional functional Finance skills, this role requires the ability to lead cross-functional teams and exercise influence to coordinate key planning workstreams and projects. This role gives the opportunity to develop a good business understanding of the key AWS services planning and reporting processes. 

Key job responsibilities
- Leading the planning cycles for a multi-billion dollar business.
- Deep-diving variances vs. plan to understand changes and uncover opportunities for efficiency improvement.
- Leading cross-functional projects involving finance and tech teams.
- Setting up processes and automating reporting. 

- 3+ years of finance, accounting, or related analytical field experience
- 3+ years of contributing to cross-functional initiatives that drive financial performance and strategy experience
- 3+ years of identifying incomplete or inaccurate data, identifying the root cause and creating/implementing an escalation plan experience
- Bachelor's degree in business, finance, accounting, tax, economics, or a related analytical field
- Experience supporting month-end closing processes and contributing to financial reporting under established procedures
- Experience in forecasting, budgeting, variance analysis, and interpreting financial results

- 4+ years of participating in continuous improvement projects in your team to scale and improve controllership with measurable results experience
- 2+ years of identifying, leading, and executing opportunities to improve, automate, standardize or simplify finance or business tools and processes experience
- Experience working with large-scale data mining and reporting tools (examples: SQL, MS Access, Essbase, Cognos) and other financial systems (examples: Oracle, SAP, Lawson, JD Edwards)
- Experience in creating process improvements with automation and analysis
- Experience working in a large team or fast-paced corporate environment
- MBA, or CPA
