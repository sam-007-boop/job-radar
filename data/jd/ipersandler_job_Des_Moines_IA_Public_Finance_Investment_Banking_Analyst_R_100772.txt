 At Piper Sandler, we connect capital with opportunity to build a better future.

Our mission is to recruit, develop, and retain high-performing people who are bright, committed, and who work in partnership with others to achieve at a high level.

 We are currently looking for a Public Finance Investment Banking Analyst to join our local government finance team in Des Moines, Iowa.  

 The Des Moines team pursues work assisting Iowa’s governmental bodies with school financing, city financing, county financing and special purpose financings, and has been involved in many of Iowa’s highest profile public projects over the past several decades.  The team is made up of three senior bankers, two junior bankers, an analyst, an intern plus professional administrative support.

 About our Public Finance Group 
 We are a national public finance investment banking franchise with a diversified business focused on middle market issuers and specialty sector expertise. Our core business is partnering with state and local governments, education sector leaders, not-for-profits, and private developers to raise capital for infrastructure projects through the issuance of tax-exempt and taxable bonds. We finance a broad spectrum of projects across our regional footprint and our specialty sector coverage. Learn more about our public finance team here .

 About the Role and Your Career Path 
 As part of the Des Moines collaborative team, you'll gain broad, hands-on exposure across the full lifecycle of municipal finance transactions, including long-term capital planning, initial and periodic update to bond sizing, cash flow modeling, debt profile and rating strategy analysis, and transaction execution, working with peers and senior bankers, including directly with clients.

 We are especially interested in candidates with exceptional technical excellence and the ability to build rapport within the team and with our clients quickly; candidates that can, communicate persuasively, read a room, and turn client interactions into trust and opportunity, and finally, candidates interested in using new technology to more efficiently and effectively serve clients. If you see yourself as part of a high-performing team this role, and this team, is designed for you.
