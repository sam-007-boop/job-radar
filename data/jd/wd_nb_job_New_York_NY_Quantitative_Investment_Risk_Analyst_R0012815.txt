Neuberger Berman — Quantitative Investment Risk Analyst
https://nb.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/NBCareers/job/New-York-NY/Quantitative-Investment-Risk-Analyst_R0012815

Neuberger is an independent, employee-owned, global asset management firm, serving the financial needs of institutional and individual investors worldwide. Our broad capabilities include traditional and alternative equity and fixed income strategies, private equity and commodities, in addition to portfolio advice and wealth planning services. We offer a broad platform to accommodate the evolving needs of our clients. The selected candidate will join the Investment Risk unit as a Quantitative Risk Analyst, working within the Private Wealth Investment Risk team. The unit is responsible for risk measurement, attribution, and analysis for the firm’s Equity, Fixed Income, Alternatives and Multi-Asset Class strategies. The Risk Department is a fast-paced, action-oriented group, which is responsible for independent risk oversight of all the varied strategies and handles numerous complex matters impacting Neuberger Berman Group LLC. We are seeking a highly motivated Analyst to join our New York office.

 Responsibilities: 
 - This person will work as an integral part of the Private Wealth Investment Risk team, taking up day-to-day coverage of several of the strategies/portfolio management teams
- The analyst will maintain and run regular and ad hoc reports; and provide support to various teams around the firm on risk and performance analytics issues
 - Translate academic and/or buy-side research into risk models, analytics, and methodologies that are implementable and actionable, leveraging large sets of data
- Perform ex-ante and ex-post portfolio risk analysis, performance and attribution analysis, stress tests, and back tests
- Stay connected and current with academic finance research and developments and present findings to team members
 - Prepare materials for presentations for reviews with portfolio managers, senior management and the firm’s Investment Risk Committee
- Solve real world risk management problems in a largely autonomous fashion while collaborating with team members

 Qualifications: 
 - 1-2 years of experience in a quantitative role, ideally in risk, portfolio analytics, or a related buy-side function
- Bachelor’s or master’s Degree in a quantitative field like Financial Engineering, Econometrics, Computer Science, Applied Mathematics, Statistics, or similar
- Strong interest in financial markets and willingness to learn on the job, including using AI tools to improve analysis and productivity.
- Required: Python programming and SQL experience
- Preferred: risk management and risk statistics knowledge, including familiarity with concepts such as risk decomposition, factor exposure and stress testing
