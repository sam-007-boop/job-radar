 The summary above was generated by AI Olo is a leading SaaS platform accelerating digital transformation in the restaurant industry, by helping customers deliver more personalized and profitable guest experiences. As a result, our digital ordering, payment, and guest engagement solutions enable brands to do more with less and make every guest feel like a regular. We're looking for a finance professional to join Olo's FP&A team and run the day-to-day of revenue planning. Olo is a Thoma Bravo portfolio company, and this role is within the core of our board reporting, which includes the Budget, Monthly Reporting Pack and ARR Snowball. You'll report to the Senior Director of FP&A, work closely with Business Analytics, RevOps, Accounting and S&O, and get direct exposure to how a private equity–backed software company plans, reports and makes decisions.
 You can work remotely from anywhere in the U.S. or at Olo’s headquarters in NYC. 
 How You’ll Make An Impact 
 - Own the monthly revenue reporting cycle: actuals, revenue and ARR bridges, and variance analysis against Budget and Forecast.

- Build the Monthly Reporting Pack (MRP) for Thoma Bravo and revenue materials for the board and senior leadership, from the numbers through to finished slides.

- Update the revenue forecast each cycle, explaining what changed and why across Olo’s business drivers, including bookings, active locations, order volumes, churn and pricing.

- Maintain ARR, gross/net retention and churn reporting, including the ARR snowball, in partnership with Business Analytics.

- Reconcile contracted ACV in Salesforce to recognized revenue, flagging discrepancies and working with Business Analytics and RevOps to resolve them.

- Work in Pigment as a primary planning tool, partnering with [MeltOne], our implementation partner, through the transition from Google Sheets, and taking day-to-day ownership of the revenue model once Revenue Planning is live in Pigment.

- Support analysis for pricing, partnership and investment decisions alongside senior FP&A leadership.

- Find and remove manual work: simplify processes, automate recurring reporting, and make the monthly cycle faster.

 What Will Set You Up For Success 
 - 3+ years in FP&A at a technology or SaaS company, or in investment banking, private equity or consulting.

- Bachelor's Degree in Business, Finance, or Accounting or equivalent experience.
- Highly proficient in financial analysis and model building with Excel and Google Sheets. 

- Experience with a modern planning platform such as Pigment, Anaplan or Adaptive is preferred but not required.

- Exceptional attention to detail, including a demonstrated standard for formatting consistency, data accuracy, and presentation quality in materials seen by board of directors and senior leadership.

- Fluency in SaaS metrics (ARR, NRR, churn, bookings) and how they connect to revenue.

- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills with demonstrated ability to work in a fast-paced and dynamic environment.
