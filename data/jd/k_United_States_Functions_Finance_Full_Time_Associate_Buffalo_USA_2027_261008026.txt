You're the brains behind our work.
 You're ready to bring your knowledge from the classroom to the boardroom — and Citi wants to help you get there! Citi's Global Functions business division is looking for driven Associates to join the Corporate Finance team in the US. Our Financial Management Associate (FMA) rotational program is designed to accelerate your career by giving you exposure to numerous roles across Corporate Finance, helping you discover your best-fit path at Citi. Citi offers significant opportunities for career growth and internal mobility.

 We provide you with the knowledge and skills you need to succeed.
 The FMA Program is a global leadership development program for Masters and MBA graduates, built to create a pipeline of future Corporate Finance leaders across disciplines including Controllers, Treasury, Financial Planning & Analysis, Tax, and business-aligned Chief Finance Offices. This 24-month program is structured around three 8-month rotations, giving you deep, meaningful exposure while working closely with senior managers to build real leadership capability. Rotations will be dependent on business needs and program availability. Upon successful completion, FMAs may be considered for opportunities within Citi Finance. As an FMA, you'll help drive Citi Finance's 4 priorities: champion financial performance & strategic execution, generate value & optimize resources, accelerate finance transformation & controls, and develop our people & culture — all while enjoying:
 - Senior Access – Exposure to Citi's senior Corporate Finance leaders who guide your growth
- Global Reach – Build a network of relationships across the global Citi Finance community
- Leadership – Sharpen your management skills through rotational responsibility
- Responsibility – High impact work aligned with Citi’s growth strategy
- Empowerment – FMAs are challenged to become involved in the fast-paced analytical and dynamic financial environment

 Your time here will look something like this...
 You'll kick things off with an energizing “Welcome Week” and comprehensive global training program starting in July. This is an opportunity to network with Citi's finance leadership and fellow FMAs — followed by an intensive curriculum covering accounting, modeling, technical and soft skills (presentation, communication, leadership), plus deep-dives into Citi's major businesses and Corporate Finance disciplines. From there, you'll roll straight into your first of three 8-month rotations, building both breadth and depth across Corporate Finance.

 We want to hear from you if...
 - You are currently pursuing an MBA or Master's degree in Accounting, Financial Strategy, Finance, Economics, or other related fields
- You're excited about building a career in Corporate Finance
- You bring at least 3 years of significant, related full-time professional work experience
- You're graduating between December 2026 – June 2027
- You won't require sponsorship for U.S. work authorization now or at time of hire
- Professional certifications such as CPA, CFA, FRM, or multilingual capabilities are considered a plus, but are not required

 Who we think will be a great fit...
 We're excited to meet motivated, globally minded MBA and MA graduates with an innovative outlook on business who bring:
 - A strategic, analytical mindset with a global perspective and a genuine passion for driving Citi Finance’s 4 Pillars.
- Initiative, creativity, and professional maturity, paired with resiliency and a growth mindset
- A sense of urgency, excellent judgment, and the confidence to step outside your comfort zone
- Strong written, verbal, and presentation skills, with the ability to build lasting business relationships and turn complex financial insights into clear, compelling stories
- A knack for process improvement — including technology-driven automation and optimization
- Technical proficiency in Excel, Word, and PowerPoint
- An AI-literate, forward-thinking mindset — eager to apply generative AI and emerging technologies to solve complex finance problems
- A demonstrated interest in Data Analytics, Database Management, Information Systems, or Digital Technology
- Sound risk management judgment — the ability to identify, assess, and escalate risk in a complex, regulated environment
