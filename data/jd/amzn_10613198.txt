Position Responsibilities:

Gather and analyze qualitative and quantitative economic, financial and other data to support financial planning and operating expense management. Prepare various deliverables to support both internal and external financial reporting needs, including insightful financial performance analysis to achieve business goals. Take ownership, identify process improvement opportunities for existing processes, reporting and controls, and drive positive change across the organization. Understand relevant accounting principles and communicate the application of GAAP to peers and business partners. Participate in writing white papers to support a given accounting position. Provide management and direction to junior analysts, including overseeing and reviewing reporting and Excel spreadsheet work papers. Document processes. Assess internal controls, identify gaps in the control process, and design additional controls to solve deficiencies. Oversee the monthly close process working closely with finance team to ensure the validity of the financials and report results to senior management. Act independently to complete multiple cross-functional projects with complex objectives. Create and maintain reports. Track down and identify sources of data, determine the validity of the data, and analyze the variance in the data between various systems and reports. Data gathered include Average Return Rates by product line, Cost per Repair, screening costs, labor costs, material costs, and scrap costs. Tasks performed to analyze the data include conducting trend analysis to identify unusual variances in data and when anomalies surface, engage business partners to determine whether unique operational circumstances during relevant periods explain the variance. Tools, techniques, and technologies used include AI tools and SQL queries to gather, segment, and synthesize data into meaningful insights. Outputs of the analysis include identification of clear explanations or specific corrective adjustments to incorporate into future forecasting cycles. Deliverables prepared include Month-End close variance reports, monthly and quarterly Profit and Loss (P&L) reports with supporting commentary. Cross-functional projects the position will complete include onboarding new business lines into the Reverse Logistics business through partnering with the Finance team to develop onboarding plans and build new financial models; collaborating with the Business Intelligence (BI) team to integrate the new business line’s data into existing dashboards. Output of those projects include enabling the Reverse Logistics business team to make decisions on the new product line, and allow tracking and use of data to forecast future costs consistent with existing business lines. This position does not have direct reports. The role provides direction and coaching to junior analysts on the team but does not plan and assign work to them.
