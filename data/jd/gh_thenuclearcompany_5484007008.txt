 About the role 
 The Senior Manager, Corporate FP&A is a strategic finance partner and hands-on builder at a fast-growing nuclear energy startup: the person who comes up with the plan and then runs with it. You will own the Annual Operating Plan (AOP), Long-Range Plan (LRP), and board decks, turning inputs from across the business into a single company-wide view that guides capital allocation, scenario planning, and the decisions that matter most as we scale, while supporting the executive team on funding. We are looking for an AI-forward finance leader who enjoys building the model and the workflow, thrives in the ambiguity of a scaling startup, and turns complex analysis into clear, decision-ready recommendations. If you want your finance work to have real impact on the future of nuclear energy, this is the role for you. 
 This is a hybrid position based in either our Columbia, SC, Lexington, KY, or Washington, DC office, with the expectation of working in the office ~3 days per week. This is not a remote position. Candidates must live within commuting distance of one of these locations or be willing to relocate. The position may also be posted in nearby markets for visibility.  

 Responsibilities 
 Strategic planning & forecasting 

 Design and own the Annual Operating Plan (AOP), Long-Range Plan (LRP), and rolling forecast end to end, consolidating inputs from functional finance partners, the Services and Deployment finance directors, and department leads into one company-wide plan 
 Define and track the KPIs that steer each segment, such as job margin, utilization, backlog-to-bill, DSO, and working capital, and hold the plan to clear forecast-accuracy standards 
 Maintain the cash runway model so leadership always has a current view of the company’s trajectory 

 Executive & board reporting 

 Own the board decks and the monthly and quarterly management reporting packages, turning variance analysis into insights and recommendations leaders can act on 
 Prepare investor reporting materials, with segment-grade reporting aligned to how the company presents its operating segments 

 Financial modeling & decision support 

 Build and evolve the corporate financial model to support scenario planning, capital allocation, and long-term planning, incorporating project-level models as inputs 
 Frame the key tradeoffs and deliver decision-quality analysis on profitability, unit economics, backlog, and working capital, bringing financial rigor and challenge to pricing, investment, and margin decisions 

 Strategic business & executive partnership 

 Act as a thought partner to the President & CFO, segment presidents, and the Services and Deployment finance directors on company-level decisions, including capital allocation and investment tradeoffs 
 Support the Treasurer and CFO on funding needs 
 Run the monthly operating review rhythm with segment leadership 

 Scaling the function with AI & systems 

 Build and deploy AI tools and agentic workflows across planning, forecasting, and reporting, scaling the function through technology rather than headcount 
 Lead the selection and implementation of FP&A systems with the Systems Analyst and Accounting, driving automation, data integrity, and a faster, more reliable close-to-report cycle 
 Stand up the processes and planning cadence that keep pace as the company grows 

 Required Experience 

 Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or a related field 
 7–10 years of progressive experience in FP&A, corporate finance, or investment banking 
 2+ years of experience owning planning and reporting processes and driving cross-functional alignment 
 Experience consolidating multi-function budgets, forecasts, and long-range plans into a company-wide plan 
 Expert financial modeling skills with the ability to build dynamic, assumption-driven models 
 Experience preparing and presenting financial insights to executives, the board, and investors 
 AI-forward mindset: hands-on experience using AI tools or automation to improve planning, forecasting, and reporting 
 Self-starter who thrives in a fast-paced, scaling environment, building structure and executing without a playbook 

 Preferred Experience 

 Experience supporting fundraising or investor relations 
 Public-company reporting experience

 FP&A system or ERP implementation experience (e.g., Adaptive, Anaplan, Mosaic, NetSuite) 
 Power/Energy/Utility, infrastructure, hardware, or manufacturing industry experience 
 Large capital expenditure project experience and project-level financial modeling
