 About the Role: 
 Wing is looking for a Senior Strategic Finance Manager to join our team. This role can be based remotely in the United States or hybrid in Palo Alto, CA .
 Reporting to the Head of Strategic Finance, you will partner with Wing’s leadership team across Marketing, Product, Partnerships, and Operations to help make better, more informed decisions on our most important business questions. As a small team, your scope will flex across Wing’s biggest priorities—from 30,000-foot strategy to unit economics, product investments, and pricing. We’re looking for someone who enjoys digging into messy data, building clear financial models, and turning complex trade-offs into simple, practical advice.
 What You’ll Do:  
 - Be a finance voice in the room on some of the company’s most important decisions: Evaluate major long-term investment decisions and competitive positioning, helping the Executive Team and Board understand the financial trade-offs of different paths.
- Guide marketing and growth investments: Partner with Marketing and Commercial leaders to figure out which user-acquisition strategies generate the highest ROI and what customer LTV/CAC looks like across markets.
- Build unit economics frameworks: Define and track our incremental cost per order and model how profitability improves as we add more volume, partners, and sites.
- Partner with Product on roadmap and feature decisions: Work with Product teams to evaluate which new product features, customer experience improvements, and fleet capabilities have the largest financial impact.
- Iterate on pricing and demand models: Analyze consumer and partner pricing structures, test how fee changes affect order volume, and forecast growth across new and existing customers.
- Evaluate market expansion and operational trade-offs: Partner with Operations and commercial teams on questions like which markets and sites to prioritize next, and how to pace local investments with our partner pipeline.
 What You’ll Need:  
 - 12+ years of experience in strategic finance, corporate strategy, management consulting, investment banking, private equity, or a similar analytical role at a high-growth company.
- Experience serving as a trusted advisor to senior business, marketing, product, or operations leaders.
- Strong track record of building unit economic, marketing ROI, pricing, or growth models from the ground up.
- Clear, concise communication skills. Able to explain complex analysis in plain English and write crisp memos for leadership.
- Experience helping scale or commercialize a growing product, marketplace, or business line.
- Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Business, Mathematics, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
- Experience in on-demand delivery, consumer marketplaces, retail/e-commerce, or logistics preferred.
