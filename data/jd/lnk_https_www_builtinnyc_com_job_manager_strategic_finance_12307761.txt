 About the role and team

 Working at Uber means solving hard problems in a high-stakes, fast-moving environment. As part of the Strategic Finance team, you will help scale financial reporting, analytics, and planning capabilities to empower decision-makers across our global functions.

 Your work and the insights you create will drive real-time business decisions and help Uber continue to grow, scale, and become more profitable, while also creating more opportunities for the many thousands of couriers, restaurants, and drivers that use our platform daily.
 To thrive here, you must be a resilient self-starter with an owner mindset, capable of building trust with diverse stakeholders while delivering precision at the speed of the business.

 What you’ll do

 - Lead financial reporting, forecasting, and capital planning across multiple high-growth and scaled products

 - Collaborate with cross-functional teams in Product, Tech, Operations, and Marketing to evaluate investment opportunities and resource decisions

 - Build and refine financial models to assess ROI, profitability, and long-term value creation

 - Establish frameworks to evaluate success across the product lifecycle and drive alignment on key performance indicators

 - Translate strategic priorities into actionable annual budgets and forecasts that reflect market conditions, customer behavior, and product stage

 - Conduct unit economics, profitability, and lifetime value analyses to support go-to-market and investment decisions

 - Drive the capital allocation process in partnership with business leaders and ensure sound financial stewardship of resources

 - Manage and develop a team of high-performing finance professionals, providing mentorship and coaching

 Basic Qualifications

 - Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or a related field (or equivalent experience).

 - 6+ years of experience in strategic finance, investment banking, or corporate finance.

 - Experience working with large-scale datasets and financial reporting systems.

 Preferred Qualifications

 - Proven ability to thrive in high-ambiguity environments and adapt to constant change.

 - Experience with SQL or similar data manipulation tools to drive business insights.

 - Strong systems thinking approach to solving complex, multifaceted business problems.

 - Excellent storytelling skills with the ability to translate technical finance data into compelling narratives for executives.

The city has a thriving fintech scene and is a major player in verticals ranging from AI to biotech, cybersecurity and digital media. It also has universities like NYU, Columbia and Cornell Tech attracting students and researchers from across the globe, providing the ecosystem with a constant influx of world-class talent.
