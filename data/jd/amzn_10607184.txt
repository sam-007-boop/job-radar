Amazon (incl. AWS data-center finance) — Senior Financial Analyst, AWS Competitive Intelligence
https://www.amazon.jobs/en/jobs/10607184/senior-financial-analyst-aws-competitive-intelligence

Amazon Web Services (AWS) is a leading global cloud computing platform, and we’re looking for a Senior Financial Analyst to join our Sales, Marketing, and Global Services (SMGS) Finance organization. In this role, you will develop competitive financial insights that help senior AWS leaders understand industry dynamics and evaluate strategic risks and opportunities.

Working closely with a Principal Finance Manager, you will build and maintain financial models for major industry participants, analyze public company disclosures, and investigate developments across the cloud computing industry. You will synthesize fragmented information into concise, executive-ready analyses that inform major business and investment decisions.

The ideal candidate combines strong financial modeling skills with intellectual curiosity, sound judgment, and clear writing. You should be comfortable working with incomplete information, challenging assumptions, and distinguishing disclosed facts from analytical estimates. This role offers the opportunity to develop a deep understanding of cloud and AI economics.

Key job responsibilities
- Build, maintain, and refine financial models that estimate industry participants’ revenue, profitability, capital investment, and cash flow using public filings, earnings transcripts, analyst research, and other credible sources.
- Analyze earnings releases and other public financial disclosures, identifying the drivers of performance and their implications for AWS’s competitive position.
- Prepare executive-ready financial summaries, charts, tables, and FAQ-style documents that communicate conclusions clearly, explain supporting evidence, and make uncertainty explicit.
- Improve research and reporting mechanisms through GenAI and automation, with appropriate human review and quality controls.

A day in the life
You will investigate new industry developments, or pressure-test a claim in third-party research, or update financial models with new industry disclosures. You will turn your findings into concise analyses and FAQs that explain what is known, what is estimated, and why it matters for AWS.

- 3+ years of finance, accounting, or related analytical field experience
- 3+ years of dissecting financial data and identifying patterns that support business strategy experience
- 3+ years of contributing to cross-functional initiatives that drive financial performance and strategy experience
- Bachelor's degree in business, finance, accounting, tax, economics, or a related analytical field

- MBA, or CFA
- Experience effectively gathering information from multiple data sources
- Experience using AI-assisted development tools in academic or professional settings
- 3+ years of experience analyzing public company financial statements (10-K, 10-Q, earnings transcripts) and deriving estimates from limited disclosures
