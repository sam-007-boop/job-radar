 What You Will Do : 

 Our Financial Transformation professionals help our clients in Defense and National Security optimize all elements of their financial management structure by transforming business processes, enhancing operational efficiency, improving transparency and performance management, promoting change management, and complying with Federal laws and regulations.
 The nature of our projects is extremely dynamic and requires proactive leaders who can manage teams, drive client outcomes, and develop innovative solutions to complex financial management challenges. Managers are expected to lead project delivery, mentor team members, interact regularly with senior government stakeholders, and support business development efforts. Our team provides specialized experience and knowledge in financial management, process analysis, risk management, performance measurement, analytics, and emerging technologies to support mission-critical initiatives.

 Specific initiatives and work products that this role supports include:

 - Leading financial transformation initiatives and partnering with senior government stakeholders to modernize financial operations through innovative business and technology solutions.
- Managing project teams and workstreams, ensuring high-quality deliverables, effective resource utilization, and successful project execution.
- Leading the design, development, and implementation of Microsoft 365 Power Platform solutions, including Power Apps, Power Automate, SharePoint, and Power BI capabilities.
- Overseeing the development of performance metrics, executive dashboards, and data visualization solutions to support informed decision-making and operational transparency.
- Identifying opportunities to improve financial management processes, tools, controls, and reporting capabilities through technology-enabled solutions.
- Leading current-state assessments, stakeholder interviews, process mapping efforts, and future-state design activities to improve business operations and mission performance.
- Developing and managing project plans, work breakdown structures, schedules, risks, issues, and dependencies to ensure successful delivery.
- Serving as a primary point of contact for client leadership and providing regular updates, recommendations, and strategic guidance to executive stakeholders.
- Ensuring financial management standards, federal regulations, internal controls, and audit-readiness requirements are incorporated into solution design and implementation.
- Leading teams in the development of financial automation, advanced analytics, data visualization, and artificial intelligence (AI) capabilities to address complex financial management challenges.
- Coordinating the development of financial metrics, dashboards, low-code applications, workflow automation, and AI-enabled solutions using technologies such as Power Platform, SharePoint, Tableau, Databricks, Snowflake, SQL, Python, and other emerging technologies.
- Mentoring and developing consultants and senior consultants by providing coaching, feedback, and technical guidance to support professional growth.
- Supporting business development activities, including proposal development, solutioning, client relationship management, and identification of new opportunities.

 - SIX (6) or more years of experience supporting business transformation, financial management, audit, accounting, data analytics, process improvement, or related consulting engagements.

 What Would Be Nice To Have :
