Amazon (incl. AWS data-center finance) — Senior Financial Analyst, Global Prime Finance
https://www.amazon.jobs/en/jobs/10613744/senior-financial-analyst-global-prime-finance

Global Prime Finance (GPF) seeks a Senior Financial Analyst to drive strategic thinking on Prime Member spend and engagement. The role owns Prime Member Gross Merchandise Sales (PMGMS), a core measure of how Prime Members spend, and turns member behavior into strategies, new metrics and investment recommendations for WW Prime. The candidate will work across recurring reporting and planning, including monthly reporting and operating-plan cycles, and strategic analyses. They will use AI-enabled workflows to transform data into insights. 

The role combines analytical ownership with AI-enabled process design. The candidate will define the business question and hypotheses, investigate how, why, when and on what Prime Members spend, and explain how results differ across countries, cohorts, benefits and plan types. They will use AI to handle repeatable work such as data gathering, reconciliations, first-draft commentary and routine analysis. They will own the parts that need finance judgment such as checking outputs against source data, challenging assumptions, resolving exceptions, and turning the analysis into a clear point of view for senior leaders. They will design reusable systems so a solution that works for PMGMS can be adapted across Prime Finance.

The right individual brings strong analytical judgment, an AI first mindset and a deep interest in Prime Member behavior. They will work with WW Prime Finance, country teams, data teams and business partners to identify the drivers of spend and engagement, test ideas, and scale the best solutions. They will be comfortable influencing without authority and will demonstrate Invent and Simplify, Dive Deep, and Learn and Be Curious by making finance insight deeper, faster and more useful.

Key job responsibilities
- Be a subject matter expert for Prime Member engagement and spend, developing the global framework for evaluating engagement initiatives, tracking performance, and understanding drivers
- Identify opportunities for process improvement and automation
- Design, test, and run AI-enabled workflows for recurring PMGMS reporting, planning, and analysis. 
- Use AI to automate repeatable work, including data gathering, reconciliations, first-draft commentary, and routine analysis. 
- Proactively partner with other Prime Finance teams and Prime business teams to identify high-value processes, redesign them with AI, and scale proven solutions across the organization.
- Influence business decisions through data driven analysis and modeling
- Partner with business intelligence teams to explore available data, identify and construct new datasets, and isolate trends and insights to informengagement and spend initiatives
- Drive scenario planning and comfortably challenge assumptions
- Partner closely with multiple finance and business leaders across Stores to establish robust reporting, forecasting models and variance analysis.
- Provide financial analyses and strategic leadership to WW Prime Engagement teams, as well as Global Finance teams supporting Amazon-wide goals
- Collect, analyze, and manage quantitative data and creates meaningful reports to drive spend growth

This position is part of a high-performing, fast-paced Finance team whose mission is to be the trusted strategic advisor to the business and the go-to source for an end-to-end understanding of the Amazon customer. This is a highly visible role within the finance team and requires strong partnership and communication.
