 What would you do? 
 Role - Senior Business Analyst – Financial Services (Accounting Domain) 
 Location – McLean, VA (5 day onsite) 
 Key Responsibilities: 
 Job Summary 
 Position Overview:  We are seeking a seasoned Senior Business Analyst with good experience in the financial services domain, with deep, hands-on expertise in accounting business use cases . The ideal candidate bridges the gap between business and technology, bringing a strong understanding of the IT ecosystem and a proven track record of partnering with IT stakeholders to drive application modernization initiatives. 
 Key Responsibilities: 
 - Elicit, analyze, document, and validate business requirements from stakeholders across finance, accounting, and operations. 
- Translate complex accounting and financial business processes into clear functional and non-functional requirements. 
- Partner closely with IT stakeholders (architects, developers, QA, infrastructure, and product owners) to drive and support application modernization programs (e.g., legacy system migration, re-platforming, cloud adoption, microservices decomposition). 
- Produce high-quality artifacts: BRDs, FRDs, user stories, process flows, data flow diagrams, use cases, and traceability matrices. 
- Conduct gap analysis between current-state (“as-is”) and future-state (“to-be”) processes and systems. 
- Facilitate workshops, requirement sessions, and stakeholder alignment across business and technology teams. 
- Support solution design discussions, ensuring alignment between business needs and technical feasibility. 
- Define and support User Acceptance Testing (UAT) – test scenarios, test cases, and defect triage. 
- Act as a liaison between business users and development teams throughout the SDLC (Agile / Scrum / hybrid). 
- Identify risks, dependencies, and process improvement opportunities during modernization efforts. 
 Experience Required: 

- 10 to 12 years of overall experience as a Business Analyst, with a significant portion in the financial domain . 
- Demonstrated experience working on accounting business use cases (e.g., general ledger, accounts payable/receivable, reconciliations, financial close, regulatory reporting, chart of accounts, sub-ledger accounting). 
                Required Skills & Qualifications: 
 - Strong domain knowledge of accounting and financial processes and the systems that support them. 
- Solid understanding of the IT ecosystem – application architecture, integrations/APIs, databases, data flows, and cloud concepts. 
- Hands-on experience driving or supporting application modernization / legacy transformation initiatives. 
- Proficiency in requirements and process modeling tools (e.g., JIRA, Confluence, Visio, Lucidchart, Enterprise Architect). 
- Strong SQL / data analysis skills for data validation, mapping, and reconciliation. 
- Experience with Agile and Waterfall delivery methodologies. 
- Excellent stakeholder management, communication, and documentation skills. 
- Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Information Systems, Business, or related field. 
 Preferred / Nice-to-Have skills: 

- Experience working in the Loan / Mortgage industry, specifically in the secondary market, within the loan accounting domain (e.g., loan sales/securitization, investor accounting, servicing accounting, gain/loss on sale, loan-level accounting, GSE/agency). 
- Professional certifications: CBAP, PMI-PBA, CSPO/CSM , or accounting-related credentials (CPA, CMA, CA). 
- Exposure to ERP / financial platforms (e.g., SAP FICO, Oracle Financials, Workday, PeopleSoft). 
- Knowledge of regulatory/compliance frameworks (e.g., IFRS, US GAAP, SOX). 
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS) in a modernization context. 
- Familiarity with data warehousing, reporting, and analytics tools.
