 Power & Utilities is consistently a top-five lead arranger of syndicated loans and debt capital markets bookrunner, with an uninterrupted track record of more than 30 years banking the most active utilities, independent power producers, and renewable energy companies in the United States. The rapid expansion of artificial intelligence, data centers, and hyperscale cloud computing is driving significant growth in electricity demand, positioning Power & Utilities clients at the forefront of investment in generation capacity and transmission and distribution infrastructure to deliver reliable power at scale.

 As an integral member of the Power & Utilities team, the Analyst will assist in managing major client relationships by providing financial analysis, marketing, and operational support to Global Relationship Managers (GRMs) in leading business development efforts. The role requires credit analysis of proposed transactions, staying abreast of client activity and financing plans, and monitoring portfolio exposures. The Analyst will also support business origination by analyzing portfolio profitability, tracking documentation, drafting pitch books, following through on deal-origination processes, and preparing economic capital analysis. The position will be based in New York.

 Major Responsibilities: 

 GRM Support - Support business development alongside the GRM(s), including industry research, production of marketing materials, coordination with product areas, and other activities identified by the GRM(s). Key responsibilities include:
 - Conducting due diligence on prospective and existing clients  
- Reviewing new business opportunities and preparing materials to screen new transactions with senior management for approval  
- Developing client marketing presentations and pitch materials under the direction of the GRM(s)  
- Maintaining the Salesforce database  
- Coordinating transaction closings with back- and middle-office teams  
- Attending client meetings, when appropriate, to observe the GRM(s) and develop a working knowledge of relationship management  
- Preparing call reports for GRM review and comment when attending client meetings

 Risk and Credit Analysis - Undertake detailed financial analysis and transaction due diligence, including review of financial models and financial statements.  
 - Preparing credit applications, including summaries of expected financial results and risk analysis, for submission to risk and control functions  
- Reviewing and analyzing transaction term sheets and documentation, with a focus on credit features and covenants in financing documents  
- Owning and managing processes required for compliance and execution, including profitability analysis, committee approvals, legal due diligence, KYC, and closing checklists

 Product Liaison - Liaise with product areas on Global Corporate and Investment Banking transactions to build knowledge of MUFG's capabilities. Apply developing product knowledge when drafting presentations and transaction memoranda.

 Qualifications:   

 - A BA/BS degree or equivalent is required  
- 12 months of banking or related experience is preferred  
- Proficiency in PowerPoint and Excel modeling  
- Previous exposure to accounting and finance coursework is preferred  
- Understanding of the power and utilities sector, energy transition trends, or related work experience is preferred  
- Proactive, resourceful self-starter capable of rapidly learning new systems and processes and working effectively in a team environment  
- Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to deliver high-quality, clear, and concise presentations and memoranda with limited assistance  
- Ability to communicate consistently in a professional, tactful, and effective manner with clients, third-party advisors, and colleagues  
- Demonstrated ability to work under pressure in a high-intensity environment, manage and prioritize multiple workstreams, and maintain a high degree of attention to detail and accuracy
