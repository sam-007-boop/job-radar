THE ROLE

As the Sales Strategy & Operations Manager for Baseten’s Solutions Architecture organization, you’ll be the day-to-day strategy and operations partner to our Head of Solutions Architecture and the SA managers who report to them. You’ll give them the analysis and operating support to run the SA org, and turn their priorities into the plans, capacity models, and operating cadence the team runs on.

Our Solutions Architects are central to how Baseten wins and grows. Revenue is consumption-based, so a deal isn’t done at signature. It grows as customers get workloads into production and scale them. SAs drive that at every stage: technical evaluations and POCs, onboarding, and the optimization work that expands usage inside existing accounts. Capacity planning, coverage, and impact measurement all have to reflect that. You’ll sit on the Revenue Strategy & Operations team and work closely with Sales leadership, Finance, Comp, Enablement, and GTM Engineering. You’ll own the SA org’s numbers, the reviews that inspect them, and the processes and systems that keep them reliable.

WHAT YOU'LL WORK ON

 - Operating cadence: Run the reviews that show SA leaders where SA time goes and what it delivers.

 - Capacity and coverage: Build the models behind SA headcount and how SAs are allocated across segments and accounts.

 - SA analytics: Measure SA engagement, technical win rates, and impact on consumption growth.

 - Process and systems: Build the SA engagement model into our systems.
