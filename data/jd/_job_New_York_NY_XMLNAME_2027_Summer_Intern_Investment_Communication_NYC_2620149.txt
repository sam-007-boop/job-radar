 As an Investment Communications Intern , you will support the CIBC Private Wealth U.S. Investment Team across communications, reporting, and research and coordination activities. This is an excellent opportunity for a student interested in investments, financial markets, communications, and business strategy.

 In this role, you will gain exposure to how investment insights are turned into materials that support clients, advisors, and internal partners. You’ll work closely with professionals across Investment, Performance, Marketing, and Compliance teams, while building practical experience in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.

 WHAT YOU’LL DO: 
 As an intern, you will contribute to a range of projects and team priorities, including:
 - Assist with quarter-end performance reporting and help coordinate deliverables across multiple teams
- Support the drafting, editing and updating of internal and external investment materials, including market commentaries, reports, presentations, and product-related content
- Conduct research on industry trends, competitors, and investment themes to support communication projects
- Create charts, graphs, tables, and other visuals for presentations and written materials
- Help with product due diligence questionnaires, including drafting responses, tracking requests, and coordinating submissions
- Maintain and organize content on SharePoint and Seismic sales enablement platform
- Help track deadlines and key milestones on the team’s shared Investment Communications calendar
- Provide general project support for investment-related initiatives, including document formatting, content review and quality control undefined

 WHAT YOU’LL LEARN: 
 - Through this internship, you’ll have the opportunity to: Gain hands-on experience in investment communications within a private wealth environment
- Develop project management, research, writing and editing skills Learn how investment teams collaborate with marketing, compliance, and other business partners
- Strengthen your ability to manage multiple priorities and work effectively under deadlines Build confidence in a professional, team-oriented setting undefined       

 WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR: 
 - We’re looking for students who are eager to learn, highly organized, and excited to contribute to a collaborative team. 
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Business, Finance, Marketing, Communications, or a related field
- 3.2 GPA minimum
- Strong written, verbal, and interpersonal communication skills
- Excellent organizational skills, attention to detail, and a strong sense of follow-through Ability to manage multiple assignments in a fast-paced environment
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills Positive attitude, strong work ethic, and willingness to support the team where needed
- Proficiency in Microsoft Office, including Excel, PowerPoint, Word, and Outlook Familiarity with PowerPoint formatting, Excel-based reporting, or business writing is a plus
- Experience with Bloomberg or FactSet is a plus
