About the Role: 

 Grade Level (for internal use): 
05 The Team:
 The Loan Platforms Commercial team is a select group of product and industry experts responsible for driving commercial success across a portfolio of established syndicated loan market offerings. The team builds strong internal and client-facing relationships and works closely with senior stakeholders to identify business priorities and translate them into commercial initiatives.

 Responsibilities and Impact:

 - Support Product Design: Partner with product analysts to help bring new features to market and contextualize client feedback, ensuring the commercial vision remains represented throughout the product development lifecycle.
- Engage with Clients: Proactively connect with customers to gather and process feedback on the product and business, helping inform the product and commercial roadmap.
- Contribute to Commercial Strategy: Work alongside commercial leadership to support the future direction of the business and execute against established objectives.
- Build Cross-Functional Relationships: Collaborate across operations, product, development, sales, and marketing to help position the business for commercial success.
- Support Sales Opportunities: Partner with sales colleagues on prospective business opportunities and provide relevant metrics, materials, and other support for active campaigns.
- Gain Industry Exposure: Develop knowledge of the syndicated loan market, Loan Platforms, and ClearPar while gaining hands-on experience across product, client engagement, sales, and commercial strategy.

 Compensation/Benefits Information:
 S&P Global states that the anticipated base salary range for this position is $20 to $25. Final base salary for this role will be based on the individual’s geographic location, as well as experience level, skill set, training, licenses and certifications.
 This role is not eligible for additional compensation such as an annual incentive bonus or sales commission plan.

 What We’re Looking For:

 Basic Required Qualifications: 
 - Currently pursuing a bachelor’s degree with an expected graduation date between Fall 2027 and Spring 2028.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to facilitate communication among multiple internal and external teams and stakeholders.
- Strong organizational, multitasking, and project management skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities effectively.
- Previous experience providing training and developing training materials for processes, software, or other tools.

 Additional Preferred Qualifications: 
 - Outgoing and collaborative personality with an interest in learning about the syndicated loan market and working across multiple functions.
- Interest in commercial strategy, financial technology, product management, client engagement, or sales.
- Ability to gather and synthesize customer feedback and communicate insights clearly to different audiences.
- Demonstrated curiosity, initiative, adaptability, and willingness to learn in a collaborative, fast-paced environment.

Note : The internship program will run from June 1, 2027 through August 6, 2027 .
