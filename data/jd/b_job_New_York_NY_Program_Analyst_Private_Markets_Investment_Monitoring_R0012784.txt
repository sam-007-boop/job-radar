Neuberger Berman — Program Analyst - Private Markets Investment Monitoring

Position Overview: 

 Neuberger is an employee-owned global investment management firm with over $600 billion in assets under management. Neuberger Private Markets is a leading and growing investor in private markets, managing over $200 billion of client commitments since inception.

 Within the Private Investment Portfolios & Co-Investment (“PIPCO”) business, the Investment Monitoring team is responsible for post-investment monitoring of primary fund investments and co-investments across the PIPCO platform. Key responsibilities include monitoring material events and headline news, preparing quarterly investment summaries, analyzing key performance data and valuation movements, and building AI-enabled tools that make monitoring more efficient and better. The role requires close partnership with the investment team, program managers, Operational Due Diligence, Sustainable Investing, data strategy, reporting, marketing, and finance teams to monitor, track, and manage risk across the portfolio.

 Primary Responsibilities: 

 - Monitor material events and headline news across the portfolio. When an event arises, work with internal and external parties to assess the potential impact
- Prepare quarterly investment summaries on primary fund investments and co-investments
- Analyze key performance data, track valuation movements and underperformance triggers, and deliver portfolio analysis and insights to the Investment and Portfolio Management teams
- Act as a point of contact on monitoring information requests and follow-ups
- Develop and maintain AI-enabled and automated monitoring workflows and support integration with internal data platforms
- Ad-hoc projects as needed

 Experience & Skills Qualifications: 

 - Bachelor’s degree in business, finance, accounting, data analytics, economics, or a related field
- 0-2 years of work experience, preferably in transaction advisory / accounting and financial, valuation, or banking
- Basic understanding of investment metrics and financial analysis
- Demonstrated use of generative AI tools to improve research, analysis, or workflow efficiency
- Ability to critically evaluate AI outputs and identify risks or inaccuracies
- High intellectual curiosity to learn the asset class and business
- Strong attention to detail, sound judgement, and critical thinking skills
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to articulate and present ideas effectively
- Ability to multi-task and manage competing priorities
- Team player with strong work ethic and positive attitude
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PPT; familiarity
