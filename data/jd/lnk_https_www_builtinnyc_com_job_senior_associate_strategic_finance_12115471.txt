   The Role 
 We are looking for a Strategic Finance Senior Associate to take meaningful ownership across the finance organization, reporting directly to the Director of Strategic Finance. This is a high-ownership, high-visibility role. You will work on key strategic initiatives alongside the leadership team and support the team as we scale.
 You will thrive in this role if you have a strong analytical foundation and want to further your operating experience at a fast-growing startup. In this position, you will build out and own sales forecasts and significant parts of the financial model. You will also have the opportunity to own and drive process around accounts payable and accounts receivable, tracking and forecasting OPEX, and leading ad-hoc analysis and strategic initiatives set by leadership.

 Required Qualifications 
 - 2–4 years of relevant experience in a similarly analytical role.

- Strong financial modeling skills and a solid understanding of accounting and finance fundamentals.

- Ability to translate complex financial analysis into clear, actionable recommendations for leadership.

- Strong attention to detail, with the judgment to connect financial results to broader business priorities.

- Demonstrated ownership and comfort managing multiple work streams in a fast-paced environment with shifting priorities.

 Preferred Qualifications 
 - Two years of experience in investment banking or consulting at a top tier investment bank or consulting firm.

- At least one year of finance experience at a high-growth startup, ideally at the Series B or Series C stage.

- Experience building sales forecasts, managing operating expense forecasts, or owning components of a company’s financial model.

- Experience improving finance operations, including accounts payable, accounts receivable, and reporting processes.

- Familiarity with SQL, business intelligence tools, or finance workflow automation.

 If you’re excited about this role but don’t meet every qualification listed, we encourage you to apply.
