Position Responsibilities:

Gather and analyze qualitative and quantitative economic, financial and other data to support financial planning and operating expense management. Prepare various deliverables to support both internal and external financial reporting needs, including insightful financial performance analysis to achieve business goals. Take ownership, identify process improvement opportunities for existing processes, reporting and controls, and drive positive change across the organization. Understand relevant accounting principles and communicate the application of GAAP to peers and business partners. Participate in writing white papers to support a given accounting position. Provide management and direction to junior analysts, including overseeing and reviewing reporting and Excel spreadsheet work papers. Document processes. Assess internal controls, identify gaps in the control process, and design additional controls to solve deficiencies. Oversee the monthly close process working closely with finance team to ensure the validity of the financials and report results to senior management. Act independently to complete multiple cross-functional projects with complex objectives. Create and maintain reports. Track down and identify sources of data, determine the validity of the data, and analyze the variance in the data between various systems and reports. Data gathered include deal and opportunity data from Salesforce/CRM; proposed deal pricing, discount, and credit/funding terms; purchase order (PO) and work order (WO) data tied to active engagements of AWS and 3rd party partners; contract and commercial terms; and historical deal economics and benchmark rates. Tasks performed to analyze the data include evaluating deal economics and pricing structures to support deal approval decisions; reconciling PO/WO data against deal terms and track the status of active engagements; analyzing margin, cost, and funding/credit implications of proposed deal terms; and modeling alternative pricing and commercial scenarios to inform deal structuring with Leadership. Tools, techniques, and technologies used include advanced Microsoft Excel, SQL, Salesforce, and business intelligence and reporting platforms. Outputs of the analysis include deal pricing and approval analyses and recommendations, PO/WO status reports and dashboards, and commercial and pricing scenario comparisons. Deliverables prepared include deal pricing and approval packages supporting commercial decisions for individual customers; deal economics, margin analyses and business case used in deal reviews; PO/WO status and reporting dashboards tracking engagement financials; and ad hoc commercial and pricing analyses and executive summaries supporting deal decisions. Cross-functional projects the position will complete include the design and build of PO/WO reporting dashboards requiring coordination across deals finance, sales, contracting team and technical/data teams; development and rollout of standardized deal/PO data-submission processes used by multiple stakeholder groups; and process-improvement initiatives to streamline PO/WO handling and strengthen deal-approval controls. Output of those projects include dashboards and reporting tool used by deals finance and business stakeholders, and improved accuracy, timeliness, and reduced manual effort in recurring deal/PO reporting.
