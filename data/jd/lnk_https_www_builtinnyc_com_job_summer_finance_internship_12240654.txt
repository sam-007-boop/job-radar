 Job Title: Summer Finance Internship 

 What You Will Experience: 
 - Support Tapestry's annual profitability benchmarking exercise, which involves detailed quantitative and qualitative research to establish acceptable levels of taxable income under Transfer Pricing principles for Tapestry's global operations
- Conduct market research and analysis to document current industry and economic trends in Tapestry's operating jurisdictions globally
- Ensure retail sales and payments are recorded accurately in the general ledger
- Reconcile cash and credit card receivable accounts
- Investigate cash deposit overages and shortages, and ensure incident reports are filed and communicated to Loss Prevention
- Track and report expenses related to our business processes
- Work directly in our systems to automate accounting and reporting for key areas of spend
- Support the financial close process in partnership with the Accounting team

 What You Will Bring: 
 - Interest in Business, Economics, Finance, and/or Accounting
- Proficiency in Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook
- Strong organizational skills and attention to detail
- Excellent interpersonal skills and strong oral and written communication skills
- Strong analytical skills
- Ability to prioritize, adapt, and handle multiple tasks
- Agile, solutions-oriented mindset with openness to trying new, innovative approaches
