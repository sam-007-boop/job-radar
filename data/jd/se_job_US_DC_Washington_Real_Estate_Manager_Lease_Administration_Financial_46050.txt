 What You Will Do : 
 Responsible for helping clients solve complex issues and manage risk in the delivery of large-scale capital projects and programs. Implements solutions from initial business case development, program management, integrity monitoring, through final close-out and transition to operations. Advises on public-private partnerships, infrastructure optimization, disaster recovery, and relocation.
 The Real Estate Manager - Lease Administration will support the FBI related projects to include but not limited to lease and real estate management, portfolio analytics, space management, and facilities management of a large and complex facility portfolio.

 Responsibilities include, but are not limited to:
 - Input, maintain, organize, and report on information regarding lease dates, dollar values, and financial accounting transactions into a lease management software database.
- Prepare, process, and reconcile accounts receivable and accounts payable transactions to ensure accurate and current lease information is maintained.
- Assist manager in resolving critical issues for clients by researching and gathering information about payment transactions, lease terms and other relevant items as requested.
- Prepare lease abstracts and edit them for accuracy.
- Edit narratives to support computer generated reports. Provide narratives on monthly variance reports. Provide lease analysis and interpretation of lease information to clients. 
- Track payables and receivables associated with the leases in portfolio. Process client rent rolls utilizing the database. Prepare export files and sends to accounting for payables processing. 
- Prepare invoices for subtenants, reconciles subtenant accounts, reviews incoming invoices for errors/accuracy with lease document and ensures correct coding. 
- Perform annual operating expense reconciliations, monitor audit windows and audit requirements contained in the lease documents. 
- Assist manager with preparation and distribution of periodic and ad hoc reports in a timely, accurate manner. 
- Run standard reports for management review and runs ad hoc reports with assistance. Assist with the design of reports utilizing a variety of software tools. 
- Prepare and distribute critical date reports; maintain tickler system for tracking important dates and tasks. 
- Create and maintain client lease files ensuring they contain current, accurate information.
