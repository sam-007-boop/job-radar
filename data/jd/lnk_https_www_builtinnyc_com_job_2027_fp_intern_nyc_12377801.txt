 About the Galaxy Summer Internship Program 
 The Galaxy NYC Summer Internship Program is an immersive, nine-week experience taking place on-site in our New York HQ office. During the nine weeks, you will gain hands-on, practical work experience on our the FP&A Team, working on real products and problems alongside the people building them. In addition to learning on the job, Galaxy interns participate in professional development programming, social events, and networking opportunities with senior leaders.
The FP&A team coverage spans all Galaxy segments, including Global Markets, Asset Management & Digital Infrastructure, and Data Centers. We track how each desk is performing, figure out what’s driving the results, and turn the numbers into clear takeaways for business leaders and senior management. As our FP&A intern, you’ll work with the team to analyze the drivers of business performance. You’ll look for trends, explain what’s moving the P&L (profit and loss) and help build the reports and dashboards leadership uses. You’ll learn about a diverse business and how to explain the story behind the numbers.
 Program Dates : June 7th – August 6th, 2027
 What You'll Do: 
 - Performance analysis : Analyze business performance by month, quarter and year-to-date. Find the key trends and explain what’s behind them, such as client activity, product mix, or expense changes. 
- P&L driver analysis : Help review the daily and month-to-date P&L. Point out the biggest moves and link them to the products, clients, or crypto price changes behind them, such as BTC or ETH. 
- KPI trends : Track trading volumes, client activity and how revenue is split across products and regions. Point out what’s growing, what’s slowing and what stands out. 
- G&A expense process : Support the monthly G&A expense process by comparing actuals to budget and forecast, following up with cost owners on variances, and summarizing what’s driving changes in spend. 
- Dashboards and reporting : Help maintain dashboards and prepare charts and short commentary for management reviews, tying every number back to its source. 
- Data quality and automation : Check and clean the data, for example client and region mappings. Find manual steps that could be made faster with Excel, Python, SQL or AI tools. 
- Summer project : Own one performance analysis from start to finish, such as a deep dive on one desk or a trend across products. Present your findings to the team at the end of the summer. 
 What We're Looking For: 
 - Currently pursuing a bachelor’s degree in finance, Economics, Accounting, Business, or a related field. 
- Strong analytical and quantitative skills, strong Excel skills (pivot tables, lookups, charts) and comfort working with large datasets.; familiarity with SQL, Python, or BI tools is a plus. 
- Excellent attention to detail, organization, and time management. 
- Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to present financial insights clearly. 
- Self-starter attitude with the curiosity to learn and the initiative to take on new challenges. 
- Available to work in-person in our New York office during the internship term. 
- Curiosity about what drives the numbers, not just what they are, and the ability to sum up a finding in two or three sentences. 
- Interest in Digital Assets, Digital Infrastructure, Data Centers, etc.
