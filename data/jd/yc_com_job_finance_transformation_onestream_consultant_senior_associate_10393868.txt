The Opportunity
As part of the Finance Consulting team, you will lead the design and implementation of transformative finance solutions using OneStream. As a Senior Associate, you will analyze complex problems, mentor junior team members, and build meaningful client relationships while navigating the intricacies of enterprise performance management. This role offers the chance to deepen your technical knowledge and enhance your personal brand in a dynamic professional environment.
Responsibilities
- Lead the design and execution of innovative finance solutions utilizing OneStream
- Analyze intricate issues and develop practical strategies for resolution
- Mentor and guide junior team members to enhance their skills
- Cultivate substantial relationships with clients to understand their needs
- Navigate the complexities of enterprise performance management
- Enhance technical knowledge and personal branding within the finance sector
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to drive project success
- Maintain standards of quality and professionalism in every deliverable
What You Must Have
- Bachelor's Degree in Accounting, Finance
- At least 4 years of relevant OneStream platform experience
What Sets You Apart
- Master's Degree in Accounting, Finance, Computer Science, Information Technology preferred
- OneStream Training Credentials preferred
- Managing large finance and data architecture transformations
- Leading design and testing of OneStream multi-dimensional models
- Configuring OneStream workflows and data management
- Managing core Accounting areas like Performance Management
- Building and maintaining client relationships
- Working in an Agile project management environment
- Demonstrating flexibility in prioritizing tasks
