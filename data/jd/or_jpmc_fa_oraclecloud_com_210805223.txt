 As a Project Manager within JPMorganChase , you will support the planning and execution of data and analytics projects and initiatives. Working under the guidance of senior team members, you will help ensure project activities stay aligned with business objectives. You will apply foundational knowledge of project management principles and your developing skills in data analytics and digital tools to help drive successful outcomes. Your role will involve supporting day-to-day project coordination, contributing to problem-solving, and partnering across teams. Your ability to stay organized, communicate clearly, and collaborate effectively will be key to your success in this role.
 Job responsibilities
 - Support the planning and execution of cross-functional data and analytics projects, applying your analytical and digital skills to help deliver results aligned with business objectives.
- Help develop and maintain project plans, tracking milestones and supporting timely communication of progress to stakeholders.
- Assist with the implementation of analytics and reporting initiatives.
- Collaborate with team members to help identify and escalate issues and risks that arise during project execution.
- Contribute to analysis that evaluates potential scenarios and their impact, supporting informed decision-making.
- Ensure project work is completed in accordance with Risk, Control, and other relevant firm-wide guidelines, helping maintain our control environment.
 Required qualifications, capabilities, and skills
 - Experience supporting or coordinating projects from start to finish, demonstrated through  4+ years of experience or equivalent .
- Developing analytical, technical, and problem-solving skills applied in a project or data context.
- Exposure to data, analytics, reporting, and BI tooling.
- Experience using data to analyze trends and support insights and recommendations.
- Proficiency using software applications and digital tools to solve problems, improve processes, and collaborate with stakeholders.
- Strong organizational and communication skills, with the ability to work effectively within a team.

 - Interest in or exposure to applying AI tools to support data-driven decision-making.

- A curious, collaborative mindset with an openness to new technologies and creative problem-solving.
