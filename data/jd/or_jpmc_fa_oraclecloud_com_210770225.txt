JPMorgan Chase — Strategic Execution Business Operations Team – Senior Associate
https://jpmc.fa.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1001/job/210770225

Be at the center of the work that keeps the Consumer & Community Banking Data and Analytics organization aligned and moving.  
 The Strategic Execution & Business Operations Team (SEBO) is Data & Analytics (D&A) chief-of-staff and operational enablement function — the "COO tower" for the approximately 2,000-person Data & Analytics organization. SEBO coordinates cross-functionally with strategy, finance, and communications teams to help realize the Chief Data & Analytics Officer’s vision. The team sits at the center of D&A's highest-visibility initiatives: book-of-work transparency, controls modernization, AI/agentic productivity measurement, and executive reporting, including Investor Day coordination.  
 As a Senior Associate on the Strategic Business Operations Execution Team, you will serve as a generalist contributor across the team's core workstreams, with particular emphasis on   operating rhythms and chief-of-staff support   for D&A leadership. You'll help run the cadence that keeps D&A aligned including leadership forums, business reviews, cross-D&A coordination. You’ll also flex into portfolio management, controls, and productivity-measurement work as priorities shift. This is a high-visibility seat for someone who wants broad exposure to how a large organization functions.   
 You'll need to be a creative thinker with superior judgment and executive presence, who thrives in a fast-paced, entrepreneurial environment. You’ll help leaders turn strategy into action through crisp operating rhythms, clear communications, and disciplined follow-through. Your ability to navigate organizational complexities and create structure from ambiguity will be key to your success.   
 Job Responsibilities:   
 - Independently develop, own, and execute initiatives within the Data & Analytics organization from inception to completion  
- Apply structured problem-solving skills to convert leadership priorities into coordinated action by tracking dependencies, flagging blockers, and following up across stakeholder groups 
- Facilitate working groups and brainstorming sessions to support leadership decision-making  
- Identify opportunities for process improvement and automation  and drive implementation of changes  
- Collaborate with team members across LOBs, central teams, and corporate functions  
- Prepare executive communication materials and present project analyses to senior executives 
- Maintain a controls mindset to ensure compliance with approved risk and controls processes 
- Drive problem-solving and develop recommendations that advance key team priorities 
- Utilize organizational and planning expertise to prioritize tasks and resolve challenges 
- Understand the leader’s intent and take initiative in the absence of specific instructions   

 Required Qualifications, Capabilities, and Skills:   
 - Bachelor’s degree and 3+ years of experience in business operations, chief-of-staff/program management, management consulting, or a similar cross-functional analytics or operations role  
- Excellent written and verbal communication with the ability to synthesize complex, cross-team information into clear, leadership-ready materials  
- Strong organizational and project-coordination skills; comfortable running multiple workstreams with competing deadlines simultaneously  
- Consulting orientation with strong client engagement skills  
- Ability to work effectively in cross-functional teams and influence business partners  
- Proficiency in PowerPoint and Excel, with comfort working with data in Monday.com, Tableau, or similar portfolio or BI tools  
- Creative problem-solver with the initiative to develop strategic solutions  
- Desire to challenge the status quo and drive change  

 Preferred Qualifications, Capabilities, and Skills:   
 - Exposure to AI/agentic tooling (e.g., GitHub Copilot, LLM-based analytics tools) and interest in productivity measurement  
- Experience in financial services or a large matrixed organization  
- Experience supporting executive-level reporting or offsite planning  
- Experience at a large consulting firm or in an internal corporate strategy function
