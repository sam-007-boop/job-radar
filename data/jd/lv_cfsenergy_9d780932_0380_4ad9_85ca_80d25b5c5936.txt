Join the power movement as Senior Finance Analyst, Operations
We're looking for a Senior Financial Analyst to partner directly with our Operations teams, including Facilities, EHS and Supply Chain, owning the forecast, building business cases for new investments, and giving leaders the financial visibility they need to run an efficient, scalable operational network.
This is a business partner role at the center of how we operate. The ideal candidate will bring rigorous forecasting and modeling skills, the ability to turn financial results into practical operational actions, with a deep curiosity about how our sites, suppliers and processes work day to day.

What you'll do:: 
 Own the forecast process for the Operations teams (including Facilities, EHS and Supply Chain), driving accuracy and accountability across monthly and quarterly cycles 
 Build annual budgets for Operations cost centers and track actuals against plan, investigating variances and flagging risks and opportunities early 
 Collaborate cross-functionally to build business case models that evaluate new investments, partnerships, and strategic initiatives 
 Translate financial results into operational insights and actions that improve business performance 
 Advise Operations leadership and budget owners on resource allocation, operating efficiencies and investment risk to inform decision-making 
 Help harmonize processes, data governance and reporting across the operational network 
 Support special projects for operational improvement, including system and tool automation that increases efficiency and frees up time for deeper analysis 
 Contribute to executive-level presentations and reporting packages for senior leadership 

What we’re looking for:: 
 Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or a related quantitative field 
 7 years of experience in FP&A, corporate finance, or operations/supply chain finance 
 Strong financial modeling skills and advanced Excel, including experience building business cases and investment analyses (NPV, IRR, payback) 
 Ability to communicate clearly with operational stakeholders and translate site, supplier and process data into financial impact 
 Comfort presenting to senior leadership and building clear, concise reporting packages 
 Detail-oriented, organized, and comfortable working with incomplete information in a fast-moving environment 

Bonus Points For:: 
 Experience supporting a high-growth company. 
 Exposure to manufacturing, facilities or supply chain cost structures (e.g., inventory, freight, capital projects) 
 Hands-on experience with ERP and planning/automation tools (e.g., Oracle EPM, Anaplan, Tableau etc) 

Must-have Requirements: : 
 Perform activities such as typing, standing, or sitting for extended periods of time 
 Willingness to travel or work required nights/weekends/on-call occasionally 
 Work in a facility that contains industrial hazards including heat, cold, noise, fumes, strong magnets, lead (Pb), high voltage, high current, pressure systems, and cryogenics
