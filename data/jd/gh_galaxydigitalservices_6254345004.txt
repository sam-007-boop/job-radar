 Who You Are: 
 Working as the Capex FP&A Associate on the Data Center Finance team, you will own the financial side of Galaxy’s data center construction program: building and maintaining construction budgets, cost-to-complete forecasts, and capital cash flow models that support project financing, equity funding, and executive decision-making. You will be the  liaison between Construction, Procurement, Accounting, and Treasury, maintaining a single, reliable view of the budget, commitments, actuals, forecasts, and funding for every project from award through closeout. You should be able to conceptualize the “big picture” and translate concepts into detailed tactical execution plans while maintaining a high degree of accuracy. You should be comfortable working in a fast-paced environment, managing multiple priorities, and demonstrating the grit and ownership required to deliver accurate, timely insights. 
 What You’ll Do: 

 Develop a deep understanding of Galaxy’s growing data center business, including project phases, construction delivery structures, and how capital flows from budget to contract to cash. 
 Partner with Procurement and the construction team to build, model and maintain construction budgets and cost-to-complete forecasts by project and phase, tracking committed, incurred, and paid costs against budget and flagging variances early. 
 Analyze construction and vendor contracts (including GMPs, purchase orders, and change orders) to determine committed scope, exclusions, contingency, and the remaining purchases still to come. 
 Track and reconcile POs, change orders, and pay applications across vendors, ensuring budget, contract, and invoice records are tied out. 
 Develop quarterly and rolling 12-month capital cash flow forecasts, including vendor payments, deposits, retainage, and funding timing, and clearly show funded versus unfunded needs. 
 Support project financing reporting, including draw packages and funded-versus-unfunded schedules, reconciliation of project budgets to lender and investor models, and lender reporting requirements. 
 Partner with Accounting on month- and quarter-end close, including accrual support for work in place not yet invoiced, capitalization of project costs, and reconciliation of the capital budget and forecast to construction-in-progress (CIP) balances. 
 Collaborate cross-functionally with business leaders and partner closely with Construction, Procurement, and the broader Finance organization, including Group FP&A, Accounting, and Treasury. 
 Create dashboards and KPIs, covering budget versus actual, committed versus uncommitted spend, contingency usage, cost per MW, and funding status for executive and lender audiences. 
 Maintain documentation of financial processes and procedures to ensure compliance and efficiency, including controls over the capital budget and forecast process, and respond to audit requests related to capital projects. 
 Provide data-driven ad-hoc analysis to support executive and business specific reporting. 
 Embrace and champion the thoughtful adoption of AI to improve team performance and business outcomes. 
 Leverage AI tools (e.g., generative AI, automation platforms, data copilots) to improve productivity, decision-making, and output quality in your day-to-day work
