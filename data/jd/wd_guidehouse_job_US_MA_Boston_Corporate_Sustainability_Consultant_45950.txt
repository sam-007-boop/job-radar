 Guidehouse is a leading management consulting firm serving the public and commercial markets.  We guide our clients forward towards new futures that build trust in society and your professional skills along the journey. We have a long history of leading sustainability work in both the public and private sector. 

 What You Will Do : 
 Consultants support project teams, primarily for clients but also and internally. Responsibilities will include client management, solution implementation and generation of insightful, technically correct, and appealing project results. As a consultant, a key function of your role will be to create and present quality deliverables that support essential project workstreams.  You will gather and analyze data, identify gaps and trends, and make recommendations related to baseline performance and structure, as well as established best practices and benchmarks. 

 Specific responsibilities include :
 - Conduct technical, economic, and financial analyses, including data collection, modeling, and tool management
- Evaluate energy efficiency measures and renewable energy technologies
- Apply sustainability analytics, including corporate GHG accounting, climate risk, ESG regulatory disclosure, and abatement analysis
- Perform structured research and fact‑finding on business, sustainability, and energy topics; assess trends and market approaches
- Develop corporate sustainability and business strategies
- Execute project management and operational support of multi-client programs 
- Conduct targeted business development activities
- Communicate insights and recommendations through client-ready reports and presentations

 What You Will Need : 
 - Must be a US Citizen or US Permanent Resident due to nature of client engagements
- Bachelors in a quantitative field including economics, finance, engineering, science and/or related discipline
- One (1+) plus years of post-graduation work experience in quantitative analyses
- Strong interest in corporate sustainability and energy transition
- Outstanding analytical and problem-solving skills
- Possess technical fluency, especially in MS Excel, PowerPoint, and Word
- Proactive and independent work style
- Strong client presence, professional demeanor and presentation skills
- Ability to assume ownership of significant portions of tasks while collaborating with a close-knit team
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills
- Ability to travel as needed
- Ability to work in a Guidehouse Office or Client Office as needed

 What Would Be Nice To Have : 
 - Preference will be given to candidates residing within 50 miles of a Guidehouse Office listed
- Master’s degree in quantitative and relevant field
- Management consulting experience
- Demonstrated experience and skill at corporate sustainability analyses
- Demonstrated experience with program management
