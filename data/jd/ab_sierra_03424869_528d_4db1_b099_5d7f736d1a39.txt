WHAT YOU’LL DO

Sierra closes large, complex enterprise deals, and RFPs are an increasingly critical part of that process. This role is the operational backbone of Sierra's RFP response function, owning day-to-day execution with an emphasis on accuracy, speed, and quality.

 - Own Sierra’s RFP response: Triage incoming RFP requests, coordinate with internal stakeholders, and deliver accurate, polished responses back to sales on time

 - Drive cross-functional coordination: Partner with subject-matter experts (SMEs) in Security, Legal, and Product to get the right answers fast, managing competing stakeholder priorities without losing momentum or quality

 - Own the content infrastructure: Maintain and continuously improve Sierra's RFP knowledge base, identifying gaps, working with SMEs to close them, and raising the quality bar on every response

 - Build toward automation: Implement and iterate on AI tooling and workflow automation to reduce manual work and increase consistency across the response process

 - Support Sales in high-stakes evaluations: Help AEs navigate complex RFP requirements, flag patterns and themes in prospect questions that inform how Sales positions Sierra, and ensure every response puts Sierra's best foot forward

WHAT YOU’LL BRING

 - 2-3 years of experience at a high-growth technology company in product operations, revenue operations, legal operations, deal desk, solutions engineering, or investment banking

 - Exceptional attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple competing priorities simultaneously without losing quality

 - Strong written communication skills - you can translate complex, technical information into clear and accurate language quickly

 - Proven ability to sustain high output and high quality simultaneously - you don’t cut corners when the workload spikes

 - A track record of improving the processes you inherit, not just running them

 - Genuine enthusiasm for applying AI and automation tools to make repetitive work faster and better

EVEN BETTER…

 - Prior experience with RFP or proposal platforms (Conveyor, Loopio, or Responsive)

 - Exposure to enterprise security or compliance questionnaires (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, etc.)

 - Experience in a sales-adjacent or customer trust function

OUR VALUES

 - Trust: We do the right thing, even when no one is looking, and even when it’s hard. When we screw up, we take accountability, fully and completely. We act in such a way that if all of our actions were completely visible to our customers, we would be proud.

 - Customer Obsession: Our entire business is designed to ensure every agent built on Sierra succeeds, and that we are accountable to our customers and their success. We do whatever it takes to make our customers successful, and we go the extra mile in how we show up as a partner with unreasonable hospitality.

 - Craftsmanship: A great company is the aggregation of thousands of things done well. As they say, “how you do anything is how you do everything”. Most important is craftsmanship in building agents that are so great that people thank them at the end of a call. That’s the standard.

 - Intensity: We operate with urgency, and we want to win. We are never complacent. We fight to win every deal, big or small, using the full force of the company. And when we fail, we talk about it openly and without blame so we succeed the next time.

 - Family: We know that balance and intensity are compatible, and we model it in our actions and processes. We work hard, and turn on the afterburners when needed. But we pick up our parents at the airport when they’re visiting from out of town. We go to our kids’ events. And we show up for one another, in moments of celebration and hardship.
