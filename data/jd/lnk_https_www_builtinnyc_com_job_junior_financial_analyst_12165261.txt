 Performs financial analysis, reporting, reconciliations, and consolidations related to company expenses, income, budgets, forecasts, and business results. Investigates variances between actual and budgeted performance, identifies trends and cost impacts, supports financial decision-making, ensures accurate general ledger allocations, and assists with special projects.  Create routine financial reporting for distribution to all levels of management
 Research, analyze, and report fluctuations in financial and business results to identify trends and research variances
 Research, analyze, and report fluctuations between actual financial results compared to budgeted amounts to advise management on potential areas of cost impacts
 Assist in the preparation of company budgets and financial forecasts for the development of business plans
 Ensure expenses are charged to the general ledger for the correct division; enforce company policies around this activity
 Perform reconciliations and consolidations
 Prepare reports that outline the company's financial position in the areas of income, expenses, and earnings to assist in financial decision-making
 Partner with Financial Analysts to analyze and interpret various financial reports and statistics to management
 Support department special projects
