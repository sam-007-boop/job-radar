 What You Will Do : 
 Our Data and Analytics Consultants on the Department of Veterans Affairs team help clients maximize the value of their data and automate business processes. This high-performing team works with clients to implement the full spectrum of data analytics and data science, from data querying and data wrangling to data visualization and dashboarding, predictive analytics, machine learning, artificial intelligence, robotic process automation (RPA), and application development. Our services enable clients to define their information strategy, generate mission-critical insights, make data-driven decisions, reduce cost and complexity, increase trust, and improve operational effectiveness. Responsible for researching and applying knowledge of existing and emerging data science principles, theories, and techniques to inform business decisions for healthcare clients. Conducts economic modeling, statistical analysis, and market surveys. Develops tools, techniques, and theories to facilitate ongoing research activities for the client. Uses predictive and prescriptive analytics tools to forecast what may happen using probabilities and current confidence levels.Our consultants focus on client services while also supporting business development, internal firm initiatives, and ongoing professional development. The role will also support development of governed analytics and data applications in Azure Databricks, including use of Unity Catalog for data and AI governance, Databricks Apps for application delivery, and Azure commercial cloud services for scalable solution development and deployment.

 What Would Be Nice To Have : 
·       Hands-on experience developing, governing, or deploying data and analytics solutions in Azure Databricks, with practical knowledge of Unity Catalog, Databricks Apps, workspace administration, access controls, and integration with Azure commercial cloud services

 ·       Knowledge of the Department of Veterans Affairs and/or Veterans Health Administration environment
 ·       Prior experience developing methodologies and performing analysis to assess health plan and provider operations

 ·       Excellent oral and written communication skills
