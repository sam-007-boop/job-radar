 What You Will Do : 

 - Collect, organize, and analyze data from multiple operational and business systems.
- Develop reports, dashboards, charts, and visualizations to support decision-making.
- Perform routine data validation, quality checks, and data preparation activities.
- Support performance measurement and readiness reporting activities.
- Assist senior analysts and project leads in executing analytical tasks.
- Develop presentations and analytical products for stakeholders.
- Document findings, methodologies, and recommendations.

 What You Will Need : 

 - An ACTIVE and MAINTAINED "SECRET" Federal or DoD security clearance 
- Bachelor's degree.
- THREE (3) or more years of experience in data analysis, business intelligence, or reporting.

 What Would Be Nice To Have : 

 - Experience supporting government or defense clients.
- Knowledge of Navy logistics, maintenance, or supply chain operations.
- Experience with Databricks, Palantir Foundry, or cloud analytics tools.
- Familiarity with statistical analysis techniques.
- Experience with Microsoft Power Platform applications.
- Experience using Excel, SQL, and at least one visualization tool (Power BI, Tableau, or Qlik).
- Ability to interpret and communicate analytical findings.
- Strong attention to detail and problem-solving skills.
