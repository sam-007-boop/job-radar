JPMorgan Chase — BWM Analytics Solutions Senior Associate

The Banking and Wealth Management team for Consumer and Community Banking is looking for a Deposits Analytics Senior Associate to drive end-to-end deposits reporting analytics and data modernization for Banking and Wealth Management, Consumer Bank deposit reporting to ensure executive-ready insights across regulatory & SEC management reporting. 
 As a Senior Associate within Banking and Wealth Management Controllers, Data & Analytics team, you will support key deposits reporting and analytics deliverables end-to-end and help drive modernization efforts that integrate new platforms/infrastructure into the ledger and downstream finance and reporting ecosystems. You will operate in a controlled environment (SOX/audit/regulatory expectations) and influence stakeholders through strong data storytelling, issue escalation, and delivery execution. 

 Job Responsibilities 
 - Own sourcing, compilation, validation, and distribution of financial data for complex quarterly/annual reporting, including deposits-related regulatory/SEC deliverables and management materials. 
- Drive stakeholder alignment across product owners, technology, LOB deposit controllers, SEC Reporting, Regulatory Reporting, firmwide reporting, and finance teams in a matrixed environment. 
- Assist strategic initiatives to automate/enhance reporting infrastructure; assess processes and implement scalable solutions and controls. 
- Partner on new product and platform initiatives by coordinating finance infrastructure changes, readiness, and control integration (including testing data solutions against submissions/manual processes). 
- Provide project management discipline (timelines/milestones) and escalate issues constructively with concise, executive-ready communications. 
- Deliver across recurring reporting, analytics, controls/documentation, and modernization/change initiatives in partnership with cross-functional stakeholders. 

 Required Qualifications, Capabilities, and Skills 
 - Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or Management Information Systems 
- 4+ years in Accounting/Finance, reporting, analytics, controllership, or related roles with demonstrated end-to-end ownership of deliverables (monthly/quarterly cycles) 
- Advanced Excel; strong proficiency in SAP and Hyperion Essbase and/or Dodeca for data sourcing, reconciliation, and reporting workflows 
- Experience with SQL, DataBricks, Tableau, Alteryx (or similar analytics/automation tooling) and use of AI tools  
- Track record of contributing to automation, data quality, or modernization initiatives within finance/controllership technology stacks 
- Proven analytical/problem-solving skills with the ability to distill complex data into concise, executive-ready insights and narratives 
- Strong communication, negotiation, and stakeholder management skills across product, technology, finance, policy, risk, and control partners 
- Strong finance/controller fundamentals, hands-on data fluency 
- Strong organization/multitasking skills; ability to manage critical deadlines and periodic off-hours work as needed 

 Preferred Qualifications, Capabilities, and Skills 
 - Experience with Python is a plus 
- Public accounting experience and/or experience operating in a controlled reporting/audit environment 
- JIRA experience and comfort partnering with technology teams on requirements, testing, release milestones, and delivery governance 
- Exposure to deposits regulatory reporting processes/controls and balance sheet/income statement reporting in a large financial institution
