Who we are 
 About Stripe  
 Stripe, LLC. is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. 
 What you’ll do 
 Responsibilities 

 Partner closely with Product leadership to define the long-range vision, strategy, and priorities for the team. 
 Lead or advise on mission-critical product and GTM strategy initiatives.  
 Help shape the product roadmap for the Crypto business through robust gathering of competitive intelligence and user insights. 
 Partner with GTM leadership to drive GTM strategy and lead commercialization of key product launches. 
 Partner with Finance to drive planning processes, including investment decisions, strategy updates, operational plans, and OKRs. 
 Maintain a pulse on the financial health of the Crypto business, highlighting key metrics and trends to develop action plans and drive improvements. 
 Oversee efficient running of the Crypto organization, creating the processes and conditions that enable the Crypto teams to do their best efficient work.
