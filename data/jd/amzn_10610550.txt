Amazon is searching for an exceptional Senior Financial Analyst to support the ReCommerce (Reverse Commerce) business. The ReCommerce team is an agile, innovative organization dedicated to maximizing recovery for Retail and FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon) customer returns and overstock to benefit our customers, company, and environment.

Key job responsibilities
We are constantly innovating to create long-term value at Amazon by investing in the future and focusing on the planet, not just on the bottom line. World Wide Returns & ReCommerce includes business, product, program, operational, data, and software engineering teams, who together manage the lifecycle of returned and damaged products, create new ways to improve Seller experience and profitability, manage fraud, liquidations, run the AW business, tech solutions for the 30K+ associates in the field and so much more.
