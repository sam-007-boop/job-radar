About this role 

 Company: BlackRock Financial Management, Inc.
 Job Title: Associate, Investment Management
 Location: 50 Hudson Yards, New York, NY 10001

 Job Duties: Partner with senior investment professionals to drive the direct investment process by performing company and industry due diligence, financial modeling, and other quantitative and qualitative analyses as part of the investment team. Assists the team with sourcing, evaluating, and executing investment opportunities. Monitors and manages existing portfolio companies over the investment period by driving value creation initiative and evaluating exit options. Supports senior members of the team in the ongoing development and maintenance of a full suite of product marketing and client content, including presentation materials, due diligence materials, internal training materials, and periodic client communications and events. Supports fund raising by delivering relevant materials, thought leadership, and responses to clients. Completes prepared materials and speaking notes for senior leadership and leads client diligence for a global set of clients.

 Qualifications: Bachelor’s Degree or foreign equivalent in Finance or related field and 36 months of experience in job offered or closely related role. Position requires three years of experience in the following: ESG and sustainability frameworks; Financial modeling skills, including 3 statement, valuation, M&A and LBO, and sensitivity analysis; Company and industry due diligence; Unit Economics, cohort analysis, and business model evaluation; Drafting investment memos, and diligence summaries; Evaluating strategic and operational value-creation initiatives; and Technologies including PowerPoint, Excel, Power BI, Microsoft Dynamics, eFront, and Smartsheets. Position requires one year of experience in the following: Investment experience in one or more private market classes, including Private Equity, Private Credit, or Real Assets; and Experience coordinating transaction advisors and strong project management abilities across workstreams.
