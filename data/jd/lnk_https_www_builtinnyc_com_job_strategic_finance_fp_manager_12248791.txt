 About the role... 
 The Financial Planning & Analysis team is looking for a finance professional to advance Duolingo’s mission by delivering financial insights and driving high-impact decisions across the business. You will work closely with our FP&A Director and teams including Growth, Monetization, and Data Science to inform decisions that support Duolingo’s continued growth and profitability. 
 You’ll become an expert on Duolingo’s monetization engine—including how user and subscriber growth translates into revenue at more than $1 billion scale. This role is based in our New York City office on a hybrid schedule.
 🧠  You will... 
 - Develop and maintain financial models that support forecasting, earnings calls, strategic planning, and budgeting.
- Translate complex financial analysis into clear, actionable insights for our partners and executives.
- Recommend and own workstreams that shape decisions across Duolingo.
- Analyze how product experiments, pricing, packaging, and new initiatives could affect Duolingo’s financial performance.
- Contribute to high-profile materials for our Board of Directors, earnings calls, and senior executives.
 ✅  You have... 
 - Experience in investment banking, private or public equity investing, or strategic finance/FP&A at a high-growth technology company.
- Advanced financial modeling skills and experience designing and owning analytical models that improve business decisions.
- A strong 80/20 mindset, with the ability to identify the core problem, focus on the insights that matter most, and prioritize the steps needed to solve it.
- Sound judgment, with the ability to make well-reasoned decisions in ambiguous situations and establish a clear path forward.
- Structured thinking, a strong command of the details, and the ability to turn complex analysis into compelling, decision-focused narratives.
- Clear written and verbal communication skills, including experience presenting financial insights to cross-functional partners and senior leaders.
- A track record of owning projects, managing competing priorities, and delivering high-quality work on time.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.
 ⭐  Exceptional candidates will have... 
 - Experience with consumer technology, subscription, or digital product business models.
- Experience analyzing monetization, subscriber growth, or revenue drivers at scale.
- Experience supporting quarterly earnings or preparing materials for a public-company board or senior executives.
- Experience using AI tools to speed up analysis, improve financial models, and make everyday workflows more effective.
- An impressive Duolingo streak!

 What you need to know about the NYC Tech Scene

 As the undisputed financial capital of the world, New York City is an epicenter of startup funding activity. The city has a thriving fintech scene and is a major player in verticals ranging from AI to biotech, cybersecurity and digital media. It also has universities like NYU, Columbia and Cornell Tech attracting students and researchers from across the globe, providing the ecosystem with a constant influx of world-class talent. And its East Coast location and three international airports make it a perfect spot for European companies establishing a foothold in the United States.
