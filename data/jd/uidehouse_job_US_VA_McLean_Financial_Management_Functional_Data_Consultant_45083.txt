 What You Will Do : 
 As a part of Guidehouse’s Financial Management (FM) practice supporting Department of War clients, you will lead initiatives that transform core financial management functions and operations. Our finance transformation consultants help our clients across the Department of War optimize all elements of their financial management structure by transforming business processes, enhancing the efficiency of operations, improving transparency and performance management, promoting change management, and complying with Federal laws and regulations. 

 This role focuses on driving finance and accounting domain excellence while leveraging data and technology solutions to deliver actionable insights, enhance operational efficiency, enable auditability, and modernize financial processes in alignment with federal standards.

 What You Will Need : 
 - Must be able to OBTAIN and MAINTAIN a Federal or DoD "SECRET" security clearance; candidates must obtain approved adjudication of clearance prior to onboarding with Guidehouse. Candidates with an ACTIVE "SECRET" or higher-level clearance are preferred.
- Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Analytics, Information Systems, or related field
- Experience with Palantir, Databricks, Advana, War Data Platform, Python, R, SQL for data analysis and visualization tools 
- At least FIVE (5) years of experience
- Hands-on experience with data analytics, automation, and AI tools applied to financial management use cases.
- Strong leadership, communication, and project management capabilities; ability to synthesize complex information and deliver high-impact solutions in fast-paced environments.

 What Would Be Nice To Have : 
 - An ACTIVE and MAINTAINED "SECRET" Federal or DoD security clearance
- Advanced degree
- Certifications in Agile, Scrum, or Project Management (PMP, CSM, SAFe)
- Background in change management or organizational design within financial environments

The annual salary range for this position is $98,000.00-$163,000.00. Compensation decisions depend on a wide range of factors, including but not limited to skill sets, experience and training, security clearances, licensure and certifications, and other business and organizational needs.
