 Overview 
Houlihan Lokey, Inc. (NYSE:HLI) is a leading global investment bank recognized for delivering independent strategic and financial advice to corporations, financial sponsors, and governments. With uniquely deep industry expertise, broad international reach, and a partnership approach rooted in trust, the firm provides innovative, integrated solutions across mergers and acquisitions, capital solutions, financial restructuring, and financial and valuation advisory. Our unmatched transaction volumes provide differentiated, data-driven perspectives that help our clients achieve their most critical goals. To learn more about Houlihan Lokey, please visit HL.com.

 Financial and Valuation Advisory 
Over the past 50+ years, Houlihan Lokey has established one of the largest worldwide financial and valuation advisory practices. Our transaction expertise and leadership in the field of valuation inspire confidence in the financial executives, boards of directors, special committees, investors, and business owners we serve. In 2025, LSEG ranked us the No. 1 global M&A fairness opinion advisor over the past 25 years. Our stability, integrity, technical leadership, and global capabilities make us a trusted advisor for clients worldwide, across a wide range of services, including the Transaction Opinions, Fund Opinions, Transaction Advisory Services, Corporate Valuation Advisory Services, Portfolio Valuation, Real Estate Valuation and Advisory Services, and Dispute Resolution Consulting practices.

 As a Financial Analyst in the Corporate Valuation Advisory Services (CVAS) practice’s Complex Securities team, you will join a small, specialized, and fast-growing team serving corporate clients from a large variety of industries. You will collaborate with both junior and senior team members on client engagements to perform valuations of complex financial instruments (e.g., debt, equity,  derivatives, and contingent consideration) for financial reporting, tax, and transaction purposes, and create statistical analyses and models. 

 Key Responsibilities/Deliverables: 
 - Must be graduating between November 2026 - June 2027 
- Develop quantitative models to value unique financial instruments 
- Improve existing internal modeling processes 
- Perform statistical and data analyses 
- Present analyses and conclusions both internally and externally 
- Draft valuation reports and support clients through audit reviews, including responding to auditor inquiries 
- Research and calibrate key valuation inputs such as volatility, credit spreads, and discount rates 
- Basic Qualifications: 
- Bachelor’s or master’s degree in a quantitative discipline (e.g. finance, statistics, mathematics, economics, engineering) 
- Experience in financial reporting valuation is a plus 

 Preferred Qualifications: 
 You enjoy rigorous thought processes, thrive in a non-hierarchical work structure, and want to join a dynamic team. 
 - Strong knowledge of financial instruments, derivatives valuation theory, and statistical methods 
- Keen analytical capabilities with high attention to detail 
- A proactive team member who takes ownership of their work and looks for ways to move things forward 
- Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to explain complex technical concepts clearly to clients, auditors, and non-technical colleagues 
- Strong interpersonal skills and the ability to build effective working relationships and collaborate with colleagues across divisions 
- Ability to manage multiple engagements and competing deadlines in a fast-paced environment without sacrificing quality 
- Receptive to feedback, eager to learn, and willing to support teammates when priorities shift 
- Experience valuing financial instruments with non-linear payoffs including convertible bonds, warrants, derivatives, earn-outs, and complex capital structures using closed form solutions, binomial lattices, and Monte Carlo simulations in either Python, R, or MATLAB 
- Experience working with large data sets, performing statistical analyses in Python, and presenting results with data visualization software such as Tableau 
- Demonstrated history of building and improving models and processes that automate routine calculations using Python and VBA 
- Passion for financial markets and data analysis 
- Experience in creating Skills and agents in Claude Code or ChatGPT Codex is a plus
