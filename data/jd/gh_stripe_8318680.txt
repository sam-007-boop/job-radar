 About the team 
 Stripe’s Tax Transfer Pricing team designs, implements, and defends the intercompany pricing policies that support Stripe’s global operating model, ensuring pricing between Stripe entities is compliant, defensible, and aligned with how the business creates value. 
 As Stripe expands its product portfolio and international footprint, our transfer pricing policies and documentation must evolve alongside the business. Transactions between Stripe affiliates are becoming increasingly complex and, in some cases, share characteristics with transactions commonly undertaken by financial services institutions. These arrangements, including those supporting Stripe’s stablecoin-related activities, may also be subject to scrutiny from regulators that oversee the financial services sector. We are seeking an experienced Transfer Pricing Specialist with financial services expertise to help develop practical, scalable transfer pricing policies, work through the tax and regulatory considerations relevant to intercompany arrangements, and provide subject matter expertise in audit defense and dispute resolution. 
 What you’ll do 
 The Financial Services Transfer Pricing Strategist will be a hands-on, collaborative member of the team responsible for developing and implementing compliant, practical transfer pricing policies and processes. The role will focus on transactions between Stripe’s legal entities that have characteristics commonly associated with the financial services sector, including intercompany arrangements supporting Stripe’s stablecoin-related activities. 
 This individual will work closely with teams across the organization and must be an excellent communicator who can explain complex transfer pricing concepts clearly to non-tax audiences. The ideal candidate will have transfer pricing experience in payments, fintech, banking, or another regulated financial services environment, as well as a track record of partnering effectively with cross-functional teams and other members of the tax organization. 
 Responsibilities 

 Partner with teams within and outside of Finance to develop, execute, and maintain transfer pricing policies for Stripe’s current and future operating structure and product offerings with a strong moral compass and integrity 
 Analyze financial information to design, prepare, and review detailed calculations, assist in preparing legal entity forecasts, and support the tax provision process 
 Address the transfer pricing impacts of special projects related to restructurings, acquisitions, and corporate initiatives, and ensure operating models are fit for purpose in the context of evolving global tax and transfer pricing frameworks and other regulations that apply to transactions between affiliates 
 Build relationships with product and business leaders to understand their organizational structure and activities, and adapt transfer pricing policies in response to changes 
 Assist finance, legal, and tax teams in government and regulatory audits, and support Stripe’s participation in tax audits, advance pricing agreements, and competent authority proceedings 
 Prepare and maintain transfer pricing documentation and compliance in accordance with Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) guidelines and local tax and regulatory requirements, ensuring efficiency and quality across a subset of assigned jurisdictions 
 Build or simplify transfer pricing models for clarity, efficiency, and ease of use
