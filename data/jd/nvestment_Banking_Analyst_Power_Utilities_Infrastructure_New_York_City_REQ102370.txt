 About Moelis: 
 Moelis & Company (“Moelis”) is a leading global independent investment bank that provides innovative strategic advice and solutions to a diverse client base, including corporations, governments, and financial sponsors. The Firm assists its clients in achieving their strategic goals by offering comprehensive integrated financial advisory services across all major industry sectors. Moelis’s experienced professionals advise clients on their most critical decisions, including mergers and acquisitions, recapitalizations and restructurings, capital markets transactions, secondary transactions and primary fundraising, and other corporate finance matters. The Firm serves clients from locations across North and South America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. For further information, please visit: www.moelis.com .

 The Opportunity: 
 We are seeking an experienced Analyst to join our Power, Utilities & Infrastructure team in New York. The team advises businesses across power generation, electric, gas and water utilities, renewables, energy transition infrastructure, and transportation, digital and social infrastructure. You will support M&A, financing and strategic advisory assignments, developing an understanding of how regulation, contracted revenues, energy demand and capital investment shape these businesses. You will translate company research and asset-level operating assumptions into financial models, valuation analyses and client presentations, working closely with Associates, senior bankers, clients and other advisors.

 To learn more about our recent work, please visit: recent transactions .

 Responsibilities: 
 - Build and maintain financial and valuation models, including operating forecasts, discounted cash flow, comparable company and precedent transaction analyses
- Analyze company and asset-level operating drivers, including, as relevant, generation capacity, contracted revenues, regulatory frameworks, capital expenditures and cash flow stability
- Research power, utilities and infrastructure markets, including regulatory developments, energy transition trends, transaction activity and investor priorities
- Prepare client presentations, marketing materials and transaction analyses that clearly communicate company positioning, sector dynamics and strategic alternatives
- Support transaction execution through due diligence, data gathering, preparation of analytical materials and process coordination
- Gather and analyze company financial information, market data and shareholder and investor profiles to support client recommendations
- Maintain accurate analytical work and supporting materials while coordinating effectively with Associates, senior bankers and other transaction participants

 Preferred Qualifications: 
 - Relevant investment banking, corporate finance or transaction-related experience, with exposure to power, utilities or infrastructure preferred
- Strong analytical, financial modeling, accounting and valuation skills, with proficiency in Excel and PowerPoint
- Clear written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to present financial analysis and work effectively with internal teams and clients
- Strong attention to detail, intellectual curiosity and an interest in power, utilities and infrastructure businesses
- Ability to manage competing priorities, work collaboratively and deliver accurate analysis under tight deadlines

 Education: 
 - Undergraduate degree required; a focus in finance, business, accounting, economics, mathematics, engineering or a related discipline preferred

 Expected Salary Range: $110,000 - $135,000
