 Title: 

 Manager, Budget & Reporting Operations (CEMI)- 2 Year Appointment

 Title: Manager, Budget & Reporting Operations (CEMI)- 2 Year Appointment  

 Location: Midtown  

 Org Unit: Budget and Planning 

 Work Days:  

 Weekly Hours: 35.00 

 Exemption Status: Exempt

 Salary Range: $145,200.00 - $162,400.00  

 *As required under NYC Human Rights Law Int 1208-2018 - Salary range for this role when Hired for NYC Offices 
 Position Summary
 Under limited supervision, the Manager of Budget and Financial Reporting Operations assists the Director of Budget and Institutional Resources in directing the day-to-day operations of the Budget Office and will be the director's designee in their absence. This position is responsible for ensuring that the work of the budget office is completed on-time and to the highest standards. This position is expected to oversee and complete complex budgeting and financial reporting tasks and projects. This position will supervise a Budget Associate position.

 This is a term position anticipated to be needed through Fall 2028 but may be ended or extended based on organizational needs, funding availability, and performance. 
 Job Responsibilities
 - Assists directing operations of the budget office including adherence to deadlines and accuracy. This position also represents the budget office in WCM and Cornell University committees and projects.
- Coordinates the preparation of presentation and financial materials throughout the year for meetings with the senior WCM and Cornell governance officials, including the Board of Trustees, the Board of Overseers and the Business and Finance Committee
- Develops and disseminates budget policies and procedures to the WCM community.
- Prepares monthly revenue and expense reports for operating budget; analyzes report for data integrity, variances, trends and cyclical events. Tracks and provides key financial indicators and analytics for major revenues and expenditures.
- Responsible for making presentations or lead training sessions to the WCM community, as needed.
- Works with institutional partners to ensure consistency in the classification of financial data in various systems, data repositories and reports; maintains periodic meetings with partners to coordinate changes or growth in the institution.
- Directly supervises the budget associate position and is responsible for all aspects of performance management for that role 
- Leads the preparation of WCMs annual operating budget; develops and maintains budget models and tools for forecasting used in the development of the budget.
 Education
 - Bachelor's Degree in related field
 Experience
 7 + years of experience in a financial-related role with at least 5 + years of experience working in a budget or financial analyst/reporting position. termediate to expert skills with spreadsheets, databases, presentation software, and internet research methods.

Experience working in a higher education, not-for-profit or health care related organization Knowledge, Skills and Abilities
 - Excellent communication skills and the ability to interact with all levels of the Medical College.
