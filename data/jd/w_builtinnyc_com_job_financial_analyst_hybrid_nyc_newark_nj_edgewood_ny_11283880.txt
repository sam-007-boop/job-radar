 Broadridge is hiring! We are seeking a strategic and analytical Financial Analyst to support our Corporate Technology Infrastructure organization. This role serves as a key finance partner to technology leaders, leading planning, forecasting, reporting, and investment analysis across infrastructure operations and initiatives. The ideal candidate brings strong financial expertise, an understanding of technology infrastructure cost drivers, and the ability to improve financial transparency, support investment decisions, and drive disciplined management of operating and capital spend.

 Responsibilities:

 - Serve as a supporting finance business partner to the Corporate Technology Infrastructure leadership team.
- Support annual planning, quarterly forecasting, and monthly close processes for infrastructure-related operating and capital expenses.
- Leverage AI tools and analytics to enhance financial planning, forecasting and reporting.
- Develop and maintain financial models to support budgeting, data center strategy, cost allocation, depreciation/amortization impacts, and investment decisions.
- Provide insightful variance analysis and identify key cost drivers across infrastructure categories such as: Hardware, software, and data center services
- Infrastructure labor and managed services
- Partner with technology leaders to evaluate business cases for infrastructure investments, optimization initiatives, and transformation programs.
- Track and report financial performance against budget, forecast, and strategic targets, highlighting risks and opportunities.
- Support capitalization analysis for eligible technology investments and ensure alignment with accounting policies.
- Improve financial transparency through dashboards, reporting packages, and KPI development.
- Collaborate with Accounting, Procurement, FP&A, and Technology teams to ensure accurate accruals, vendor spend tracking, and contract visibility.
- Drive process improvements in planning, reporting, and financial governance for technology infrastructure spend.
- Support ad hoc analysis for senior leadership, including scenario modeling, cost optimization opportunities, and long-range planning.

 Qualifications:

 - 1 - 3 years of experience in finance, FP&A, or business partnership roles, preferably supporting technology, infrastructure, or corporate functions.
- Familiarity with AI tools and analytics techniques to improve financial analysis, forecasting, reporting, and decision support.
- Experience with budgeting, forecasting, financial modeling, and variance analysis.
- Understanding of technology infrastructure cost structures, including data center, software, hardware, telecom, and labor-related expenses.
- Experience supporting both OpEx and CapEx planning.
- Strong Excel and financial systems skills; experience with tools such as IBM TM1, Anaplan, Workday and Power BI is a plus.
- Excellent communication skills with the ability to present financial insights to non-finance stakeholders and senior leaders.
- Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
- Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
- Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business, or related field.
- This is an Individual Contributor role
