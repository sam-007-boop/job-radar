 What You Will Do : 

 We are currently searching for Data Analyst to provide support services the Division of Comparative Medicine (DCM) to satisfy the overall operational objectives of the National Institutes of Health (NIH). The primary objective is to provide services and deliverables through performance of support services.  This is a full-time, on-site opportunity in Bethesda, MD.

 - Analyze and evaluate, using quantitative and qualitative methods, the effectiveness of programs, grant operations, and organizational activities in meeting established goals, measures, and objectives.
- Review and analyze financial, narrative, statistical, and other program data to identify trends, issues, and programmatically significant findings.
- Establish appropriate study methods and analytical techniques and independently interpret findings resulting from program studies, reviews, and audits.
- Develop and present recommendations to improve program operations, organizational processes, work methods, procedures, systems, efficiency, and cost effectiveness.
- Perform program and project cost analyses, including life-cycle cost, cost-benefit, and economic evaluations.
- Prepare timely analytical, statistical, summary, and program status reports for review and decision-making at multiple organizational levels.
- Develop and recommend new or modified work methods, management processes, operating procedures, guidelines, and administrative approaches for program operations.
- Participate in meetings and discussions with agency officials, subject matter experts, technical personnel, and other federal stakeholders to investigate issues, present findings and recommendations, develop alternative courses of action, and resolve program-related matters.
- Serve as a point of contact for assigned program analyses, planning and science policy requests, and provide guidance concerning the management of contract information.
- Resolve programmatic and administrative issues by identifying appropriate sources of information and coordinating with program staff to accomplish requirements.
