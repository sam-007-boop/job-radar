ERM — Consulting Senior Associate, Geology

Ready to Launch Your Career in Environmental Consulting? 
 If you’re passionate about geology and want to make a real impact on environmental challenges, this is your chance to join a global leader in sustainability. At ERM , you’ll work alongside industry experts, gain hands-on experience, and help shape a cleaner, safer future.

 Why This Role Matters 
 Environmental issues are complex—and solving them requires innovative thinking and technical expertise. As a Consulting Senior Associate, Geology based in Charlotte, NC, you’ll play a critical role in investigating and remediating contaminated sites, ensuring compliance, and supporting sustainable solutions for clients across diverse industries.

 What Your Impact Is 
 - Contribute to projects that protect communities and ecosystems.
- Apply cutting-edge investigation and remediation technologies.
- Build foundational experience toward professional registration.
- Support ERM’s mission to create a more sustainable world.

 What You'll Bring 
 Required: 
 - Bachelor’s degree in geology, hydrogeology, engineering, environmental science, or related discipline (Master’s a plus).
- GPA of 3.0 or higher or equivalent experience.
- 1+ years of relevant experience.
- Willingness to perform field work (standing/walking full days).
- Strong commitment to safety and adherence to Health & Safety protocols.
- Excellent communication, organization, and analytical skills.
- Ability to work independently and collaboratively.
- Driver's License Required: This position requires a valid driver's license and/or the ability to operate a company vehicle due to the nature of job duties, which include frequent travel to various client locations across a large geographical area.
- Ability to lift at least 40 pounds.
- This position is not eligible for immigration sponsorship. 
 Preferred: 
 - 40-hour OSHA HAZWOPER certification.
- GIT or EIT certification.
- Experience with technical data recording and report writing.
- Local candidates preferred; regional candidates considered.

 Key Responsibilities 
 - Conduct field investigations: soil, sediment, groundwater, surface water, soil vapor, and indoor air sampling.
- Oversee drilling, soil logging, and installation of monitoring wells.
- Perform aquifer testing, well development, and remedial system operations.
- Assist with groundwater and wastewater system performance monitoring.
- Support Phase I and II environmental assessments for property transactions.
- Compile and analyze data; assist in preparing technical reports.
- Prepare compliance work plans, permits, and reports for air quality, hazardous waste, stormwater, and wastewater.
- Ensure adherence to federal, state, and local regulations.
- Communicate effectively with subcontractors, regulators, tenants, and clients during field activities.
