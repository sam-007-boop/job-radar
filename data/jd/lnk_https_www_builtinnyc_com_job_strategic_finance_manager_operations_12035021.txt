 About the Team: The Finance team partners with Operations to turn business priorities into disciplined investment decisions and stronger operating performance. Working closely with leaders across our kitchens, fulfillment, supply chain, and expansion initiatives, we connect financial planning with the realities of running and scaling CookUnity’s operations. 
 The role: As our Strategic Finance Manager, Operations you will combine rigorous financial analysis with hands-on partnership across our operations. You will help teams identify where performance can improve, determine which investments are worth making, and translate those decisions into measurable results. 
 Expansion and automation will be important areas of focus, alongside opportunities to improve efficiency across the business. You will spend time with operators to understand how work gets done, challenge assumptions, and build practical business cases. You will also bring financial discipline to planning and performance reviews, ensuring that insights lead to clear decisions and follow-through. 
 This role suits a finance professional who is comfortable building a model from scratch, working directly with operating teams, and influencing decisions in a fast-moving environment. 
 Responsibilities: - Partner directly with operating teams to understand workflows, investigate performance gaps, and translate drivers such as labor productivity, throughput, and capacity utilization into practical improvement opportunities. 
- Build financial models and business cases for expansion, automation, and other operational investments, comparing scenarios and evaluating expected returns, cash requirements, implementation risks, and key assumptions. 
- Help leaders prioritize initiatives and allocate resources, translating operational plans into financial targets and forecasts. 
- Partner with initiative owners to establish baselines and success measures, compare results with the approved business case, and recommend corrective actions when outcomes differ from expectations. 
- Lead financial performance reviews for your areas of responsibility, explaining the drivers behind results and recommending actions to address risks and opportunities. 
- Develop clear reporting and decision tools that connect operational metrics with financial outcomes and make performance visible to stakeholders. 
- Partner across Finance and Operations to improve planning processes, data quality, and accountability as the business scales. 
 Qualifications: - At least 6 years of experience in operations finance, FP&A, or a closely related analytical role, with a track record of influencing operational or investment decisions. 
- Advanced financial modeling skills, including building models from the ground up and translating operational drivers into financial outcomes. 
- Ability to work with imperfect operational data, validate assumptions with business partners, and turn analysis into clear, actionable recommendations. 
- Strong business partnering skills, with the confidence to challenge assumptions constructively and work effectively with both operators and senior leaders. 
- A hands-on approach to understanding operations, solving ambiguous problems, and building processes in a growing business. 
- Working knowledge of accounting principles and financial controls, with strong attention to detail and sound commercial judgment. 
- Willingness to travel to operating sites and work directly with local teams. 
- Ability to leverage generative AI to increase output quality and speed. 
 Preferred requirements: - Experience in a business with complex physical operations, such as food production, fulfillment, logistics, retail, or manufacturing. 
- Exposure to expansion, automation, or capital investment projects, including evaluation of results after implementation. 
- Experience using FP&A or business intelligence tools to improve planning and performance analysis.
